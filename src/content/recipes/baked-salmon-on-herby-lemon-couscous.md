## Method

1. First preheat the oven to 180–185 °C and prepare a baking dish (20 x 30 cm).
2. Prepare the couscous according to the package instructions. When it is ready, add the mint, parsley, lemon zest and juice, basil, cream, salt and pepper. Mix together, then pour it all into the baking dish.
3. Place the salmon fillets on the couscous skin side up, put the dish in the preheated oven and bake for 15–17 minutes.
