## Method

1. Prepare a 22 cm round baking tin and line it with baking paper.
2. Chop the walnuts, almonds and dates finely or pulse them in a food processor. Combine the base ingredients in a food processor and run it until you get a smooth, homogeneous mass.
3. Spread the base evenly over the bottom of the tin and place it in the fridge until needed.
4. Combine the raspberry layer ingredients in a medium saucepan. Bring to the boil while stirring constantly. Cook for 2 minutes from boiling, then remove from the heat.
5. Spread the raspberry cream over the chilled brownie base and return it to the fridge for at least 2 hours.
6. Take it out of the fridge, carefully remove it from the tin and sprinkle with desiccated coconut. Cut into smaller pieces.
7. It makes a perfect accompaniment to coffee, or a treat for the snack box.
