## Method

1. Cut the peaches into wedges. Sear them in a hot frying pan with a little oil until they turn a nice golden brown.
2. Toast the bread slices in a toaster or in a pan with a little oil until crisp.
3. Place the creamy burrata on the toasts, sprinkle with a little arugula, then arrange the grilled peach wedges and fresh basil leaves on top. Finally, drizzle everything with thick balsamic glaze.

## Tips

- You can also cook the peaches on a grill for an even smokier flavour.
- Use a good-quality, thick balsamic glaze so it does not make the bread soggy.
