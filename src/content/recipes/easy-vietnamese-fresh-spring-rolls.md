## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt, fry them in a pan as a thin omelette, leave to cool, then cut into thin strips.
2. Peel the cucumber and carrot and cut them into thin matchsticks, and slice the avocado.
3. **Assembly and wrapping:** Dip each rice paper briefly into lukewarm water (about 5 seconds) to soften it, then lay it on a flat cutting board.
4. On the lower third of the sheet layer a lettuce leaf, omelette strips, cucumber, carrot and avocado slices.
5. Fold the lower part of the rice paper over the filling, fold in the two sides, then roll up tightly into a cylinder.

## Tips

- School snack tip: wrap each roll separately in a slightly damp paper towel and keep in a closed container in the fridge, so the rice paper does not dry out and stays perfectly fresh until the next day.
