## Method

1. **Preparation:** Take the tofu out of its packaging, drain it and gently blot off the excess moisture with paper towel, then cut into even 2 cm cubes. Clean the shiitake mushrooms and slice them thinly. Wash the fresh spinach thoroughly and drain.
2. Peel the ginger and cut it into fine matchsticks (julienne). Peel the garlic and crush or finely chop it. Slice the spring onions thinly, keeping the white and green stalk parts separate. Slice the red chilli into thin rings for garnish.
3. **Cooking:** In a larger pot, heat 10 ml of the sesame oil over medium heat. Add the ginger strips, crushed garlic and the white part of the spring onion, and fry for about 1 minute until an intense, pleasant aroma is released (take care not to burn it).
4. Add the sliced shiitake mushrooms and fry for another 2 minutes. Then pour in the vegetable broth, add the soy sauce and a pinch of salt. Bring the soup to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 5 minutes so the flavours meld nicely.
5. Carefully slide the diced tofu into the soup. Cook over low heat for another 3-4 minutes so the tofu cubes heat through and soak up the aromatic ginger broth. Finally add the fresh spinach and stir it into the hot broth: the spinach leaves wilt to a beautiful deep green in 1 minute. Turn off the heat and drizzle the soup with the remaining 5 ml of fragrant sesame oil.
6. **Serving:** Ladle the hot tofu and spinach soup into deep Asian bowls. Garnish with the reserved green spring onion rings, toasted sesame seeds and, to taste, the sliced red chilli for a pleasantly spicy kick. Serve immediately while hot!

## Tips

- For an even crunchier texture, add fresh mung bean sprouts or bamboo shoots to the soup when serving.
- Always add the spinach to the soup right before turning off the heat, so it keeps its fresh, vibrant green colour and valuable nutrients.
