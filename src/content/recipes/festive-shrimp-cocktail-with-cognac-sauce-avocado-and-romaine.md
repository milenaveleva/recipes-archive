## Method

1. Lightly sauté the cooked shrimp in a little olive oil and garlic, then add fresh herbs, salt, pepper and a few drops of lemon juice. Set aside.
2. For the sauce, mix together the mayonnaise, ketchup, Tabasco, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard and cognac, and season to taste with salt, pepper and citrus juice.
3. Peel the avocados, cut them into wedges and sprinkle with citrus juice to keep them green.
4. Wash the lettuce and separate it into leaves.
5. To serve, spoon some sauce into the bottom of a glass, add a layer of avocado, a few lettuce leaves, a little more sauce, then the shrimp. Serve as a starter with a lime or lemon wedge and a little extra sauce on the side.

## Tips

- Works with any kind of shrimp.
- If you don't like heat, leave out the Tabasco.
