## Method

1. **Preparation:** Cut the tofu and the washed tomatoes into roughly 1.5-2 cm cubes. Slice the spring onions thinly, separating the white and light green parts from the dark green stalks (set the green aside for serving). Chop the garlic and ginger finely.
2. In a small bowl lightly beat the 2 eggs with a fork. In another small glass stir the cornstarch smooth with 2 tablespoons of cold water.
3. **Cooking:** In a medium saucepan heat the cooking oil over medium heat. Add the chopped garlic, ginger and the white part of the spring onions and fry until aromatic, about 1 minute, taking care they do not burn.
4. Add the diced tomato and a pinch of salt to the pan. Cook, stirring constantly, for 3-4 minutes until the tomato releases its juice and softens nicely.
5. Pour in the hot vegetable broth and add the soy sauce. Bring the soup to the boil, then add the prepared tofu. Simmer gently for about 3-4 minutes so the tofu takes on the flavour of the tasty broth.
6. Stir the cornstarch mixture again, then slowly pour it into the gently simmering soup, stirring. Cook for 1 more minute until the soup thickens softly and gets a velvety texture.
7. Turn the heat to the lowest setting. With a ladle start a gentle circular swirl in the soup, then pour the beaten egg into the pan in a thin stream. Leave for 10-15 seconds so that silky egg ribbons form, then stir once gently with a wooden spoon. Turn off the heat.
8. **Serving:** Drizzle the sesame oil over the soup and sprinkle with the reserved fresh spring onion rings. Serve hot in deep plates or Asian soup bowls.

## Tips

- The egg ribbons come out most perfect and delicate if you set the soup swirling gently and pour the egg slowly in a thin stream onto the surface while the soup is no longer boiling vigorously.
- To taste you can add a pinch of white pepper or a few drops of black rice vinegar when serving for an authentic Beijing feel.
