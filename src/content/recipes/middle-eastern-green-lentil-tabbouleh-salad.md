## Method

1. **Cooking the lentils and preparing the ingredients:** Rinse the lentils thoroughly in cold water in a sieve, then put them in a pot with plenty of lightly salted water. Bring to a boil, then cook over moderate heat until al dente (about 18–20 minutes), taking care that the grains stay tender but don't burst. Drain, rinse with cold water to stop the cooking, and let drain completely and cool.
2. While the lentils cool, prepare the vegetables. Quarter the cherry tomatoes, halve the cucumber lengthwise, scoop out the watery seeds with a spoon and cut into small cubes. Slice the spring onions thinly. Pick the parsley and mint leaves off the thicker stems and chop them very finely with a sharp knife.
3. **Toasting the pine nuts and making the dressing:** Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until golden and fragrant, shaking the pan constantly, then tip them into a small bowl to cool.
4. In a small bowl, mix the freshly squeezed lemon juice, the finely crushed garlic, ground cumin, fine salt and ground black pepper. Drizzle in the extra virgin olive oil in a thin stream while whisking into a smooth emulsion.
5. **Assembly and serving:** In a spacious mixing bowl, combine the cooled lentils, diced tomatoes, cucumber and spring onions. Pour over the lemon dressing and toss thoroughly. Finally add the generous amount of fresh parsley and mint and fold in gently.
6. Serve the lentil tabbouleh on a large platter or in deep plates. Crumble the creamy feta on top and sprinkle with the golden pine nuts. Warm the pitas in a pan until soft, cut into wedges and serve alongside the salad.

## Tips

- The secret of lentil tabbouleh is lots of fresh herbs: fold the parsley and mint into the salad right before serving so they stay fresh, crisp and bright green.
- If you like a more piquant flavour, sprinkle a pinch of sumac over the feta when serving!
