## Method

1. Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta. When the water is at a rolling boil, add fine salt, then add the dry tagliatelle and cook for 1-2 minutes less than the package time so it stays al dente. Before draining, scoop out at least 150 ml of the starchy cooking water.
2. In a dry frying pan over low or medium heat, toast the pine nuts until golden brown and fragrant, 2-3 minutes, shaking the pan constantly so they do not burn. Set aside in a small bowl.
3. In a bowl, stir the creamy ricotta smooth with the extra virgin olive oil, the finely grated organic lemon zest, 2 tablespoons of freshly squeezed lemon juice, the crushed fresh garlic, half of the grated Parmesan, a little fine salt and freshly ground black pepper. Add 50-100 ml of warm pasta water to get a silky, creamy sauce.
4. Return the drained hot tagliatelle to the warm pot or a deep frying pan. Pour the prepared lemon ricotta cream over it and add the fresh rocket. Toss thoroughly over low heat, or off the heat, for 1 minute: the heat of the hot pasta just wilts the rocket and the ricotta sauce coats the strands beautifully. If necessary, add another spoonful of cooking water to reach the desired creamy consistency.
5. Serve immediately, hot, in deep plates. Sprinkle with the toasted pine nuts, the remaining grated Parmesan and freshly ground black pepper.

## Tips

- The untreated peel of an organic lemon gives the sauce its irresistible fresh aroma, so be sure to use an organic or unwaxed lemon.
- Toss the rocket into the hot pasta only at the very end, right before serving, so it keeps its bright green colour and pleasantly peppery, crisp character.
