## Method

1. **Preparation:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C (top and bottom heat). Wash the kápia peppers, halve them lengthwise and carefully remove the seed core and ribs, keeping the stems intact so they form boats. Place the pepper halves cut side up on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
2. In a dry pan over medium heat, toast the walnuts for 2-3 minutes until fragrant, then take off the heat and let cool slightly. Once cooled, chop roughly.
3. **Filling and baking:** In a bowl, mash the feta with a fork. Add the Greek yogurt, crushed garlic, dried oregano, chilli flakes and freshly ground black pepper. Mix thoroughly until you have a creamy filling. Salt only carefully, as feta is already salty.
4. Spoon the creamy feta mixture evenly into the pepper boats. Sprinkle the tops with the roughly chopped toasted walnuts.
5. Drizzle the stuffed peppers with the extra virgin olive oil, then drizzle over the honey. Put in the preheated oven and bake for 25-30 minutes until the peppers are tender, the cheese layer is lightly browned and the walnuts are golden and caramelised.
6. **Serving:** Take the tray out of the oven and let it rest for 5 minutes. Sprinkle with freshly chopped parsley. Serve warm or lukewarm with crusty whole-wheat bread or whole-wheat pita.

## Tips

- For an even softer texture, you can blend the feta and Greek yogurt until creamy in a food processor.
- Can be prepared ahead: fill the peppers hours earlier and store in the fridge, making this ideal for dinner parties.
