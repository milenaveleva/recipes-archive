## Method

1. Heat olive oil in a large frying pan and fry the slices of bread until golden brown. Season with grated lemon zest, then take them out of the pan and cut into small cubes (croutons).
2. Cut the zucchini into 1 cm cubes. In the same pan, start frying the crushed garlic and a whole sprig of parsley. Add the zucchini and pour in a little water so the garlic does not burn.
3. Stir in half a tablespoon of ricotta, add a few basil leaves and the chopped green beans. Cook the vegetables until done.
4. Cook the spaghetti al dente (firm to the bite) in a large pot of salted water. Drain, then toss with the vegetables in the pan.
5. Stir in the remaining ricotta, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle over the lemony croutons and the fresh lemon zest. When serving, sprinkle a little more ricotta on top and drizzle with olive oil.
