## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 195–200 °C and prepare a roughly 25 cm × 30 cm baking tray.
2. Peel the onion and chop it roughly. Wash the zucchini, pepper and tomatoes, then cut them into larger cubes. Peel the carrots and slice them into thin rounds. Put all the cut vegetables in the tray. Add the peeled garlic cloves, the dried basil, 3 tablespoons of olive oil, salt and pepper. Toss thoroughly, then bake in the oven for 35–40 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, bring water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta. When it boils, add salt and cook the whole-wheat pasta in it for the time stated on the packet (about 13–14 minutes). Before draining, set aside 100 ml of the cooking water.
4. When the vegetables are roasted, take the tray out of the oven. Pour in the plant-based cooking cream and the reserved 100 ml of cooking water. Blend everything with a stick blender until creamy and smooth, then fold in the cooked pasta.
5. Serve the pasta on plates, garnished with fresh basil leaves.
