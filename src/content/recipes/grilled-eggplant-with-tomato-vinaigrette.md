## Method

1. Wash the cherry tomatoes and cut them into eighths. Put them in a bowl, add the crushed garlic and the chopped capers, then season with one tablespoon of olive oil, the red wine vinegar, a pinch of salt and pepper. Mix well and chill in the fridge to mature while the eggplant is being prepared.
2. Wash the eggplant and wipe dry. Cut it into slices about 1 cm thick and drizzle with the remaining olive oil.
3. Heat a grill (or grill pan) to about 230 °C. Place the eggplant slices on it and grill for about 5 minutes per side until nicely striped and soft.
4. Lightly salt the grilled eggplant slices and arrange on a serving dish. Pile the chilled tomato mixture on top (making sure the excess liquid stays in the bowl).
5. Sprinkle with fresh basil leaves, drizzle with a little more olive oil and serve immediately.

## Tips

- For a more filling dish, serve with fresh whole-wheat baguette or sprinkle with a little crumbled feta.
