## Method

1. Peel the beetroot, carrots, apple and ginger, then cut them into smaller pieces depending on the size of your blender. With a stronger countertop blender, it is enough to halve the vegetables and fruit and cut the ginger into a few smaller pieces.
2. Squeeze the lime and the half orange into the blender, then add the other prepared ingredients. Blend until the smoothie is completely smooth. If you do not have a countertop blender, you can use a hand blender, which takes a little longer but gives the same result.

## Tips

- If you want a cooler drink, add a few ice cubes before blending.
