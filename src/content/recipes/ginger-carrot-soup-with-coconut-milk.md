## Method

1. Peel the onions and cut them into larger pieces. Peel the carrots and slice them into thin rounds. Peel the ginger and chop it roughly.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot and ginger and sauté together for 6-7 minutes. Pour in the vegetable broth and coconut milk, season with salt and pepper, and bring to the boil. Once boiling, reduce the heat and cook for 20-21 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
3. After cooking, turn off the heat and blend the soup until creamy with an immersion blender. Serve warm.

## Tips

- Garnish with a little coconut milk, fresh coriander or toasted pumpkin seeds for an even richer experience.
