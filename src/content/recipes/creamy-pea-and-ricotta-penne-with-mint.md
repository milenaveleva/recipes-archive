## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the pasta:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, then salt it generously with the fine salt. Cook the dry penne al dente according to the package instructions. Before draining, scoop out and set aside about 150-200 ml of the starchy cooking water; it will be key to the silky sauce.
2. Peel the garlic and chop finely or grate it. Wash the organic lemon thoroughly, finely grate the yellow zest, then squeeze the juice of half of it. Pick the fresh mint leaves from the stems and chop finely.
3. **Making the sauce and assembly:** In a deep frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped garlic and gently fry for half a minute until fragrant (take care it doesn't turn too dark). Add the frozen peas and cook for 3-4 minutes until the peas thaw and soften but keep their bright green colour. Season with a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper.
4. Lower the heat, then add the ricotta and 100 ml of the reserved pasta water to the pan. Stir thoroughly with a wooden spoon until the ricotta and cooking water form a creamy, velvety emulsion around the peas. If it seems thick, add a little more cooking water.
5. Stir the grated lemon zest, the lemon juice and half of the freshly grated Parmesan into the sauce. Finally fold in the cooked hot penne and the chopped fresh mint. Toss thoroughly for 1 minute over low heat so the pasta is coated in every groove with the fresh, creamy sauce.
6. Serve the penne immediately, hot, in deep plates. Sprinkle the remaining grated Parmesan on top with a few drops of extra virgin olive oil and garnish with little fresh mint leaves.

## Tips

- The pasta cooking water is essential for the creaminess of the ricotta: the starch in it helps emulsify a smooth, glossy sauce without the ricotta turning grainy.
- Add the fresh mint and lemon zest right at the very end of cooking so they keep their freshness and wonderful aroma.
