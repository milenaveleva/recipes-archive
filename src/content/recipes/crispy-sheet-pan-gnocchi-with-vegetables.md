## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 220 °C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. Peel the onion and cut it into thicker slices. Wash the cherry tomatoes. Wash the bell pepper, remove the core and cut it into about 2x2 cm cubes. Press the garlic and mix it with the oil and dried basil. Put all the vegetables on the tray together with the gnocchi, drizzle with the garlic-basil oil and bake for 15-20 minutes at 210-220 °C.
3. Take the tray out of the oven. Tear the mozzarella into larger pieces and distribute it over the vegetables and gnocchi. Sprinkle everything with grated Parmesan, then return to the oven for 1-2 minutes until the cheese melts.
4. As soon as the cheese has melted, take the tray out, divide the food between two plates and serve immediately.
