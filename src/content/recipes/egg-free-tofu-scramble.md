## Method

1. Pat the tofu dry with a paper towel, then mash it thoroughly with a fork in a bowl.
2. Finely chop the red onion and sauté it in a little oil (or water) until translucent.
3. Add the mashed tofu to the onion. Season with salt and pepper and sprinkle with the sweet paprika.
4. Pour in a little water to keep it juicy, then fry everything together for a few minutes, stirring constantly. Serve warm.
