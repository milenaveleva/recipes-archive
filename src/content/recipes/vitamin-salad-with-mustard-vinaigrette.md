## Method

1. For the vinaigrette, put the mustard, vinegar, a pinch of salt and pepper in a tall glass, add the oil and, starting from the bottom, draw up an immersion blender until you get an even, creamy dressing.
2. Wash and dry the salad greens if necessary.
3. Cut the tomatoes into wedges.
4. Cut the carrots into strips.
5. Toss the fresh vegetables together and serve with the vinaigrette.

## Tips

- The vinaigrette is perfect for any salad.
- You can also use Dijon or wholegrain mustard.
- Put any vegetables in the salad: cucumber, beetroot, endive and so on.
- The salad can also be made with leftover roast meat or a soft-boiled egg.
