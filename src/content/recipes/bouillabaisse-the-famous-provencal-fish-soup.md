## Method

1. **Prepare the vegetables:** Finely chop the onion, leeks, garlic and tomatoes.
2. **Sauté the vegetables:** Sauté the onion, leeks and garlic in olive oil.
3. **Add the tomatoes:** Add the diced tomatoes and continue cooking for a few minutes.
4. **Season:** Add the thyme, bay leaves, saffron, salt and pepper.
5. **Add the wine and stock:** Pour in the white wine, bring to the boil, then add the stock.
6. **Simmer:** Cook over low heat for 30 minutes so the flavours meld.
7. **Add the fish:** Add the mixed fish and cook for 10-15 minutes.
8. **Add the seafood:** Add the mussels, shrimp and crab, then cook for another 5-7 minutes until everything is tender.
9. **Season to taste:** Taste, and add salt and pepper if needed.
10. **Serve:** Sprinkle with fresh parsley and serve hot.
11. Serve the bouillabaisse with toasted whole-wheat baguette spread with a garlicky yogurt sauce. Experiment with different seafood for more varied flavours.
