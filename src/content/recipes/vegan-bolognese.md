## Method

1. Finely chop the onion, carrots and celery. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and sauté the vegetables, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly browned (about 8 minutes).
2. Add the sliced garlic and fry briefly, until you start to smell it.
3. Stir in the vegan mince, turn up the heat, and crumble it into smaller pieces while stirring constantly, then fry until browned.
4. Add the tomato paste and let it caramelize briefly, then pour in the passata and the water (or broth). Stir well so that any browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pot dissolve.
5. Add the rinsed, uncooked red lentils, the rosemary, the chopped basil, the salt and the pepper.
6. Turn the heat down to the minimum and let the sauce simmer slowly, uncovered, for at least an hour.
7. Stir occasionally and add a little water or broth as needed.
8. It is ready when the vegetables are completely soft and the sauce is even and thick.
9. Serve the finished sauce over freshly cooked pasta, and add vegan cheese to taste if you like.
