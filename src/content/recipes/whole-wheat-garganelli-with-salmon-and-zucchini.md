## Method

1. Place the salmon fillets in a mixture of 60 g salt and 1 litre water for 2 hours.
2. Then fry them on both sides for 3 minutes each so they stay slightly pink inside, and set aside to rest while you prepare the rest of the dish.
3. For that, cook the pasta in salted water (follow the packet; whole-wheat pasta usually needs 1-2 minutes longer than white), sauté the garlic in olive oil, pour in the broth and reduce it, then add the cream and the thinly sliced zucchini.
4. Flake the salmon into bite-sized pieces by hand.
5. Toss the pasta with the sauce, sprinkle with chives or any herb you like, and serve with the salmon pieces and Parmesan shavings.
6. If you like, squeeze a little lemon over the top.

## Tips

- It can also be made with smoked salmon.
- Instead of zucchini you can use other vegetables, such as broccoli, leek, carrot or Brussels sprouts.
