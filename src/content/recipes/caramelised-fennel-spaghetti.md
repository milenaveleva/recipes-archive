## Method

1. **Preparation:** Wash the fennel bulbs thoroughly, cut off the green stalks and the fine dill-like fronds and set them aside for serving. Halve the bulbs, cut out the hard core in a wedge, then slice the bulbs into hair-thin strips. Thinly slice the garlic cloves, finely grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze 1 tablespoon of juice from it.
2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant and golden brown, taking care that they do not burn. Take them out of the pan and set aside in a small bowl.
3. **Cooking and caramelising:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot and salt it once it boils. Add the spaghetti and cook until al dente (firm to the bite), 1 minute less than the packet says. Before draining, scoop out 150 ml of the pasta cooking water.
4. While the pasta cooks, heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large deep frying pan. Add the thinly sliced fennel with a pinch of salt and fry over medium-high heat, stirring often, for 7–8 minutes, until the fennel is soft and gets a beautiful golden-brown, caramelised layer. Add the sliced garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes and fry for another minute until the garlic becomes fragrant.
5. Pour half of the reserved pasta water into the pan, add the extra olive oil and the freshly squeezed lemon juice. Stir with quick movements so that the starchy water and the fat form a silky emulsion.
6. **Assembly and serving:** Pour the drained hot spaghetti straight into the fennel sauce in the pan. Sprinkle over the grated lemon zest, the freshly ground black pepper and half of the finely grated pecorino. Over medium heat, tossing and shaking continuously for 1 minute, toss until the sauce coats the strands beautifully (add a few more tablespoons of cooking water if needed).
7. Divide the pasta among deep plates. Sprinkle with the toasted pine nuts, the reserved fresh fennel fronds and the remaining grated pecorino. Serve immediately, piping hot.

## Tips

- Do not throw away the fennel fronds: their aniseed-like, refreshing aroma is the perfect garnish and flavour booster when serving.
- The pasta water is key: its starch, mixed with the olive oil, thickens the sauce naturally without needing any cream.
