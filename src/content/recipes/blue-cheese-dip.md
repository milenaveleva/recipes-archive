## Method

1. In a large bowl, mash the blue cheese with a fork until creamy.
2. Stir in the lemon juice, sour cream, mayonnaise and vinegar. You should get a creamy dip.
3. Season with salt and pepper, and that is it, ready!

## Tips

- You can serve this dip with practically anything: roast meats and fish, raw vegetable sticks, and it is also great with chips and tortillas. Make it a bit thicker and you can spread it on a sandwich; make it a bit thinner and use it as a salad dressing.
