## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the pasta:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, then salt it generously with the fine salt. Cut the broccoli into small florets, and peel and finely dice the stalk.
2. Drop the dry pasta and the broccoli into the boiling water at the same time. Cook the pasta for 1–2 minutes less than the packet says (al dente), so the broccoli becomes soft enough to mash.
3. **Frying and tossing:** Meanwhile, in a roomy frying pan heat the extra virgin olive oil. Add the thinly sliced fresh garlic and the sliced red chilli. Fry over low-medium heat for 1–2 minutes until the aromas are released, taking care that the garlic does not burn.
4. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the cooked pasta and broccoli straight into the garlic and chilli olive oil. Add 1 ladle of pasta cooking water, then mash the soft broccoli florets slightly with a wooden spoon so they form a creamy emulsion with the olive oil.
5. Toss the pasta over high heat for 1 minute, season with freshly ground black pepper and sprinkle with the grated Parmesan. Serve hot.

## Tips

- The starch in the cooking water is essential for a silky sauce – never pour away all of it when draining the pasta!
- For an even more authentic Puglian flavour, use aged pecorino instead of Parmesan, or sprinkle toasted whole-wheat breadcrumbs on top.
