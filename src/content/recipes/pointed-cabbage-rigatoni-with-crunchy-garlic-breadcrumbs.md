## Method

1. **Preparation and crunchy crumbs:** Halve the pointed cabbage, remove the tough core, then cut it into thin, long strips. Peel the garlic cloves: finely chop one clove for the crumbs and slice the rest thinly. In a frying pan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Add the chopped garlic and the breadcrumbs, then toast over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 3–4 minutes until golden brown and crisp. Transfer to a small bowl and mix in a pinch of salt and a little finely grated lemon zest.
2. **Pasta and cabbage sauce:** Put plenty of water on to boil in a large pot. When it is boiling, salt it generously with the fine salt, add the rigatoni and cook for 1 minute less than the package time, to a pleasant al dente. Before draining, set aside 150 ml of starchy cooking water. While the pasta cooks, heat the remaining 3 tablespoons of olive oil and half of the butter in a wide frying pan. Add the sliced garlic and chilli flakes and fry for half a minute until fragrant but not browned. Tip in the shredded pointed cabbage. Season with salt and the ground black pepper, then toss over medium-high heat. Fry for 6–8 minutes until the cabbage collapses and its edges caramelise to a nice golden brown. Pour in about 100 ml of the reserved pasta water, add the remaining butter and simmer for 1 minute to form a silky emulsion around the cabbage.
3. **Assembly and serving:** Tip the hot, drained rigatoni straight into the caramelised cabbage. Take off the heat, squeeze over the juice of the organic lemon and sprinkle with two thirds of the grated Parmesan. Toss thoroughly for 1 minute; if needed, add a little more cooking water for a perfectly creamy sauce. Serve in warm deep plates. Sprinkle the reserved Parmesan on top and spoon over a generous amount of the crunchy garlic breadcrumbs for an irresistible texture. Eat fresh, immediately!

## Tips

- The crunchy garlic breadcrumbs (pangrattato in Italian) are best made fresh just before serving so they keep their crunch on top of the silky pasta.
- The starchy pasta water is key: mixed with the butter and cheese it binds the ingredients into an irresistible, glossy sauce.
