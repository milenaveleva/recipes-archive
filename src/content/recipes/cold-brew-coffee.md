## Method

1. Many people confuse cold brew with simple iced coffee, but the two are completely different, and cold brew brings out a whole new side of your favourite coffee. Try this simple but great recipe and find out for yourself!
2. Grind the whole coffee beans coarsely. Make sure they are not ground too fine, as that makes them harder to strain later.
3. In a large jar (a preserving jar works well), mix the ground coffee with 3 cups of water.
4. Let it settle for 5 minutes, then stir again.
5. Seal the jar airtight and put it in the fridge for about 18 hours.
6. After 18 hours, set a fine-mesh sieve over a bowl. Line it with a paper coffee filter (a tea towel or cloth works perfectly too), then pour in the coffee. Leave it until it has finished dripping.
7. After straining, pour the coffee into another airtight jar; in the fridge it keeps for up to 2 weeks.
8. Serve with plenty of ice, half water and half cold brew. Finish with a little oat milk if you like.
