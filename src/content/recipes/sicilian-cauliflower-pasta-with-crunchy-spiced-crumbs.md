## Method

1. **Preparation and crunchy crumbs:** Wash the cauliflower and break it into small, bite-sized florets. Cut the garlic into thin slices, chop the chilli finely, and chop the fresh parsley.
2. Toast the pine nuts golden in a dry frying pan, then set aside. In the same pan, heat one tablespoon of olive oil, add the breadcrumbs, a pinch of salt and a little chopped parsley. Fry over medium heat, stirring constantly, until crisp and golden brown, then tip into a bowl.
3. **Combining the cauliflower sauce and pasta:** Bring plenty of lightly salted water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta. When it boils, add the spaghetti and cook until al dente (firm to the bite) according to the packet time. Before draining, scoop out a cup of pasta cooking water.
4. In a large frying pan, heat 3 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil. Add the cauliflower florets and fry over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, for 8-10 minutes until a nice golden-brown crust forms and they are tender.
5. Add the sliced garlic, the chopped chilli and the raisins to the pan. Fry together for another 1-2 minutes until the aromas are released, then season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
6. Toss the hot cooked pasta straight into the cauliflower pan. Add 4-5 tablespoons of pasta cooking water and the toasted pine nuts, and toss thoroughly so the pasta becomes creamy with the sauce.
7. **Serving:** Serve the pasta in deep bowls, sprinkled generously with the crisp spiced breadcrumbs, fresh parsley and freshly grated Parmesan. Serve immediately, hot.

## Tips

- It is worth cutting the cauliflower into small florets so it caramelises evenly and quickly in the pan, giving it a sweet, nutty flavour.
- In Sicilian cuisine, the fried spiced breadcrumbs (muddica atturrata) traditionally served as the poor man's Parmesan, but combined with cheese they give the pasta a fantastic crunchy texture.
