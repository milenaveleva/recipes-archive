## Method

1. **Preparing the base:** Chop the shallot and garlic finely. In a deeper frying pan, heat half of the olive oil and soften the shallot and garlic over medium heat until translucent, 2-3 minutes.
2. Add the tender peas, stir, season with salt and pepper, then pour in about 200 ml of warm water or vegetable broth. Cook covered for 6-8 minutes until the peas are soft.
3. **Combining the pasta and finishing:** Blend one third of the cooked peas with a ladle of cooking liquid until creamy, then pour it back into the pan with the remaining whole peas to get a silky base.
4. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water until al dente, 1 minute less than the packet says. Drain, keeping 100 ml of the cooking water.
5. Tip the hot pasta into the pea pan, add most of the grated Pecorino, the remaining olive oil and the finely torn fresh basil. Toss with a few tablespoons of pasta water until a creamy emulsion coats the pasta. Serve sprinkled with the remaining Pecorino and freshly ground black pepper.

## Tips

- For the best result use tender, sweet frozen peas, which keep their fresh flavour and bright green colour thanks to quick freezing.
- For an even creamier texture, toss the hot pasta in the pan with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil in addition to the Pecorino before serving.
