## Method

1. Halve the peaches, remove the pits and cut them into wedges. Put the peach slices in a bowl, drizzle with one tablespoon of olive oil and toss. Grill the peach wedges on a hot outdoor grill or contact grill on both sides until golden brown, completely tender and lightly caramelised.
2. Spread the arugula on a serving plate together with the sliced tomatoes (larger ones in rounds or wedges, cherry tomatoes halved).
3. Add the warm grilled peaches and the sliced sun-dried tomatoes.
4. Take the burrata out of its liquid, tear it into smaller pieces and place it on the plate on top of the warm peaches so it melts slightly from the heat.
5. Finally garnish with fresh basil leaves, season with a pinch of salt and ground black pepper, and drizzle with the remaining olive oil and balsamic glaze. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- Take the burrata out of the fridge 15–20 minutes before serving so it reaches the perfect creamy texture at room temperature!
