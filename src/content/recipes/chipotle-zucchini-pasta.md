## Method

1. Spiralize the zucchini into noodles.
2. Blend the avocado, almond milk, chipotle seasoning, garlic, onion powder, tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, lemon juice and salt until smooth. It should be thick but pourable; loosen with a little more almond milk if needed.
3. Warm the sauce gently in a large saucepan without letting it get hot, which makes it gluey.
4. Add the zucchini noodles and toss until coated.
5. Divide between bowls, scatter with cilantro and serve straight away.

## Tips

- For a heartier bowl, use 225 g dry brown rice noodles, cooked in salted water and drained, in place of the zucchini.
- Add up to 1 tsp chipotle seasoning for more heat.
