## Method

1. In a deep frying pan or pot, fry the finely chopped shallots and the sliced garlic golden in half of the olive oil.
2. Add the oyster mushrooms cut into larger pieces. Halve the shiitake mushrooms and leave the smaller caps whole. Continue frying everything together for a few minutes until the mushrooms collapse.
3. Sprinkle in the orzo and mix it with the mushrooms. Add the chopped thyme. Pour in the vegetable broth and let it bubble gently over low heat for about ten minutes, until the orzo is tender and has absorbed the liquid. If needed, top up with broth or water.
4. As soon as the orzo is ready, stir in the remaining olive oil and half of the finely grated Parmesan for a creamier texture. Season with salt and pink pepper to taste. Serve sprinkled with the remaining Parmesan shavings.

## Tips

- For an even creamier texture, add a splash of unsweetened oat milk at the end of cooking.
