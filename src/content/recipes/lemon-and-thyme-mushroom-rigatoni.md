## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Bring a large pot of generously salted water to the boil, then cook the rigatoni in it until al dente according to the packet instructions. Set aside 1 ladleful (about 100 ml) of the pasta cooking water before draining.
2. Clean and slice the brown mushrooms. Chop the garlic cloves finely, grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze out its juice, and pick the leaves of the fresh thyme from the stalks.
3. In a frying pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced mushrooms and fry for 5-6 minutes, until nicely golden brown.
4. Stir in the finely chopped garlic and the fresh thyme, then fry for another minute. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
5. Pour the lemon juice, the grated lemon zest and half of the reserved pasta water over the mushrooms. Add the drained rigatoni and half of the grated Parmesan.
6. Toss gently for 1-2 minutes, until the creamy sauce coats the pasta nicely. Serve immediately, garnished with the remaining grated Parmesan and fresh thyme.

## Tips

- Before grating the zest of the organic lemon, wash the fruit thoroughly with warm water and make sure to grate only the yellow part of the peel.
- If you would like an even creamier texture, you can stir 2-3 tablespoons of fresh ricotta or plant-based cooking cream into the sauce at the end of cooking.
