## Method

1. **Prep:** Wash the leeks thoroughly to remove any soil between the layers, then cut them into thin rounds. Peel the garlic cloves and chop them finely. Grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze out 1 tablespoon of its juice from it. Grate the Parmesan finely.
2. **Cook and assemble:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, add a little salt, then cook the penne al dente (1–2 minutes less than the packet time). Before draining, set aside 1–2 ladlefuls of starchy cooking water.
3. In a deep frying pan over medium heat, warm together the 2 tablespoons of olive oil and half of the extra olive oil (15 g). Add the sliced leeks and a pinch of salt and sweat over low heat for 8–10 minutes, until the leeks are completely soft and silky with a sweet aroma. Add the garlic and fry for another 1 minute, taking care that it does not brown.
4. Pour the plant-based cooking cream and 1 ladleful of cooking water into the pan and simmer over low heat for 2 minutes, until the sauce thickens creamily. Season with the lemon zest, 1 tablespoon of lemon juice, freshly ground black pepper and a little more salt if needed.
5. Toss the cooked penne and the remaining extra olive oil (15 g) into the silky leek sauce. Turn off the heat, sprinkle in most of the grated Parmesan and toss energetically until the sauce coats the pasta as a glossy emulsion. If needed, add a little more pasta water for the perfect juicy texture.
6. Serve the hot pasta in deep plates, topped with the remaining Parmesan, freshly ground black pepper and a little extra lemon zest.

## Tips

- Always cook leeks slowly and patiently: in a mixture of oil and a little extra olive oil, the natural sugar of the vegetable caramelises beautifully without burning.
- The reserved starchy pasta water is key in Italian pasta: combined with the plant-based cooking cream, olive oil and Parmesan it forms a perfect emulsion, so the sauce does not split and clings flawlessly to the penne.
