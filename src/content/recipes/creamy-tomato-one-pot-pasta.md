## Method

1. Blend the cashews, tomatoes, onion powder, garlic, red pepper flakes, dried herbs and water until smooth. Set aside.
2. Heat a medium non-stick pan over medium-high heat and cook the onion and sun-dried tomatoes for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the onion turns translucent.
3. Add the asparagus and zucchini and cook over high heat for another 3–5 minutes until lightly browned and starting to soften.
4. Stir the sauce, pour it into the pan and add the pasta. Mix well, lower to a simmer, cover and cook for 10–14 minutes until the pasta is al dente and the sauce has thickened. If it is still thin, uncover for a few more minutes.
5. Serve straight away, topped with basil.

## Tips

- Green beans work in place of the asparagus.
- Chickpea, pea or brown rice pasta can replace the lentil pasta.
