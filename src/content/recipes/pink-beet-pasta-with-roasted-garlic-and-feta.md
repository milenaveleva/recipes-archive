## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 185 °C. Peel the raw beetroot and cut it into smaller pieces. Place it on aluminium foil, drizzle with one tablespoon of oil, toss together and wrap tightly. Cut the top off the garlic bulb so the cloves are exposed, drizzle with one tablespoon of oil and wrap in foil as well.
2. Place both packets on a baking tray and bake in the preheated oven for about 50–60 minutes, until the beetroot is completely soft. Let the roasted beetroot and garlic cool slightly, and meanwhile put salted water on to boil for the pasta.
3. When the water boils, add the pasta and cook according to the packet instructions. Put the soft roasted beetroot into a blender with the feta, the parsley, a ladleful of pasta cooking water and the roasted garlic cloves (squeeze them out of their skins). Season with salt and pepper and blend into a smooth, pink sauce. If it is too thick, add a little more cooking water.
4. Mix the finished sauce with the cooked pasta and serve. Sprinkle the top with crumbled feta and chopped parsley. Enjoy!

## Tips

- Use bow-tie (farfalle) pasta for an even more stylish look!
