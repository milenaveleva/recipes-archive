## Method

1. Rinse the fish fillet thoroughly, pat dry and remove the skin if necessary. Cut the flesh into even cubes of about 1-2 cm.
2. Squeeze the juice of the limes and lemon. In a glass bowl pour the citrus juice over the fish cubes so that it covers them completely. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes. The acidic juice "cooks" the fish in the meantime.
3. While the fish marinates, prepare the vegetables: dice the tomato and cucumber. Halve the avocado, remove the stone and skin, and dice it too. Chop the red onion and the seeded jalapeño very finely. Chop the fresh cilantro.
4. After resting, mix the vegetables with the fish. Salt to taste, then gently toss everything together with the marinade. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- Serve with crunchy tortilla chips for the perfect texture.
