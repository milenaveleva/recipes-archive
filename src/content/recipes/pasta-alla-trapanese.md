## Method

1. **Prepare the pesto:** Put a large pot of generously salted water on to boil for the pasta. In a dry frying pan over medium heat, toast the almonds golden brown for about 2–3 minutes, until fragrant, then let them cool slightly.
2. Halve the washed cherry tomatoes. In a small food processor (or mortar) put the toasted almonds, the peeled garlic cloves, the fresh basil leaves, a pinch of salt and ground black pepper. Chop coarsely.
3. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and the 60 ml of extra virgin olive oil and pulse into a creamy but still slightly chunky pesto. Finally stir in half of the grated Parmesan.
4. **Cook and assemble:** In the boiling water, cook the pasta for 1 minute less than the packet says, so that it stays perfectly al dente. Before draining, scoop out a small cup (about 50–80 ml) of the cooking water.
5. Immediately tip the hot, drained pasta into a large bowl or pan and pour over the fresh, raw Sicilian tomato and almond pesto. Add a little warm cooking water and toss thoroughly, so that the sauce coats the pasta beautifully.
6. Serve immediately in deep plates, garnished with the remaining freshly grated Parmesan and a few fresh basil leaves.

## Tips

- The point of the sauce is freshness: the pesto does not need to be cooked! The hot pasta and the warm cooking water warm it just enough for the flavours to develop perfectly.
- In the Trapani region it is traditionally made with busiate or fusilli, because the almond pesto clings fantastically to the twisted shapes.
