## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line or grease a 12-hole muffin tin.
2. For the streusel, mix the oats and oat flour in a small bowl, add the honey and melted coconut oil, and rub together with your fingertips until crumbly. Set aside.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk the ground flaxseeds with the water, then whisk in the honey, applesauce, coconut oil and vanilla.
4. In a second bowl, whisk the oat flour, brown rice flour, baking soda, baking powder and cinnamon until free of lumps.
5. Stir the wet mixture into the dry until just combined; the batter should be thick but still pourable. Loosen with a splash of water or stiffen with a spoonful of flour if needed.
6. Fold in the diced apple.
7. Fill the muffin cups to the rim and sprinkle each with streusel.
8. Bake for 20–24 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
9. Leave in the tin for 5 minutes, then cool completely on a wire rack.

## Tips

- Best eaten the day they are baked; store leftovers in an airtight container.
