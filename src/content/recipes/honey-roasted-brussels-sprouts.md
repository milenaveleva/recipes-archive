## Method

1. Heat the oven to 230°C (450°F) and line two baking trays with parchment.
2. Trim the Brussels sprouts and cut them in half from top to stem.
3. Blend the lemon juice, honey, garlic, cayenne, paprika, chilli flakes and half the salt into a smooth glaze. Toss the sprouts in it in a large bowl.
4. Lay the sprouts cut side down on the trays, leaving the extra glaze in the bowl.
5. Roast for 15–20 minutes, until golden. Grill for a final minute if you want them crisper.
6. Tip the hot sprouts back into the bowl, toss in the remaining glaze, sprinkle with the rest of the salt and serve right away.
