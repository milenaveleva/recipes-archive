## Method

1. **Preparation and marinating:** Remove the woody outer leaves of the lemongrass and chop the soft lower part very finely. Peel the shallots and garlic and crush or finely chop them.
2. In a bowl, mix the cleaned shrimp with the finely chopped lemongrass, half of the shallots, 1 clove of crushed garlic, 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of fish sauce, 1 teaspoon (5 g) of cane sugar and 1 tablespoon of sunflower oil. Leave for 15 minutes so the aromas penetrate the shrimp.
3. Soak 200 g of rice noodles according to the package instructions (about 3-4 minutes in hot water), then drain immediately and rinse with cold water so they stay springy and don't stick together.
4. **Vegetables and the nước chấm dressing:** Make the traditional nước chấm: in a small bowl, mix 2 tablespoons (30 ml) of fish sauce, 20 g of cane sugar, the freshly squeezed juice of 2 limes, 60 ml of lukewarm water, 2 cloves of finely crushed garlic and the thinly sliced red chilli. Stir until the sugar has completely dissolved.
5. Cut the carrot and cucumber into thin matchsticks (julienne). Tear or roughly chop the fresh mint and coriander. Crush the roasted unsalted peanuts roughly in a mortar or with a chopping knife.
6. **Cooking and assembly:** Heat a wok or frying pan over high heat with 1 tablespoon (15 ml) of sunflower oil. Add the remaining chopped shallots, then the marinated shrimp, and fry over high heat for 1.5-2 minutes per side until nicely pink and lightly caramelised.
7. Assemble the bowls: divide the rice noodles between two deep bowls. Arrange the crunchy carrot and cucumber strips, the fresh mint and the coriander alongside.
8. Place the hot, fragrant lemongrass shrimp on the noodles. Sprinkle generously with the crushed roasted peanuts and serve drizzled with the homemade nước chấm.

## Tips

- Always rinse Vietnamese rice noodles thoroughly under cold running water in a sieve before serving - this gives you separate, springy strands that soak up the dressing perfectly.
- For an extra crispy texture, fry part of the shallots separately in oil until crisp (hành phi) and sprinkle them over the bowl when serving.
