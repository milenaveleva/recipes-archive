## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the pasta:** Bring a large pot of plenty of water to the boil, then salt it generously with the fine salt. Wash the Brussels sprouts, remove the outer leaves, cut off the stems, then halve or quarter them to an even size. Cut the garlic into thin slices, grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze out its juice.
2. Add the tagliatelle to the boiling water and cook it 2 minutes less than the packet says, until al dente. Before draining, scoop out and set aside 1-2 ladlefuls of starchy cooking water for the sauce.
3. **Searing the sprouts and making the sauce:** In a wide frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil and half of the extra olive oil that replaces the butter over medium-high heat. Place the Brussels sprouts cut side down in the pan and fry for 4-5 minutes without moving them until they develop a deep golden-brown, caramelised crust.
4. Turn the sprouts, add the thinly sliced garlic and the pinch of chili flakes. Fry for another 1-2 minutes over low heat, taking care that the garlic does not burn but only becomes fragrant. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. **Assembly and serving:** Add the drained tagliatelle, the remaining olive oil, the grated lemon zest, 1-2 tablespoons of lemon juice and 1 ladle of the reserved pasta water to the seared sprouts. Toss and shake the pan over high heat for 1-2 minutes until the oil and the starchy cooking water form a glossy, silky emulsion on the pasta.
6. Take the pan off the heat, sprinkle in half of the freshly grated Parmesan and mix. Serve immediately in deep bowls, topped with the remaining Parmesan, a few drops of fresh lemon juice and freshly ground black pepper.

## Tips

- Caramelising the sprouts is key: the crust browned at high heat adds a sweet, nutty aroma to the vegetable and completely removes any bitter aftertaste.
- Do not skip adding the pasta water! The starch in the water combines with the olive oil to give a creamy, glossy coating on the tagliatelle even without cream.
