## Method

1. **For the lentil bites:** Put the lentils in a medium saucepan and add about 600 ml of water. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium heat. Simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes, adding water if needed so the lentils don't dry out. When the lentils are soft but not completely mushy, remove from the heat and mash with a potato masher to a rough purée, leaving some whole lentils. Set aside.
2. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 160 °C. Spread the finely chopped walnuts on a baking sheet and toast for 9-12 minutes until lightly golden and fragrant. After toasting, raise the oven temperature to 175 °C.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok and add the finely chopped mushrooms and garlic. Season with salt and cook over medium-high heat for 7-9 minutes until most of the mushroom liquid has evaporated. Then add the kale, toasted walnuts, cranberries and herbs. Stir and cook for a few more minutes until the kale wilts. Remove from the heat and stir in the mashed lentils.
4. In a small bowl, mix the ground flaxseed and water. Stir for 10 seconds, then immediately pour it into the pan mixture. Stir well so the whole mass comes together. Then add 100 g of oat flour and stir until fully incorporated. Season with salt and pepper. The mixture should be moist and sticky. If it is too sticky, add a little more oat flour; if it is dry, add a tablespoon of water.
5. Line a baking tray with baking paper. Shape balls from the lentil mixture and pack them tightly so they hold together. Place them on the baking paper about an inch apart. Repeat with the rest. If the mixture is too hot, let it cool a little first, then continue.
6. Bake the lentil balls at 175 °C for 15 minutes. Remove from the oven, carefully turn them over and bake for another 11-13 minutes until golden and firm on the outside.
7. **For the cranberry sauce:** Put the cranberries, pear and honey into a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium. Simmer uncovered for 10-20 minutes until the mixture thickens. Mash the pear with a potato masher.

## Tips

- If the mixture seems dry (this can happen if the lentils were cooked too long), feel free to add a little water so the mixture is moist enough to hold together.
