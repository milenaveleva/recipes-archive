## Method

1. Grate the cheddar on the large holes of a grater. Cut the red onion into small cubes, and trim the broccoli and break it into small florets.
2. Crush the garlic and sweat it together with the chopped onion in the olive oil in a larger pot until translucent. Pour in the broth, the plant-based cooking cream, the oat milk and the lemon juice.
3. Add the broccoli florets and the dry pasta to the pot. Stir and cook for about 10–15 minutes, stirring from time to time, until the pasta is tender and the sauce has thickened.
4. When the pasta is ready, add the salt, pepper, grated cheddar and nutmeg. Stir until the cheese has melted completely and the pasta turns creamy. Serve warm.
