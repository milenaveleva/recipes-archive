## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Peel the parsnips and cut them into roughly 1.5 cm cubes. On a baking tray, toss them with 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil, the finely crushed garlic and a pinch of salt and pepper. Spread evenly and roast for 20-25 minutes until golden brown, fragrant and caramelised.
2. Rinse the spelt (farro) thoroughly under cold running water, then cook in plenty of lightly salted hot water until al dente (about 20-25 minutes). When cooked, drain well and let it cool to room temperature.
3. **Toasting and assembly:** In a dry frying pan over low or medium heat, toast the pine nuts to golden brown in 2-3 minutes, stirring constantly, then immediately tip them into a bowl so they don't burn.
4. In a large mixing bowl, toss the cooled spelt with the sweet roasted parsnips and the fresh, crisp rocket.
5. In a small bowl mix the remaining 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil with the balsamic vinegar, the fine salt and freshly ground black pepper. Pour it over the salad, toss thoroughly, and finally sprinkle with the toasted pine nuts and thinly shaved aged pecorino.

## Tips

- You can add a few sprigs of fresh rosemary or thyme to the tray with the parsnips while roasting, which gives the roasted vegetables an even deeper Tuscan aroma.
- This salad is perfect lukewarm or cold, and the flavours meld even better by the next day, so it is ideal as a make-ahead office lunch.
