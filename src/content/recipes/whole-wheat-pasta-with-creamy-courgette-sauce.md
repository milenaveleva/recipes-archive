## Method

1. First take a larger pot, pour in enough water to cook the pasta, salt it and bring it to the boil over high heat. As soon as the water starts to boil, add the pasta and cook it for 17-18 minutes over medium heat.
2. Meanwhile, make the courgette sauce: peel and finely chop the onion and garlic. Cut the courgette into smaller cubes. Then take a larger frying pan and heat the olive oil in it over medium heat. As soon as the oil is hot, add the chopped onion, garlic and courgette cubes to the pan and fry for 4-5 minutes over medium heat. Then pour in the water, season with salt and pepper and cook for 7-8 minutes over medium heat.
3. Drain the cooked pasta. Grate the Parmesan finely. As soon as the courgette is soft in the pan, transfer the mixture to a tall blender jug, pour in the plant-based cooking cream, add the grated Parmesan and the fresh basil, and blend smooth with a stick blender. Pour the blended sauce back into the pan, add the drained pasta and season with salt and pepper.
4. Reheat the finished dish over low heat if needed, then serve.

## Tips

- Before serving, sprinkle the portions with finely grated Parmesan and garnish with a few fresh basil leaves.
