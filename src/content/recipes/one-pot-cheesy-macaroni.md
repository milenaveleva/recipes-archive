## Method

1. Grate the cheddar on the large holes of a grater.
2. Pour the oat milk into a large pot and bring to the boil over medium heat. Add the dry (not yet cooked) pasta, then the salt, pepper and ground paprika. Mix thoroughly.
3. Reduce the heat and cook the pasta, stirring frequently, for 10–15 minutes, until it is tender and has absorbed most of the milk.
4. When the pasta is cooked and surrounded by a creamy sauce, take the pot off the heat. Stir in the grated cheese and keep stirring until the cheese has melted completely and coats the pasta evenly. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- For an even creamier result you can use a richer oat drink or stir in a little extra virgin olive oil at the end.
