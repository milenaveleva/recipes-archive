## Method

1. Cook 200 g of linguine or spaghetti until al dente (reserve a cup of the drained cooking water).
2. Fry the garlic in a little olive oil (it should just dance in the oil, not brown).
3. Add the anchovies and chilli flakes.
4. Stir, then add the olives and capers.
5. When the base of the sauce is sizzling, add the canned tomatoes and cook over high heat for 3-4 minutes.
6. Add the al dente pasta and a little cooking water.
7. Cook together for 1-2 more minutes until the sauce coats the pasta evenly.
8. Serve the pasta with freshly chopped parsley.
