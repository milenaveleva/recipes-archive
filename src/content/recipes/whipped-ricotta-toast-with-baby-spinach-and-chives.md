## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200-205 °C. Prepare a baking tray and line it with baking paper.
2. Place the slices of toast bread on the tray and bake in the preheated oven for 6-7 minutes. Halfway through, turn the slices so both sides toast evenly.
3. While the bread toasts, make the cream. In a tall container put the baby spinach, chives, ricotta, plain yoghurt and olive oil. Season with salt and pepper, then blend with a hand blender until completely smooth and fluffy.
4. Take the toasted slices out of the oven. Spread them generously with the ricotta cream and serve immediately. Garnish with fresh sprouts or radishes if you like.
