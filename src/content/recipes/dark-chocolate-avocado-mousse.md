## Method

1. Place the chopped chocolate in a heatproof bowl and melt it slowly over gently simmering water.
2. Let the melted chocolate cool for 5 minutes.
3. Halve the avocados, remove the stones and spoon the flesh into a food processor fitted with the blade. Add the milk, dates, applesauce, maple syrup and melted chocolate. Blend for 3-4 minutes until the mixture is perfectly smooth.
4. Spoon the cream into 4 medium bowls or glasses, sprinkle with hazelnuts, decorate each with a square of 70% dark chocolate and serve immediately.

## Tips

- You can use plant-based milk, and serve with fresh berries instead of (or alongside) the chopped hazelnuts.
