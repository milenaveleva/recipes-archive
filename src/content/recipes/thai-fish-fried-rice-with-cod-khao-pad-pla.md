## Method

1. **Preparation:** If using fresh jasmine rice, cook it in lightly salted water, then spread it on a tray so it cools completely and the excess steam evaporates; the finest, loose fried rice is made from cold rice from the day before.
2. Pat the cod fillet dry with paper towel, then cut it into roughly 2-3 cm bite-sized cubes. Crush the garlic finely, slice the spring onions into rings (separating the white and green parts), and slice the chilli thinly.
3. In a small bowl, mix the fish sauce, soy sauce and cane sugar until the sugar is completely dissolved.
4. **Stir-frying in the wok:** In a wok or deep frying pan, heat half of the sunflower oil. Add the cod pieces and fry over high heat for 2-3 minutes until they get a nice golden crust and are just cooked through. Carefully lift them onto a plate, taking care that the fish pieces don't fall apart.
5. Pour the remaining oil into the pan, add the crushed garlic and the white part of the spring onion, and fry for 30 seconds until fragrant. Push the aromatics to the edge of the wok, crack the lightly beaten eggs into the cleared space and stir quickly into a soft scramble.
6. Add the cold jasmine rice, then stir-fry over high heat, turning continuously, for 2 minutes so the grains separate and take on the toasted flavours. Drizzle the prepared seasoning sauce around the edge of the pan and toss thoroughly.
7. Finally, gently fold the fried cod back in, sprinkle in the green part of the spring onion and the chopped red chilli. Toss for half a minute so the fish heats through but doesn't break up.
8. **Serving:** Pile the hot fried rice into deep plates. Serve with fresh cucumber slices, fresh cilantro and lime wedges, whose fresh juice perfectly balances the rich flavours of the fried fish.

## Tips

- The secret of a loose, authentic texture is cooled, drier rice: freshly cooked, steaming rice can easily clump and go soft in the wok.
- Fry the cod separately and only fold it back in at the end: that way the outside of the fish stays nicely golden, and the inside tender and juicy without falling apart while stirring.
