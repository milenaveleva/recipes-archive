## Method

1. **Preparation:** Put a large pot of generously salted water on to boil for the pasta.
2. Halve the radicchio, remove the core and cut into thin strips. Chop the garlic finely and cube the gorgonzola.
3. In a dry frying pan over low heat, toast the roughly chopped walnuts until golden and fragrant, 2-3 minutes, then set aside in a bowl.
4. **Cooking and making the sauce:** Cook the penne in the boiling water for 1-2 minutes less than the packet says, so it stays perfectly al dente. Before draining, set aside a cup of pasta cooking water.
5. In a deep frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil, add the chopped garlic and fry for half a minute until fragrant.
6. Add the radicchio strips to the pan, season with salt and pepper and fry over medium heat for 3-4 minutes until wilted and slightly caramelised.
7. Pour in the plant-based cooking cream and add the cubed gorgonzola. Stir over low heat until the cheese has melted completely and forms a creamy, velvety sauce.
8. **Assembly and serving:** Toss the cooked pasta into the creamy radicchio sauce. If needed, add a few tablespoons of pasta water so the sauce coats the penne beautifully.
9. Serve immediately while hot, sprinkled with the toasted walnuts, freshly ground black pepper and plenty of grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- If you like the characteristic, pleasantly bitter taste of radicchio less, soak the sliced strips in cold water for 10 minutes after cutting, then drain them well before frying.
- For the sauce choose the softer, sweeter Gorgonzola Dolce for a perfectly creamy texture.
