## Method

1. Cut the tomatoes into thin slices, and halve or quarter any cherry tomatoes.
2. Season them lightly with salt and pepper.
3. To serve, pile the tomatoes into a bowl with the mozzarella in the middle, drizzle generously with good olive oil and scatter with fresh basil leaves.
4. If you like, add a few drops of wine vinegar or lemon juice and use other fresh herbs. A few sun-dried tomatoes make a nice, more characterful addition. With a slice of whole-wheat sourdough toast it also works as a main course.
