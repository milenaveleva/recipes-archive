## Method

1. Roast the beetroot in the oven. Sprinkle a spoonful of sea salt onto a piece of foil, add a crushed garlic clove and 1–2 sprigs of thyme, wrap the beetroot in it and roast at 160 °C (320 °F) for about 1 hour.
2. Peel them while still warm. Cut half into wedges and blend the other half until smooth with the mascarpone.
3. For the risotto, heat the broth and keep it hot.
4. Finely chop the onion and sauté it with the crushed garlic in the olive oil.
5. When it turns glossy and translucent, add the rice and heat it through for 4–5 minutes, stirring so the onion doesn't burn.
6. Pour in the white wine and stir it in.
7. The rice absorbs the wine in about 2 minutes, then you can start adding the hot broth.
8. Add it one ladleful at a time, as much as the rice takes up, making sure the grains always stay under the liquid and the risotto keeps simmering gently. These two points are very important!
9. When the rice is almost tender, season with salt, pepper, a few drops of lemon juice and the mascarpone-beetroot puree. Always leave the result a little looser, because it will thicken by the time it reaches the table. Stir in the butter and grated Parmesan only at the very end with a wooden spoon or silicone spatula. The ideal texture is when it spreads slightly on the plate by itself once served.
10. Serve with good soft goat cheese, the roasted beetroot wedges, a few beet leaves and fresh herbs; you can brighten it with a little grated lemon zest or preserved lemon.
