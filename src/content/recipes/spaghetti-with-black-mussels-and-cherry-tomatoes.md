## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a deep frying pan, drop in the whole garlic clove, then add the washed and cleaned black mussels. Start frying them.
2. Add the quartered cherry tomatoes to the mussels. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti al dente in salted water in a pot. About 1 minute before the end of the cooking time, drain the pasta and add it to the pan with the mussels.
3. Pour in a little of the pasta cooking water and cook together for another minute so the flavours come together. Finally, sprinkle with freshly grated organic lemon zest and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Eat only the mussels that opened during cooking. Discard any that stayed closed.
- Nutrition counts about 100 g of mussel meat for the 300 g of mussels in shell.
