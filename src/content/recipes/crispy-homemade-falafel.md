## Method

1. **Preparation:** Soak the dried chickpeas in cold water for 10–12 hours. Then drain, rinse thoroughly with cold water and let drain completely in a colander.
2. Finely chop the onion, garlic, parsley and chilli pepper. Put them in a food processor with the chickpeas, salt and cumin and blend finely. Let the resulting mixture rest in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
3. **Shaping and frying:** Form 20 balls from the rested mixture, then press them lightly with your palm into patties about 1 cm thick.
4. Heat plenty of oil to 160 °C (320 °F) in a pot over medium heat. Fry the falafel in batches for 2–2.5 minutes per side until golden brown. It is important that the falafel floats in the oil. Transfer the finished falafel to a plate lined with paper towels to absorb excess fat.

## Tips

- You can add a pinch of baking soda to the mixture to make the falafel even lighter and crispier.
- Serve in fresh whole-wheat pita with tahini sauce and plenty of fresh vegetables.
