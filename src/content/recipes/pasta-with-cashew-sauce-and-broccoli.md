## Method

1. Soak the cashews in water for at least 2 hours. Then pour off the water, put them in a blender, add 100 ml of fresh water and blend until smooth. Add the nutritional yeast, garlic granules, salt and pepper and blend again until you get a creamy sauce. Do not worry if it looks a little thin, the pasta will absorb the excess liquid.
2. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions. Divide the broccoli into florets and steam until tender. Halve the cherry tomatoes and cube the marinated tofu or tear it into smaller pieces.
3. Mix the cooked pasta with the cashew sauce, then gently fold in the steamed broccoli, tomatoes and tofu. Mix thoroughly and it is ready to serve.

## Tips

- For an even creamier texture, you can use plant-based cream instead of the soaking water in the sauce.
