## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil for the pasta. Cook the penne according to the packet instructions, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. When the oil is hot, add the finely chopped onion and the whole garlic cloves. Fry over medium heat for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Cut the bell pepper into large pieces of about 2 × 2 cm. Add to the pan and fry for a further 5-6 minutes. Then pour in the vegetable broth and add the chopped tomatoes and the oregano. Simmer over medium heat for 12-13 minutes, until the pepper is completely soft.
4. Take the pan off the heat, add the fresh basil, then blend everything with a stick blender into a smooth, creamy sauce. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Add the cooked penne to the smooth sauce, toss thoroughly, then divide into portions and serve.

## Tips

- When serving, garnish the plates with fresh basil leaves and drizzle with a little high-quality extra virgin olive oil. You can also sprinkle grated Parmesan over the top.
