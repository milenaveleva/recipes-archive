## Method

1. **Preparation:** Peel and deseed the pumpkin and cut it into roughly 2 cm cubes. Finely chop the onion, garlic and fresh ginger. Rinse the canned lentils and drain in a sieve.
2. Put the basmati rice on to cook in twice its volume of lightly salted water, then after it boils, cook covered over low heat for 12-15 minutes until tender and fluffy.
3. **Cooking:** In a large saucepan, heat 2 tablespoons of coconut oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and fry for 4-5 minutes until translucent and golden. Add the crushed garlic and grated ginger and fry for another minute until fragrant.
4. Sprinkle the ground cumin and garam masala into the pan and stir for half a minute so the spices release their aroma. Take care that they do not burn.
5. Pour in the canned chopped tomatoes and the creamy coconut milk, stir thoroughly, then add the diced pumpkin and 1 teaspoon of salt. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes until the pumpkin cubes are butter-soft.
6. Add the rinsed canned lentils and the fresh baby spinach to the curry. Cook for another 3-4 minutes until the spinach leaves have just wilted and the curry has thickened nicely. If needed, taste and season with another pinch of salt.
7. **Serving:** Serve the hot, fragrant pumpkin lentil curry alongside freshly steamed basmati rice. Sprinkle generously with fresh chopped coriander and drizzle with fresh lime juice for a perfectly balanced, exotic flavour.

## Tips

- Leftover curry keeps perfectly for 3 days in a closed container in the fridge. When reheated the flavours meld even more; if it is too thick, loosen it with a little water or coconut milk.
- For an even more substantial meal, serve warm garlic naan bread or crispy papadums with it.
