## Method

1. Finely chop the red onion and tear the basil leaves into smaller pieces.
2. Cut out the centres of the tomatoes and dice the flesh into roughly 0.5 cm pieces. Put them in a bowl, add the onion, salt, pepper, basil, vinegar and olive oil, and mix well.
3. Toast both sides of the bread slices in a pan over medium heat until golden brown. While still warm, rub them with the garlic, then pile the tomato mixture on top and garnish with Parmesan shavings.

## Tips

- For the best result, use ripe, juicy tomatoes and good-quality whole-wheat sourdough bread.
