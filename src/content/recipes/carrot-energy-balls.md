## Method

1. Peel and grate the carrots.
2. Grind the oats, desiccated coconut and almonds finely.
3. Pit the dates and purée them with 150 ml of water.
4. Combine everything in a bowl, add a pinch of salt and flavour with cinnamon.
5. Mix it all together, then shape into balls and roll them in desiccated coconut.
