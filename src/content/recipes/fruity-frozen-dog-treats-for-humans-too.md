## Method

1. Blend the fruit with 2 tablespoons of water, then pour into small moulds. An ice cube tray works perfectly too, so you can give your pet smaller cooling bites. It can also be spread into a LickiMat; the main thing is that you can easily get it out. Then place in the freezer. For dogs that are not lactose intolerant, the water can be replaced with plain yogurt.
