## Method

1. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the onion until translucent, adding the garlic at the end and sautéing for another 30-60 seconds (be careful not to burn it).
2. Then add the spices and the sweet potato and pour in the broth or water. Bring to the boil and cook covered until soft: about 15-20 minutes over low heat. Add the coconut milk and blend with a hand blender until smooth.
3. Dietitian Dorottya Szarka's tip: "To make the soup even more substantial and richer in pigments and antioxidants, cook one or two carrots, a parsley root, a piece of celeriac or any other leftover vegetables along with the sweet potato. It is an excellent way to use up leftovers. Not only will the dish be richer in fibre, its flavour will also be deeper and more distinctive, so it can even stand as a one-pot meal!"
4. Source: Mentes Anyu EXPRESSZ cookbook; portrait photo: Gábor Nagy
5. This soup is not only nourishing and a feast for the eyes, every spoonful is a real treat. The silky coconut cream and the sweetness of the sweet potato make a perfect pair, completed by the warmth of the spices. It is quick to make yet special, a perfect choice for a cool weekday evening or a relaxed weekend lunch.
