## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil, lower in the whole squash and boil for 10 minutes, turning it over halfway. Lift it out and let it cool.
2. Peel the squash, halve it, scoop out the seeds and cut the flesh into cubes; you should have about 4 cups.
3. Put the squash in a pot with the broth, onion, garlic, curry powder, ½ tsp salt and the lime juice. Bring to a boil, then keep at a brisk simmer, stirring often, for 15 to 20 minutes until the squash is soft.
4. Blend everything until smooth, starting slowly with the lid vented, adding the water if the soup is too thick and the remaining salt to taste.
5. Serve warm, sprinkled with red pepper flakes if you like.

## Tips

- Butternut squash, acorn squash or sweet potato work in place of kabocha; use about 6 cups cubed.
- Choose a low-sodium vegetable broth without added oil, or use water.
