## Method

1. **Preparation:** Press the tofu thoroughly with paper towels and wipe it dry, then cut it into small cubes of about 1.5 cm.
2. Peel the carrot and cut it into small cubes, slice the spring onions (set the green parts aside for serving), crush the garlic and finely grate the ginger. Beat the eggs in a small bowl with a fork and a pinch of salt.
3. For fried rice, the best is leftover cold cooked rice from the day before (or freshly cooked rice spread out and cooled completely), so it stays loose and doesn't clump while frying.
4. **Frying in the wok:** Heat a wok or heavy-bottomed pan over high heat until smoking. Pour in 1 tablespoon of sunflower oil, add the tofu cubes and fry for 4–5 minutes until all sides are golden and crispy. Remove to a plate.
5. Pour a little more oil into the hot pan, add the beaten eggs and stir quickly for 30 seconds until they form a soft scramble, then set them aside next to the tofu.
6. Pour the remaining oil into the wok, add the diced carrot, garlic, ginger and the white part of the spring onions. Stir-fry over high heat, stirring constantly, for 2 minutes, then add the frozen peas.
7. Tip the cold cooked rice over the vegetables, break up the grains with a wooden spoon and fry over high heat for 3–4 minutes so it heats through and picks up a slight crust.
8. Return the fried tofu cubes and soft egg to the wok. Drizzle with the soy sauce and sesame oil and toss for 1 more minute so the flavours come together perfectly.
9. **Serving:** Take the wok off the heat. Spoon the fried rice onto plates or into bowls and sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and the reserved fresh spring onion rings. Serve immediately, piping hot!

## Tips

- The secret of fried rice is cold, dry-surfaced rice: freshly cooked warm rice easily turns mushy in the pan, so it is always worth cooking ahead or using leftover rice from the day before.
- If you like heat, drizzle with sriracha sauce when serving or sprinkle with a little chilli flakes.
