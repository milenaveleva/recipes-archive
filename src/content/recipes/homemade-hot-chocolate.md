## Method

1. In a small saucepan, mix the cocoa powder with the cornstarch and sugar.
2. Pour in the milk, stir, and start heating over low heat. Keep stirring continuously so it doesn't go lumpy.
3. When it starts to bubble, turn the heat down and add the chopped dark chocolate.
4. Heat until the chocolate has melted. Add more sugar to taste if you like.
5. When it starts to bubble again, turn off the heat.
6. Pour the finished hot chocolate into two mugs and serve topped with whipped cream.
