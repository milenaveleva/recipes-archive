## Method

1. **Making the pesto:** Chop the ingredients or put them in a food processor and pour in some of the olive oil. Blitz, drizzling in more oil as you go. Keep blending to a paste; it doesn't matter if it stays chunky, that's good for the texture.
2. **Assembly:** While making the pesto, put the pasta on to cook (follow the packet; whole-wheat pasta usually needs 1-2 minutes longer than white).
3. Fry the onions in olive oil in a pan and season. Spoon in the pesto and pour in the cream. Simmer the sauce together, then toss in the cooked pasta. Sprinkle with the grated cheese and mix thoroughly so everything is evenly coated.
4. Serve fresh and hot, sprinkled with grated Parmesan and fresh parsley.
