## Method

1. Cook the pasta in salted water until "al dente", then drain.
2. In a saucepan, heat the olive oil over medium heat, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute, whisking, until smooth.
3. Slowly pour in the oat milk and the plant-based cooking cream, stirring constantly.
4. Add the Dijon mustard, smoked paprika, salt and pepper, then gradually stir in the gruyère and parmigiano until completely melted and creamy.
5. Stir in the cooked pasta and toss thoroughly with the silky cheese sauce.
6. For a crisp-topped version, pour it into a baking dish, sprinkle with breadcrumbs and extra cheese, then bake at 180°C for 10-15 minutes, until golden brown.
7. Enjoy it hot, creamy and irresistibly cheesy!
