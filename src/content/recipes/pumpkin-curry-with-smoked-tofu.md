## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Wash the pumpkin, halve it and scrape out the seeds. Cut it into slices about 1.5 cm thick, place on a baking tray, drizzle with a little olive oil and roast for 15 minutes.
2. **Making the curry:** Cut the onion into strips and slice the garlic. Heat 1 teaspoon of olive oil in a frying pan and fry the onion and garlic. Add the cubed smoked tofu and fry for a few minutes.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and curry paste. Fry for a minute, then pour in 200 ml of water and the coconut milk. Finally add the roasted pumpkin pieces. Season with soy sauce and salt and simmer together. Serve sprinkled with fresh Thai basil and chilli to taste.
4. **Serving:** Serve the finished curry hot with steamed rice.

## Tips

- For a creamier texture, purée some of the roasted pumpkin into the sauce.
- You can use fresh coriander instead of Thai basil for garnish.
