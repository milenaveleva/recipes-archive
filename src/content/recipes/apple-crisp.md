## Method

1. Heat the oven to 160°C (325°F).
2. Spread the chopped apples in a baking dish of about 28 × 18 cm and toss them with the cinnamon, lemon juice and lemon zest.
3. In a bowl, stir together the oats, pecans, coconut, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, honey and coconut oil until evenly coated.
4. Scatter the oat mixture over the apples.
5. Bake for 40–45 minutes, until the topping is golden and the apples are tender.
6. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- Walnuts work as well as pecans.
- For a more biscuit-like topping, swap ½ cup of the oats for almond meal.
