## Method

1. Finely chop the red onion. Tear the basil leaves into smaller pieces.
2. Cut out the cores of the tomatoes, then dice the flesh into roughly 0.5 cm pieces. Put in a bowl, add the chopped onion, salt, pepper, basil, vinegar and olive oil, and mix well.
3. In a pan over medium heat, toast both sides of the bread slices until golden brown. Rub the still-warm bread with the peeled garlic clove, pile on the tomato mixture and finally garnish with Parmesan shavings.
