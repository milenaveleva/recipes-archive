## Method

1. Finely chop the onion, carrots and celery. Heat the olive oil in a large pot, then sweat the vegetables over low heat, stirring occasionally, for about 8 minutes, until soft and lightly browned. Add the crushed garlic and fry briefly until fragrant.
2. Stir in the vegan mince, turn up the heat and fry, stirring constantly, until browned, breaking it into smaller pieces with a wooden spoon. Add the tomato paste, let it caramelise briefly, then pour in the passata and the vegetable broth. Add the rinsed red lentils, the finely chopped rosemary and basil, and season with salt and pepper.
3. Turn the heat down to the minimum and let the sauce bubble gently, uncovered, for at least an hour. Stir occasionally and, if needed, add a little more broth or water. The vegetables must be completely soft and the sauce thick and uniform.
4. Serve the finished sauce with freshly cooked pasta. If you like, sprinkle with vegan cheese.

## Tips

- The sauce tastes even better if it rests in the fridge overnight, so the flavours can meld.
