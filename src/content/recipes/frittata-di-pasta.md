## Method

1. If you are using leftover pasta, take it out of the fridge so that it comes to room temperature. If you are making it fresh, cook the pasta according to the packet instructions, then leave it to cool.
2. In a bowl, beat the eggs with the grated Parmesan. Add the diced mozzarella and the finely chopped onion. Stir in the cooked pasta too and toss thoroughly so the egg coats everything. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
3. In a medium frying pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Pour the pasta and egg mixture into the pan and spread it out evenly. Cook the frittata for 5-7 minutes, until the bottom is golden brown and the top starts to set.
4. If your pan is ovenproof, finish cooking under the grill until the top is golden brown too. If not, place a large plate over the pan, carefully flip the frittata onto it, then slide it back into the pan and cook the other side for a further 3-5 minutes.

## Tips

- You can use any type of leftover pasta and happily vary it with different cheeses or vegetables, depending on what you have in the fridge.
