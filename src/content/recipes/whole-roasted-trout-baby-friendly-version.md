## Method

1. Check the cleaned trout carefully and scrape off any scales you find, then rinse it and pat off the excess water with a kitchen towel.
2. Crush or very finely chop the garlic and mix it thoroughly with the salt, a little pepper and the oil. Rub this seasoned oil over the trout inside and out, then place a few slices of unwaxed citrus and the herb inside the fish.
3. Place the fish on a baking tray lined with baking paper and roast at 185 °C (365 °F) for about 20 minutes until cooked through.

## Tips

- Children under 1 year can safely eat this fish, but be very careful with the bones. For a baby, serve it with soft steamed vegetables, either pureed or in pieces; adults can have it with roasted or steamed vegetables.
