## Method

1. **Preparation:** Cut the carrot and cucumber into thin matchsticks (julienne).
2. Put the carrot in a bowl, add 1 tablespoon sugar, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar and a little fish sauce. Let it stand so the flavours meld.
3. Pour hot water over the glass noodles, cover for 3 minutes, then drain. Let them drain thoroughly, then cut into shorter pieces, about 3-4 cm.
4. **Assembly:** In a large bowl mix the glass noodles, bean sprouts, pickled carrot, chopped spring onions and fresh cilantro.
5. Make the dressing by mixing the soy sauce, the remaining fish sauce, the sugar, the sesame oil and the finely chopped chilli. Pour it over the salad and toss thoroughly.
6. **Cooking and serving:** Quickly sear the shrimp in a little oil with the crushed garlic and grated ginger. Season with a little fish sauce, salt and pepper, then place on top of the salad.
7. Toast the peanuts in a dry frying pan, let them cool, then crush roughly.
8. Sprinkle the toasted peanuts and sesame seeds over the served salad. Add more salt and pepper afterwards if you like.
