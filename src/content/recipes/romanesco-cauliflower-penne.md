## Method

1. Put a large pot of generously salted water on to boil for the pasta. Wash the Romanesco cauliflower, then cut it into bite-sized florets. Put the pasta into the rolling boiling water, and halfway through the cooking time (after about 5–6 minutes) add the cauliflower florets too, so that they cook al dente (crisp) together with the pasta.
2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan over medium heat until golden, 1–2 minutes, taking care that they do not burn, then set them aside in a small bowl.
3. In the same pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil together with the additional olive oil. Add the thinly sliced garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes, and fry gently for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Before draining the pasta and cauliflower, set aside a small mugful (about 100 ml) of the starchy cooking water. Drain the pasta and cauliflower florets, then pour them into the garlicky oil in the pan.
5. Add the finely grated Parmesan, the grated zest of the organic lemon and a little lemon juice, plus salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste. Pour in 2–3 tablespoons of cooking water and toss, shaking the pan, until a silky emulsion coats the pasta.
6. Serve immediately while hot, sprinkled with the toasted pine nuts and a little extra grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- It is worth cooking the Romanesco florets with the pasta for the last few minutes: they stay crunchy, and the pasta water gives even richer flavour to the emulsion sauce.
- If you like it hotter, fry a little fresh chilli pepper with the garlic in the pan.
