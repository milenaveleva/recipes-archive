## Method

1. Pour the water into a saucepan and bring to a boil. Sprinkle the dried wakame into the boiling water and simmer over moderate heat for 10 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, cut the tofu into cubes, slice the spring onions into thin rings and chop the coriander finely.
3. Add the diced tofu, spring onions and soy sauce to the boiling water and cook for another 5 minutes, then stir in the miso paste thoroughly.
4. Cook for the last 5 minutes until the paste has completely dissolved. Serve sprinkled with the chopped coriander and sesame seeds.
