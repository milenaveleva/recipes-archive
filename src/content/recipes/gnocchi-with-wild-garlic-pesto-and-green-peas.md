## Method

1. **Making the pesto:** Wash the wild garlic thoroughly and tear it into smaller pieces. Put the wild garlic, the finely grated Pecorino, the olive oil, the lemon juice and the hazelnuts (ground hazelnuts can be used too) into a tall container. Season with salt to taste, then blend with a stick blender into a smooth pesto. Store any leftover pesto in an airtight jar covered with olive oil (to prevent oxidation) in the fridge for up to a month.
2. **Cooking the gnocchi and peas:** In a frying pan, in a little olive oil, quickly fry the frozen peas for about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Cook the gnocchi according to the packet instructions (the cooking time depends on whether you use fresh or dried gnocchi). Toss the cooked gnocchi with 4 tablespoons of the pesto and the fried peas. When serving, sprinkle with grated Pecorino.
3. **Serving and storage tips:** The pesto can be frozen; in that case store it in a plastic container and do not pour extra oil over it. This pesto is excellent with pasta and risotto, spread on toast with a boiled egg, or even to flavour baked potatoes.

## Tips

- If you have no Pecorino, you can use Parmesan or Grana Padano for the pesto too.
- Toast the hazelnuts in a dry frying pan before serving for an even more intense flavour.
- Only about 4 tablespoons of the pesto go on the gnocchi, so the rest of the batch keeps for later meals.
