## Method

1. Cook the pasta in boiling salted water according to the packet instructions, taking care that it stays al dente.
2. In a frying pan, heat the olive oil and sweat the finely chopped onion until translucent. Add the crushed garlic and fry briefly until fragrant. Pour in the peeled tomatoes, rinse the cans with about 100 ml of water and add that to the sauce as well. Season with salt and pepper, then cook over low heat for about 20 minutes.
3. Blend the tomato base with a stick blender until silky smooth. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the plant-based cooking cream (do not boil it any further). Taste and, if needed, season with more salt and pepper, and add chili flakes to taste.
4. Toss the cooked pasta with the sauce and the chopped parsley. Serve sprinkled generously with finely grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- If you want an even creamier result, set aside a little of the pasta cooking water and stir it into the sauce when you add the pasta.
