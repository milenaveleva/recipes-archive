## Method

1. **Preparation and marinating:** Wash the vine tomatoes, cut them into small cubes and put them in a roomy mixing bowl. Add the finely crushed garlic, the generous amount of extra virgin olive oil, one teaspoon of salt and a pinch of freshly ground black pepper.
2. Mix thoroughly, then tear in half of the fresh basil leaves. Leave at room temperature for 15–20 minutes so the tomatoes release their juice and the flavours come together.
3. Drain the mozzarella, then cut it into small cubes and set aside.
4. **Cooking and assembly:** Bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot. Cook the penne al dente (firm to the bite) according to the packet time.
5. Drain the pasta, then toss it immediately, while still hot, with the marinated tomato mixture resting in the bowl. The hot pasta gently warms the raw tomatoes and releases their aromas.
6. Now add the diced mozzarella too and mix lightly so that the cheese softens slightly but does not turn runny.
7. **Serving:** Divide the pasta among deep plates, garnish with the remaining fresh basil leaves and sprinkle with freshly grated Parmesan. Serve immediately!

## Tips

- Always choose fully ripe, aromatic summer tomatoes, because the sauce is not cooked and the tomatoes' own flavour defines the dish.
- The pasta can also be eaten lukewarm or even as a cold pasta salad on a hot summer day or at a picnic.
