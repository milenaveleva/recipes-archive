## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C (180 °C with fan). Rinse the millet thoroughly with hot water in a fine sieve to remove any bitter taste. Put the millet in a medium saucepan with 500 ml of lightly salted water. After it comes to the boil, turn the heat to the lowest setting, cover and cook for 15–18 minutes, until the millet has absorbed all the liquid and is tender. Turn off the heat, leave covered to steam for another 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
2. Wash the pears, cut them into wedges and remove the cores. Place on a baking tray lined with baking paper, toss with 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil and a pinch of salt, and bake in the preheated oven for 18–20 minutes until nicely golden and caramelised.
3. Meanwhile, toast the almonds in a dry pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant and crunchy, then set aside to cool.
4. Make the dressing: in a small bowl, whisk the remaining extra virgin olive oil with the Modena balsamic vinegar, honey, a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper until you get a silky emulsion.
5. **Assembly:** In a large salad bowl, mix the still-warm millet with the fresh arugula and two thirds of the dressing. Pile onto plates, top with the warm roasted pear wedges, crumble over the creamy goat cheese, sprinkle with the toasted almonds and finally drizzle with the remaining balsamic dressing. Serve immediately while lukewarm.

## Tips

- You can also throw 1–2 sprigs of fresh thyme or rosemary onto the tray with the pears for more intense Tuscan herbiness.
- Excellent as a lukewarm autumn dinner or as a cold office lunch the next day; the millet beautifully absorbs the flavours of the honey-balsamic dressing.
