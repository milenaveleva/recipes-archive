## Method

1. **Make the pangrattato (crispy breadcrumbs):** In a small frying pan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil, then add the breadcrumbs. Toast over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 3–4 minutes, until beautifully golden brown and crunchy. Turn off the heat, stir in a pinch of salt and set aside in a small bowl.
2. **Cook the pasta and broccoli:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, then salt it generously. Cut the broccoli into small florets and slice the tender stem parts into thin rounds. When the water is at a rolling boil, add the dry pasta and the broccoli pieces at the same time and cook until al dente for the time stated on the pasta packet. Before draining, scoop out 1.5–2 ladlefuls (about 150 ml) of the cooking water, because you will need it for the silky sauce!
3. **Assemble and serve:** In a deep, wide frying pan, heat the remaining olive oil. Add the thinly sliced garlic and the chili flakes and fry over low heat for 1 minute, until fragrant (take care not to burn it). Tip the drained broccoli pasta into the pan, pour in the reserved starchy cooking water and toss over high heat for 1–2 minutes, so that the broccoli coats the pasta creamily.
4. Take the pan off the heat, sprinkle with freshly grated Parmesan, the grated zest of the organic lemon and a little freshly ground black pepper. Toss once more, then divide into deep plates and top generously with the golden, crunchy breadcrumbs. Serve immediately, piping hot!

## Tips

- Cooking the broccoli and pasta in one pot not only saves time, but the broccoli also becomes perfectly soft and, mixed with the starchy cooking water, forms a soft, creamy coating on the pasta even without cream.
- The freshness of the lemon zest and the crunchy breadcrumbs (pangrattato) are an essential part of the southern Italian pasta experience: the silky and crunchy textures complement each other perfectly.
