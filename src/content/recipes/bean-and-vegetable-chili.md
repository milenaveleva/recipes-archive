## Method

1. Soak the kidney beans overnight, drain, then boil hard in fresh water for 10 minutes and simmer for about 1 hour until tender. Drain.
2. Set a deep pot over medium-high heat and cook the onion for 3–5 minutes until soft, adding a little water if it sticks.
3. Add the garlic, carrot and red pepper and cook for another 3–5 minutes.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, beans, water, cumin, paprika, oregano, chili powder, coconut aminos and salt.
5. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer and cook uncovered for 15–20 minutes until the carrot is tender and the chili has thickened.
6. Serve hot with a squeeze of lime and a scattering of cilantro.

## Tips

- Use vegetable broth instead of water for a deeper flavour.
- Add a second tablespoon of chili powder for more heat.
