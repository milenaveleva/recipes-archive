## Method

1. **Cook the pasta:** Bring water to the boil in a large pot. When it boils, add salt, then add the penne. Cook over medium heat for 10-12 minutes, or according to the packet instructions, until al dente. Before draining, set aside a mug (about 125 ml) of the cooking water, then drain the pasta.
2. **Prepare the vegetables:** Peel the onion and garlic. Chop the onion finely and crush or very finely chop the garlic. Chop the fresh basil too.
3. **Make the sauce:** In a deeper frying pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onion and sweat for 2-3 minutes. Then add the garlic and chilli flakes and fry for another 30-60 seconds. Stir in the tomato paste and fry for 1 more minute.
4. **Thicken:** Pour in the plant-based cooking cream, add the cayenne pepper and the chopped basil. Cook over low heat for 1-2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. **Assemble the dish:** Add the drained pasta and the reserved cooking water to the sauce. Toss thoroughly so the sauce coats the pasta everywhere. Finally add the olive oil and the finely grated Parmesan. Stir over low heat for 1-2 minutes, until the cheese has melted completely and forms a creamy coating on the pasta.
6. **Serving:** Divide the creamy tomato pasta between plates and serve immediately. You can garnish with extra basil or Parmesan.
