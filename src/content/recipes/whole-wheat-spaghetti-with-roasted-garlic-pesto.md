## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 210–220 °C.
2. Prepare a piece of aluminium foil of about 30 × 30 cm. Place the garlic cloves in the middle of the foil and drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Wrap the garlic tightly, put it in the oven and bake for about 45 minutes, until completely soft and fragrant.
3. Meanwhile, boil salted water in a large pot and cook the spaghetti according to the packet instructions. Drain when done.
4. As soon as the garlic is roasted, take it out of the foil and put it in a bowl. Add the remaining olive oil, the chopped parsley, the basil, salt and pepper. Mash the roasted garlic thoroughly with a fork and mix everything into a thick pesto.
5. Toss the pesto with the cooked spaghetti. If necessary, warm it together in a frying pan over medium heat. Sprinkle generously with grated Parmesan when serving.
6. To garnish, you can drizzle with a little more olive oil and make it even more appealing with fresh basil leaves.
