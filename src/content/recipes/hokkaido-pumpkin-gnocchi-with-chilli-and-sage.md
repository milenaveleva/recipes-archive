## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Peel the pumpkin, cut it into cubes and roast it in an oven preheated to 180 degrees for about 30 minutes, until completely soft.
2. After roasting, leave it to cool, then mix in the white pepper, the ground nutmeg and the salt. Gradually work in the flour until you get a soft, non-sticky dough. If necessary, add a little more flour.
3. **Shaping and cooking the gnocchi:** Divide the dough into 4 equal parts. Shape each into a roll, then cut it into 1 cm pieces. Shape these into balls and press them with a fork to give them the characteristic gnocchi shape.
4. Cook the gnocchi in salted water enriched with a little oil. Cook for about 2-3 minutes, until they float to the surface.
5. **Finishing and serving:** In a frying pan, in the olive oil, fry the sliced garlic and the chilli cut into strips, then add the whole sage leaves and the thyme sprigs. Finally toss with the cooked gnocchi and fry them together. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Serve the gnocchi sprinkled with freshly grated Parmesan.
