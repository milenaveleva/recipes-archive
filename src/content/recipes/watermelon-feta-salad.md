## Method

1. Dice the watermelon and put it in a bowl with the sliced cucumber, crumbled feta and the fresh herbs. Toss gently.
2. For the dressing, whisk the olive oil with the honey and lime juice until creamy.
3. Pour over the salad, toss, and you're done - a refreshing summer treat!
