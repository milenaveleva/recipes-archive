## Method

1. Steam the potatoes over a few inches of simmering water, covered, for 20 to 25 minutes until soft.
2. Mash the potatoes smooth in a large bowl, then work in the potato starch and knead to a soft dough that no longer sticks, adding a little more starch if needed.
3. Meanwhile, dry-fry the onion in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for 3 to 5 minutes until soft.
4. Add the ginger, curry powder, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, chopped potato and broth, stir, cover and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until the potato is tender.
5. Add the peas, cilantro and lemon juice and cook uncovered for 2 to 3 minutes more, until the liquid is gone and the peas are bright green. Set aside.
6. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment.
7. Dust the counter with potato starch, halve the dough and roll each half into a circle about 20 cm wide and 6 mm thick.
8. Cut each circle into 4 wedges, spoon about 1 tbsp of filling onto each, fold the corners up over the filling and pinch the seams closed into a triangle.
9. Set the samosas on the baking sheet and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until browned. Let them cool for 5 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- Any potato works; Yukon Gold is a good choice.
- Cauliflower can replace the chopped potato in the filling, and water can replace the broth.
- Makes 8 to 10 samosas.
