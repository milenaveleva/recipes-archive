## Method

1. Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F).
2. Mash the bananas in a medium baking dish, then stir in the water, honey, vanilla and cinnamon.
3. Add the oats and baking powder and mix until evenly combined. Fold in the strawberries.
4. Level the top. Press a few banana and strawberry slices into the surface if you like.
5. Bake uncovered for 35–40 minutes, until set and lightly golden but still soft in the middle.
6. Rest for a few minutes to firm up, then serve with a drizzle of honey.

## Tips

- Frozen strawberries work too: thaw and drain them first.
- Quick-cook oats give a softer, pudding-like bake.
