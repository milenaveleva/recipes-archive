## Method

1. Put a large pot of water on to boil and, when it is boiling, salt it generously. Add the orecchiette and cook for 1-2 minutes less than the package time so that it stays truly al dente. Before draining, scoop out a small mugful of the cooking water, because it will form the base of the silky sauce.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat the extra virgin olive oil in a spacious pan. Add the thinly sliced garlic and the sliced chilli. Fry over medium heat for 1 minute, taking care that the garlic does not burn.
3. Pour the halved cherry tomatoes into the pan. Fry for 3-4 minutes, shaking the pan, until the skins start to wrinkle slightly and the tomatoes release sweet, flavourful juice. Season with salt to taste.
4. Toss the drained pasta directly into the tomato ragù in the pan. Add a few tablespoons of the reserved cooking water and toss over high heat so the pasta takes up the garlicky tomato sauce.
5. Turn off the heat, then immediately sprinkle the fresh arugula and two thirds of the finely grated pecorino over the pasta. From the residual heat of the pan the arugula just wilts and keeps its fresh, crisp aroma. Serve immediately in deep plates, topped with the remaining pecorino and a little fresh arugula.

## Tips

- Arugula wilts in no time, so be sure to mix it into the pasta only off the heat; that way it stays bright green and crisp.
- If you like even more character, you can dissolve 2-3 finely chopped anchovy fillets in the olive oil together with the garlic, as is traditional in Puglia.
