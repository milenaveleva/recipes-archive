## Method

1. Peel the garlic cloves, then gently crush them with the flat of a knife blade to release their precious aromas.
2. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat 3 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil. Add the garlic cloves and cook over low heat, watching constantly, for 2–3 minutes until fragrant but not browned at all.
3. Pour in 1.2 litres of water and add the bay leaves, thyme sprigs, rosemary sprig, salt and freshly ground black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, covered, for 20–25 minutes so the aromas fully infuse the soup.
4. While the soup cooks, drizzle the baguette slices with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and toast them in a dry pan or the oven until golden and crisp.
5. Take the finished soup off the heat and strain it (or leave the garlic cloves in and lift out the herb sprigs). Gently poach the eggs one at a time in the simmering garlic broth for 3–4 minutes until the white is set but the yolk is still runny.
6. To serve, place a slice of toasted baguette in the bottom of each deep plate, carefully set a poached egg on top, then ladle over the hot, fragrant garlic broth. Garnish with fresh herbs and serve immediately.

## Tips

- According to Provençal tradition, you can mash the garlic cloves cooked in the soup with a fork and spread them on the toasted baguette for an even richer experience.
