## Method

1. I worked the whole-wheat pita ingredients (recipe in the video) into a small ball of dough and divided it into equal portions. Don't worry if the dough stays a bit sticky, it will still be perfect!
2. I baked them in a 180 °C oven for 7 minutes on each side.
3. I cleaned, cut and washed the cauliflower. I seasoned it with a little olive oil and spices, then it went into the oven and turned crispy in about 20 minutes.
4. This time I used a curry sauce base on the cauliflower, which also contained some vegetables (feel free to add whatever you have in the fridge). The curry sauce can also be made with cooking cream or yoghurt, you only need basic curry powder, which I recently bought because I had long wanted to try a similar recipe.
5. I love this recipe because the result is very special, yet you don't need to overthink the preparation at all. It is ready in about 30 minutes and with the whole-wheat pita it is very filling!
