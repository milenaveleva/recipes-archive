## Method

1. Bring water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta. Salt it, then cook the whole-wheat pasta according to the packet instructions (about 17-18 minutes). When done, drain and rinse under cold water.
2. While the pasta cooks, make the dressing in a large bowl: mix the lemon juice, olive oil, chili flakes, salt and pepper.
3. Peel the avocado, remove the stone and cut it into small cubes. Peel the red onion and slice it thinly. Wash the cherry tomatoes and halve them. Add the avocado, onion, tomatoes and the drained corn to the dressing.
4. Finally, tear the basil into the bowl, add the cooled pasta and toss thoroughly. Let it rest in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before serving so the flavours come together.
