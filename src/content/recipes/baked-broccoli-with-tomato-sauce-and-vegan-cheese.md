## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C.
2. Brush a heatproof glass dish with a little sunflower oil. Add the broccoli, cut into 5-7 cm florets, then add the sun-dried tomato vegetable spread.
3. Add the halved cherry tomatoes next to the broccoli and grate the vegan cheese over the top. Season with salt to taste and put into the preheated oven.
4. Bake for 30 minutes until done. If your oven has a grill setting, switch it on for the last 8 minutes.
5. Serve hot with fresh, washed rocket and a little finely sliced spring onion.

## Tips

- It is worth using a vegan cheese that melts.
- You can add more flavour with fresh oregano and thyme.
- Several types of cherry tomatoes can be used.
