## Method

1. Blend the coconut water and bananas until smooth.
2. Add the frozen berries, cinnamon and nutmeg and blend again until thick and even.
3. Pour into glasses and serve.

## Tips

- Plain water works in place of coconut water.
- Add an extra banana for a sweeter, thicker smoothie.
