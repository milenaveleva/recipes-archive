## Method

1. **Preparation and frying the aubergine:** Wash the aubergines, cut them into cubes of about 1.5–2 cm, salt them lightly and leave them for 10 minutes on a clean kitchen towel or in a colander so they release their excess moisture.
2. Heat 3–4 tablespoons of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the aubergine cubes and fry until golden brown, crisp outside and soft inside, in about 8–10 minutes, then lift them out onto a plate.
3. **Making the sauce and pasta:** To the oil left in the pan add the very finely chopped or crushed garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes, and fry over low heat for 1 minute until fragrant (take care that it does not burn).
4. Pour in the canned chopped tomatoes, season with salt and pepper to taste, then simmer gently over low heat for about 12–15 minutes until the tomato sauce has thickened nicely.
5. Meanwhile, bring plenty of lightly salted water to the boil in a large pot and cook the penne according to the packet time to al dente (firm, about 10–11 minutes). Before draining, set aside 1 ladle of cooking water.
6. **Assembly and serving:** Stir two thirds of the fried aubergine cubes, the drained hot penne and half of the fresh basil leaves into the thickened tomato sauce. If needed, loosen it with the reserved pasta water.
7. Serve warm: sprinkle with the remaining crisp aubergine cubes, the fresh basil leaves and plenty of grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- The starch in the pasta water helps to emulsify the tomato sauce perfectly and make it silky, so it clings to the pasta.
- In the traditional Sicilian style you can also serve it with salted ricotta (ricotta salata), which gives the dish an even more authentic flavour.
