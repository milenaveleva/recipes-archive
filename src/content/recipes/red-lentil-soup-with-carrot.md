## Method

1. Peel the red onion, garlic, potatoes and carrots. Chop the onion finely, crush the garlic, and cut the potatoes and carrots into small cubes.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3-4 minutes.
3. Stir in the crushed garlic and fry for another 30-60 seconds.
4. Add the diced potatoes, carrots, bay leaves, red lentils and 750 ml water to the pan. Bring to the boil over high heat, then reduce the heat and cook uncovered for 15-20 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
5. Meanwhile, finely chop the fresh parsley.
6. When the cooking time is up, remove the bay leaves. Ladle a third of the soup into a small bowl and purée the rest smooth in the pan with a hand blender.
7. Pour the reserved chunky part back into the puréed soup and stir in the chopped parsley. Season with salt and pepper and serve, divided into 3 portions.

## Tips

- When serving, a little lemon juice or a spoonful of sour cream makes the soup even creamier and tangier.
