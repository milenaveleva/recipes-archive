## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting the eggplant:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Wash the eggplants, cut off the ends, then slice them lengthwise into slices about half a centimetre thick.
2. Place the eggplant slices on a baking tray lined with baking paper, brush them thinly with olive oil (about 15 ml), then sprinkle with a little salt and pepper. Bake in the oven for 15 minutes until soft and easy to roll, then let cool slightly.
3. **The sauce and ricotta filling:** In a small bowl, mix the ricotta with half of the grated Parmesan (20 g), one crushed garlic clove, half of the finely chopped fresh basil, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
4. Pour the canned chopped tomatoes into the bottom of a baking dish, stir in the remaining crushed garlic, the dried oregano, 15 ml olive oil, a pinch of salt and pepper, and spread evenly in the dish.
5. **Filling, rolling and baking:** Spoon a tablespoon of the ricotta filling onto the wider end of each baked eggplant slice, then roll them up tightly and place the rolls on top of the tomato sauce, seam side down.
6. Sprinkle the tops of the rolls with the remaining Parmesan and torn mozzarella.
7. Put the baking dish into the 190 °C oven and bake for 20-25 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and the cheese has melted and turned golden brown on top.
8. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving, then sprinkle with the remaining fresh basil leaves and serve warm, on its own or with crusty bread.

## Tips

- For an even creamier texture, mix a teaspoon of grated lemon zest into the ricotta, which gives the filling a fresh, summery citrus aroma.
