## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190 °C and prepare a baking tray. Cut the top off the head of garlic so that every clove is exposed. Peel the butternut squash, remove the seeds and cut it into small pieces.
2. Put the squash on the baking tray, add the head of garlic, season with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil. Bake in the preheated oven for about 25 minutes, or until the squash and garlic are soft.
3. Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins and put everything from the tray into a blender. Add the thyme leaves, the plant-based cooking cream and the broth and blend into a smooth, thick sauce. If the sauce is too thick, add a little more broth (or water).
4. Pour the raw gnocchi into a baking dish, add the squash sauce, mix well and smooth the surface.
5. Sprinkle with the grated mozzarella and the finely grated Parmesan, then bake in the hot oven for about 25 minutes, until the gnocchi are cooked through and the top is golden brown.
