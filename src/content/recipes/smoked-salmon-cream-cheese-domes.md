## Method

1. Finely chop 50 g of the smoked salmon, then mix it in a bowl with the cream cheese, ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice and chopped dill.
2. Line small bowls with cling film so that the edges overhang. Then line the inside of the bowls with the remaining salmon slices so they completely cover the bottom and sides, with the slices also overhanging the rim.
3. Fill the prepared bowls with the cheese and salmon cream. Fold the overhanging salmon slices over the filling, then fold the edges of the film tightly over it.
4. Refrigerate the bowls for at least 30 minutes so the flavours come together and the mousse sets.
5. Before serving, carefully turn out of the bowls, remove the film and garnish with fresh dill. Serve with toast.

## Tips

- Grate only the yellow zest of the lemon; the white part can be bitter.
- You will get the nicest shape with bowls about 8 cm in diameter and 5 cm high.
