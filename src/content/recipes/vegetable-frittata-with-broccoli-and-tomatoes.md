## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 185-190 °C. Prepare an ovenproof pan at least 27 cm in diameter and grease it with 1 tablespoon of olive oil.
2. Wash the cherry tomatoes and cut them in half. Wash the broccoli and break it into small florets. Finely grate the Edam. In a large bowl, beat the 8 eggs, add the oat milk, grated cheese, broccoli florets, halved tomatoes, salt and pepper, and mix thoroughly.
3. Pour the egg mixture into the prepared pan. Place it in the oven and bake for 40-45 minutes at 185-190 °C until the top is golden brown and the egg has set.
4. After baking, take the frittata out of the oven, cut it into 4 portions and serve fresh.
5. **Tip:** Sprinkle the frittata with finely chopped fresh chives when serving for an even fresher flavour.

## Tips

- To make it even more filling, add diced boiled potatoes or fried bacon.
