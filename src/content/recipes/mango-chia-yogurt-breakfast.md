## Method

1. In a bowl, mix the Greek yogurt, chia seeds and honey. Work it together thoroughly so the seeds are evenly distributed in the yogurt.
2. Peel the mango, remove the stone and cut the flesh into small cubes or slices. If you prefer a creamier texture, you can also blend the mango into a purée.
3. Divide the yogurt mixture among glasses, add the mango, decorate with fresh mint leaves and sprinkle with chopped hazelnuts. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or overnight) so the chia seeds soak up the liquid and soften.
4. Serve cold as a breakfast or a refreshing dessert.

## Tips

- Chia seeds need time to hydrate, so for the best result make it the evening before.
