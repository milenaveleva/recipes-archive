## Method

1. Put the lime zest and juice, soy sauce, sesame oil, peanut butter, ginger, garlic, a little water, the salt and the pepper in a blender. Close it tightly and blend until a smooth sauce.
2. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions, adding the broccoli florets for the last five minutes. When done, drain.
3. Put the pasta and broccoli in a large bowl, then add the carrots, red bell pepper, spring onions and chopped basil. Pour over the peanut ginger sauce and toss well so everything is coated.
