## Method

1. First make the sweet potato purée. Peel the sweet potatoes, clean them and cut them into cubes. In a pot, bring 1.5 litres of water to the boil, add the diced sweet potato and cook until soft, about 15-20 minutes. Then drain and blend smooth with a stick blender. This quantity gives about 600 g of purée.
2. Cook the pasta in lightly salted boiling water according to the packet instructions, then drain. The cooking time is usually 5-10 minutes, depending on the type of pasta.
3. Put the sweet potato purée into a saucepan, add the rice-based cooking cream, stir together and cook over low heat for 5 minutes. Then add the grated cheddar and stir until the cheese has melted completely. Take it off the heat, season with salt and pepper, then fold in the cooked pasta.
4. Serve the mac 'n' cheese immediately, or, as in the traditional recipe, put it into an ovenproof dish and bake at 180 °C for 8-10 minutes until done.

## Tips

- The traditional recipe uses elbow macaroni, but feel free to experiment with other whole-wheat or gluten-free versions.
