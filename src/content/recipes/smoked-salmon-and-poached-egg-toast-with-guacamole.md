## Method

1. Toast the rye bread slices in a toaster or pan until crisp.
2. Place the toasted rye bread on a plate.
3. Spread generously with guacamole.
4. Place 2 slices of smoked salmon on each avocado toast.
5. To make the eggs, bring a pot of water to the boil, then add a tablespoon of white vinegar. Then reduce the heat.
6. Crack the eggs into separate small bowls. Slowly pour each egg into the pot, one at a time.
7. Cook the eggs for about 3-4 minutes, or until the white is set but the yolk is still runny.
8. With a slotted spoon, carefully lift the poached eggs out of the water and place them on top of the salmon.
9. Garnish with fresh parsley and season with salt and pepper to taste.
10. Serve immediately and enjoy the delicious salmon and egg toast with guacamole!
