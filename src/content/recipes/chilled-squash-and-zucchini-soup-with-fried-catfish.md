## Method

1. Peel the squash, halve the zucchini lengthwise and scrape out the seeds, then do the same with the squash. Cut both into small cubes and salt lightly.
2. Cut the onion into small dice and sweat it with the crushed garlic in a little oil. Add three quarters of the zucchini and squash and cook them together.
3. Pour in the broth, season with salt and pepper, then blend and chill immediately so the soup keeps its nice colour. Season it properly at the end, when it is cold.
4. Cut the catfish into cubes, season with salt and pepper, toss in a little cornmeal and fry in plenty of oil until golden.
5. Season the soup with a little vinegar and salt and serve cold with the freshly fried catfish, fresh herbs, the remaining salted raw squash and zucchini cubes, and the Greek yogurt.
6. You can use another fish instead of catfish, and the soup is also perfect as a vegetarian summer refresher. You can use pattypan squash or yellow and green zucchini too; it is worth playing with colours.
