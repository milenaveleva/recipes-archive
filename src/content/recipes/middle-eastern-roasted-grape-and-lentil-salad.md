## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F). Wash the grapes, drain them and toss with 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil, a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper. Spread on a baking tray lined with baking paper and roast for 15–20 minutes until the grapes are soft, burst and beautifully caramelised.
2. Rinse the lentils thoroughly, then put them in a pot with three times their volume of lightly salted water. Bring to a boil, then simmer on low heat for 20–25 minutes until tender but still pleasantly firm (al dente). Drain and let cool to room temperature.
3. Meanwhile, sauté the thinly sliced red onion in a pan with 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat for 8–10 minutes until golden and sweet. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes until fragrant, then chop them roughly.
4. **Assembly and serving:** Make the dressing: in a small bowl, mix the remaining 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil, the freshly squeezed lemon juice, ground cumin, salt and black pepper.
5. In a large salad bowl, combine the cooked lentils, caramelised red onion, washed fresh baby spinach, and the finely chopped parsley and mint. Pour over the spiced lemon-olive oil dressing and fold gently.
6. Pile the hot oven-caramelised grapes on top of the salad, crumble the creamy feta over it and sprinkle with the crisp-toasted walnuts. Serve lukewarm.

## Tips

- For the grapes, choose seedless red or black grapes, as the darker skin gives deeper, caramel flavours when roasted.
- Don't overcook the lentils: brown or mountain lentils are best when they keep their shape and texture in the salad.
