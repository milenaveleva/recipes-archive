## Method

1. Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente, following the packet instructions. Meanwhile, peel the shallots and garlic. Cut the shallots into half-rings and the garlic into thin slices.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and fry the shallots until golden. Add the garlic and fry it briefly too, taking care that it does not burn. Pour the oil off the sun-dried tomatoes, add the tomatoes to the pan and fry everything together briefly.
3. In another, dry frying pan, toast the pine nuts until light brown, then set aside. Briefly scald the rocket with hot water; it loses volume and its flavour becomes milder.
4. As soon as the pasta is ready, drain it and mix it into the tomato base. Add the prepared rocket, then season with salt and pepper to taste. Toss everything thoroughly.
5. To serve, sprinkle with grated Parmesan and the toasted pine nuts.

## Tips

- For an even more intense flavour, use a little of the seasoned oil from the sun-dried tomatoes to fry the shallots.
