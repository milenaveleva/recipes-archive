## Method

1. **Preparation:** Cut the tofu into small cubes of about 1.5 cm and set aside. Wash the fresh tomatoes and dice them. Peel the garlic and fresh ginger and chop finely. Slice the spring onions into rings, keeping the white and green parts separate. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and beat lightly with a fork.
2. **Cooking:** In a pot, heat a little vegetable oil over medium heat. Add the chopped ginger, garlic and the white part of the spring onion and fry for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the diced tomatoes and cook for 3-4 minutes until the tomatoes release their juice and break down slightly.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth and soy sauce. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a simmer. Carefully slide the diced tofu into the soup, season with salt and pepper to taste, and cook over low heat for 5 minutes.
4. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of cold water until lump-free, then pour it slowly into the simmering soup, stirring continuously. Let it thicken for 1 minute to get a pleasantly silky texture.
5. **Finishing and serving:** Stir the soup in a circular motion to create a gentle whirlpool, then drizzle in the beaten egg in a thin stream. Leave it untouched for a few seconds so lovely egg ribbons form, then gently pull through with a wooden spoon. Turn off the heat, drizzle with the fragrant sesame oil, sprinkle with the reserved green spring onion rings, and serve hot.

## Tips

- The secret of perfect egg ribbons: always add the starch to the soup before the egg, because the slightly thickened broth stops the egg strands from sinking and helps create a fluffy texture.
- If you like it spicier, add a few drops of chilli oil to your bowl or sprinkle with fresh cilantro when serving.
