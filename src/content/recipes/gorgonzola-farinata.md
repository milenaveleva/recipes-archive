## Method

1. **Making the batter:** In a deep bowl, whisk the 200 g chickpea flour with the 600 ml water, 1 tsp salt and 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil until lump-free.
2. Let the thin batter rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes, then gently skim off the foam that has formed on the surface with a spoon.
3. **Baking and serving:** Preheat the oven to the highest possible temperature (230-250 °C). Grease a large baking tray or a heavy cast-iron pan with the remaining extra virgin olive oil and put it in the oven for 5 minutes to heat up.
4. Carefully pour the chickpea mixture into the preheated pan (about 5-8 mm thick) and bake for 15 minutes until golden with crisp edges.
5. Take it out of the oven, crumble the 150 g gorgonzola over the hot flatbread, sprinkle with 2 sprigs of chopped fresh rosemary and 1 pinch of ground black pepper, then return to the oven for 3-4 minutes until the cheese melts softly on top. Serve hot, sliced.

## Tips

- The secret of a perfectly crisp crust is a hot pan and high temperature. If you have a pizza stone in the oven, you can get an even more authentic result on it!
- Instead of gorgonzola you can try another strong blue cheese or creamy taleggio.
