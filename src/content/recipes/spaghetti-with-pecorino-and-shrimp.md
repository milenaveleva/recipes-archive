## Method

1. Cook the pasta until firm to the bite (al dente) in at least 3 litres of boiling water seasoned with the salt. Drain, but set aside 80 ml of the cooking water.
2. Peel the shrimp and set the heads aside; cut the shrimp meat into 5 pieces.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat, squeeze the contents of the shrimp heads into it, then put the heads themselves in the pan as well. Fry for 1 minute. Add the cut shrimp meat and fry for another minute, stirring frequently. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt and most of the pepper, then pour in 40 ml of the reserved cooking water. Stir, then remove and discard the shrimp heads.
4. Add the cooked pasta to the pan together with the remaining cooking water, the olive oil and the grated pecorino. Stir until the sauce turns creamy and glossy. Sprinkle over the remaining pepper and the finely chopped chives and toss together. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- The 200 g of whole shrimp is the weight with heads and shells; the edible meat is roughly half of that, so nutrition is counted on about 100 g. Only a small part of the salt in the pasta water is absorbed. The serving count of 1 comes from the source.
