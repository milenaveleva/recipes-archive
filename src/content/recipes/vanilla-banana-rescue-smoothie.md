## Method

1. This extra sweet vanilla-cinnamon smoothie is the great saviour of overripe bananas. There is no need for extra sugar or other sweetener, because the banana is sweet enough on its own. Drink immediately!
2. Put the 2 bananas in a blender, pour in the 400 ml oat milk, a pinch of cinnamon and a little vanilla extract. Blend thoroughly.
3. If it is frothy enough, top it with a little cinnamon and almonds as decoration; if you do not find it frothy enough, you can make some frothed oat milk to go with it for the full experience.
4. Rescuing leftovers does not have to be overthought. If we do not throw the bananas in the bin but make very good use of them, we have done just as well as if we had made a whole lunch from leftovers! It all starts with small steps! Go for it!
