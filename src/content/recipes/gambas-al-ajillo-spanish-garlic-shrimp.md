## Method

1. If using raw shrimp, peel them (you can leave the tail on for easier serving) and remove the vein. If using pre-cooked shrimp, simply rinse and pat dry.
2. Peel the garlic and slice it thinly. Cut the chilli into thin rings (if you prefer it less spicy, remove the seeds or use less).
3. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the garlic slices and chilli and fry until the garlic just begins to turn golden. Watch it constantly, as garlic burns easily and turns bitter!
4. As soon as the garlic is golden, add the shrimp and season with salt and pepper. If using raw shrimp, cook about 2–3 minutes per side until pink and firm. If using pre-cooked shrimp, just heat them for 1 minute on each side in the seasoned oil.
5. Serve immediately, sprinkled generously with freshly chopped parsley. Offer fresh whole-wheat baguette alongside for dipping in the delicious oil!
