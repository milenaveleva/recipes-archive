## Method

1. **Prep and vegetables:** Wash the leeks thoroughly between the layers, then cut them into thin rounds. Peel the fresh garlic cloves and crush or chop them finely. Chop the parsley finely.
2. Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta and add a pinch of salt.
3. **Caramelise and make the sauce:** In a deep frying pan over low to medium heat, warm together the 30 ml of olive oil and half of the extra olive oil (15 g). Add the sliced leeks, a pinch of salt and black pepper, then sweat and caramelise slowly under a lid for 12–14 minutes, until sweet, soft and buttery.
4. Add the chopped garlic and fry for another 1 minute, until fragrant. Then pour in the vegetable broth with a squeeze of lemon and simmer for 2 minutes, so that the flavours come together.
5. **Cook the pasta and assemble:** Meanwhile, cook the tagliatelle in the rolling salted water until al dente (about 1–2 minutes less than the packet time). Before draining, scoop out 150–200 ml of the starchy cooking water.
6. Toss the drained tagliatelle directly into the leek base in the pan. Add the remaining extra olive oil (15 g), the finely grated Parmesan and a little cooking water. Mix and toss vigorously for 1–2 minutes, so that a silky, creamy emulsion coats the strands.
7. Take off the heat and sprinkle with the chopped fresh parsley and freshly ground black pepper. Serve immediately in warm deep plates, topped with a little extra grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- The key to the silky texture is the starchy pasta water, olive oil and Parmesan quickly mixed together in the hot pan, which creates an authentic Italian sauce even without cream.
- A splash of vegetable broth with a few drops of fresh lemon juice gives the sauce a pleasant acidity.
