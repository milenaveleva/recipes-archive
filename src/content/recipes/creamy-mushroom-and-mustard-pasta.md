## Method

1. Clean and slice the mushrooms.
2. In a pan over high heat, sear the mushrooms on both sides in a little olive oil for a few minutes.
3. Make the sauce: put the plant-based cream, mustard and a quarter of the fried mushrooms in a blender and blend until creamy. Add salt and pepper to taste.
4. Cook the pasta, then serve it with the creamy sauce and the fried mushrooms, garnished with fresh parsley leaves.
