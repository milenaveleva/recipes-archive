## Method

1. **Making the jasmine rice and shrimp mixture:** Rinse the jasmine rice in cold water, then steam it in twice its volume of lightly salted water until tender, about 12–15 minutes. Take off the heat and keep warm, covered. Wash the shrimp thoroughly and dry them with paper towels. Finely blitz two thirds of the shrimp in a food processor, and chop the remaining third by hand into coarse small pieces so the finished cakes stay juicy and have some bite. In a mixing bowl, combine the minced and chopped shrimp, the crushed garlic, the finely chopped fresh cilantro, the soy sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch and the lightly beaten egg. Mix and work together thoroughly until you have a sticky, shapeable mixture.
2. **Breading and frying until crisp:** Pour the panko into a flat dish. With wet hands, shape the shrimp mixture into 6–8 flat patties, then roll them thoroughly in the panko so the crumbs stick evenly everywhere. In a deeper frying pan, heat the sunflower oil to medium-high (about 170–180 °C). Add the shaped shrimp cakes and fry for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown and crisp. Lift out onto paper towels to drain the excess oil. To serve, spoon steaming jasmine rice onto plates, place the crispy hot shrimp cakes beside it and serve with sweet chilli sauce, thin cucumber slices and lime wedges.

## Tips

- The secret of the genuinely crispy Thai texture is panko, which does not absorb the oil, so the coating stays extra crisp while the shrimp stays juicy and soft inside.
- For an even more exciting presentation, squeeze a few drops of lime juice directly onto the hot shrimp cakes just before eating.
