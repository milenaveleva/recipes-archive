## Method

1. **Making and chilling the polenta:** Bring the vegetable broth to the boil in a saucepan with a pinch of salt. Pour in the cornmeal in a thin stream, stirring constantly, then cook over moderate heat, stirring continuously, for 10-12 minutes until thick.
2. Pour the hot polenta into a small baking tray or dish lined with baking paper to a thickness of about 2 cm, smooth the top and leave to cool completely and set (about 30 minutes). Once firm, cut into bite-sized cubes.
3. **Frying and baking:** Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the polenta cubes and fry, turning, until all sides are golden brown and crisp, about 6-8 minutes, then lift onto a plate.
4. Wash, core and cut the pears into wedges. In the same pan fry the pear slices in 1 tablespoon of olive oil for 2-3 minutes per side until nicely caramelised but not too soft.
5. Lightly toast the walnuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2 minutes, then chop coarsely.
6. **Dressing and assembly:** Make the dressing: in a small bowl mix the remaining 1 tablespoon of extra virgin olive oil, the balsamic vinegar, the honey, a pinch of salt and the freshly ground black pepper.
7. In a large bowl toss the washed arugula with half of the dressing. Divide among salad plates, pile on the crisp warm polenta cubes and the caramelised pear. Crumble the creamy gorgonzola over the top, sprinkle with the fragrant toasted walnuts and drizzle with the remaining dressing. Serve immediately!

## Tips

- The polenta can be cooked the night before and left to set in the fridge, so the salad can be assembled in 15 minutes for lunch or dinner.
- Instead of gorgonzola, milder camembert or goat cheese also goes very well with the sweet pear and crunchy walnuts.
