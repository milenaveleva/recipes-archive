## Method

1. **Preparation and vegetable base:** Chop the onions and garlic finely. Cut the carrots and parsley root into rounds, and the celery root and peeled potatoes into even cubes.
2. Heat the sunflower oil in a larger pot, add the onions and sweat over medium heat until translucent. Add the crushed garlic and fry for 1 more minute.
3. Take the pot off the heat, sprinkle in the sweet paprika, stir quickly, then immediately pour in 1-2 dl of water so the paprika does not burn and turn bitter.
4. **Cooking:** Return the pot to the heat and add the prepared carrots, parsley root and celery root. Salt to taste, then pour in about 1.2 litres of water or vegetable stock.
5. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook over low heat for about 15 minutes. Add the potato cubes and cook for another 10-12 minutes until the vegetables are almost completely soft.
6. **Csipetke and finishing:** While the goulash cooks, make the csipetke: work the flour together with the egg and a pinch of salt into a firm, elastic dough. With floured hands, pinch off tiny, bean-sized pieces.
7. Drop the finished csipetke into the simmering vegetable goulash and cook for another 3-4 minutes, until the pieces of dough float to the top and are soft.
8. Take off the heat, sprinkle with the finely chopped fresh parsley leaves and serve hot with crusty bread and plant-based sour cream, if you like.

## Tips

- The vegetable goulash is even tastier if you use homemade vegetable stock instead of water.
- If you like spicy flavours, slice a fresh hot pepper into it when serving.
