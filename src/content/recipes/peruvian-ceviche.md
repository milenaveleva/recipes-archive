## Method

1. Fillet and bone the sea bream and remove the skin. Cut it into roughly 1 x 1 cm cubes and salt it.
2. Squeeze the citrus juices and mix them together in a large bowl.
3. Chop the coriander, slice the onion thinly, and deseed and slice the chilli.
4. Add the fish, onion, chilli and coriander to the citrus juice and mix well.
5. Let the marinade "work" for 5-10 minutes, then it is ready to eat. Serve straight away, well chilled.

## Tips

- In this recipe the citrus juice "cooks" the fish, as the acid denatures the proteins. Depending on how raw you like your fish, you can play with the acidity of the marinade and how long you leave the fish in it.
- This dish is at its best cold and spicy. You can control the heat of the chilli by leaving in more or fewer seeds.
