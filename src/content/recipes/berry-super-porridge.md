## Method

1. You can make the super porridge the evening before: put the oats, chia seeds, desiccated coconut and nuts in a small jar.
2. Pour in and stir enough water or plant-based milk to get a thinner, creamy consistency.
3. Put it in the fridge overnight.
4. Serve with fresh berries; you can also layer some mashed fruit underneath.
