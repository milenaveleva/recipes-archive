## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the squash:** Peel the squash, remove the seeds and cut the flesh into small cubes of about 1 cm. Crush the garlic cloves finely. In a deep frying pan, heat the 30 ml of extra virgin olive oil and 1 tablespoon of the additional olive oil. Add the squash cubes and the crushed garlic, season with salt and pepper, then fry over medium heat for 3-4 minutes.
2. Pour in about 1 dl of water (or later some of the pasta cooking water), cover and cook over low heat for 10-12 minutes until the squash is completely soft. When it is soft, add the plant-based cooking cream and work it with a stick blender or potato masher into a creamy, silky sauce.
3. **Crisping the sage, cooking the pasta and serving:** Meanwhile, bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, salt it and cook the spaghetti al dente (1-2 minutes less than the packet time). Before draining, set aside 1.5 dl of starchy cooking water.
4. In a smaller frying pan, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Throw in the fresh sage leaves and fry them over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until crisp and lightly hazelnut brown. Take the crisp leaves out onto a plate and pour the remaining fragrant sage oil into the squash sauce.
5. Toss the drained hot spaghetti straight into the squash sauce. Sprinkle with the finely grated Parmesan and gradually add as much of the reserved pasta cooking water as needed to make the sauce glossy, silky and creamy. Serve immediately in deep plates, topped with the crisp sage leaves and extra Parmesan.

## Tips

- Cut the squash into small 1 cm cubes, so that under a lid in a frying pan it becomes butter-soft in just 10-12 minutes, with no need for lengthy roasting in the oven.
- The starchy pasta cooking water is key: it gives the authentic Italian creamy emulsion without making the sauce too heavy.
