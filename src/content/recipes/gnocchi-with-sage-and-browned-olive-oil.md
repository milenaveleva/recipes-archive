## Method

1. Bring a large pot of water to the boil, salt it generously and set it over the heat. Wash the sage leaves; cut the larger ones into smaller pieces and leave the smaller ones whole.
2. When the water is nearly boiling, heat a frying pan. Add the olive oil together with the sage and warm for a few minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sage turns crisp and the oil becomes fragrant. Then add the crushed garlic, salt and pepper and fry for one more minute.
3. As soon as the water boils, add the gnocchi. When they float to the surface, lift them out immediately and place them straight into the pan with the oil sauce. Sprinkle over half of the finely grated Parmesan and toss gently. The sauce should be glossy and silky; if it is too thick, add a little of the gnocchi cooking water.
4. Serve immediately, sprinkled with the remaining Parmesan and garnished with the crisp sage leaves.

## Tips

- Do not leave the sage in the hot oil unattended, as it burns easily. Once the sage is crisp and the oil smells fragrant, it is done!
