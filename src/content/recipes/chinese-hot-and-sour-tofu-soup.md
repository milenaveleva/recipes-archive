## Method

1. **Preparation:** Cut the tofu into thin, bite-sized strips. Cut the shiitake mushrooms and the peeled carrot into fine matchsticks (julienne). Grate the fresh ginger and slice the spring onions into rings (keep the white parts for the soup and the green parts for serving).
2. Beat the eggs loosely with a fork in a small bowl. Mix the cornstarch with 3 tablespoons of cold water in a separate glass until smooth.
3. **Cooking and thickening:** In a medium saucepan, pour in the vegetable broth, then add the soy sauce, grated ginger, carrot strips and sliced shiitake mushrooms. Bring to the boil, then cook over moderate heat for 5 minutes until the vegetables soften slightly.
4. Add the prepared tofu strips and the white parts of the spring onions to the soup. Stir the cornstarch mixture again and trickle it into the boiling soup while stirring constantly until it thickens slightly and becomes silky.
5. **Seasoning and serving:** Turn the heat to the lowest setting. Slowly stir the soup in a circle with a wooden spoon to create a whirl and drizzle in the beaten egg in a thin stream, so that beautiful, delicate egg ribbons form in the soup.
6. Turn off the heat. Season the soup with the rice vinegar, ground white pepper and the toasted sesame oil. Taste and adjust the sourness or heat as needed.
7. Ladle the hot soup into deep bowls and sprinkle the reserved fresh green spring onion rings on top. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- If you want an even spicier flavour, drip a little chilli oil on top of the soup when serving.
- White pepper gives the soup its authentic, characteristic heat; add it to taste.
