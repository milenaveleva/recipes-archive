## Method

1. Beat the egg yolks with the sugar until pale, then mix in the mascarpone thoroughly.
2. Beat the egg whites to stiff peaks and gently fold into the mascarpone cream.
3. Mix the cooled coffee with the amaretto in a bowl and quickly dip the ladyfingers into it. Arrange half of the ladyfingers in the bottom of a glass baking dish, then spread half of the mascarpone cream over them. Repeat the layering with the remaining ladyfingers and cream. The top layer should be cream. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours (ideally 12 hours).
4. Dust thinly with cocoa powder before serving.

## Tips

- For best results, use fresh farm eggs and quality Italian mascarpone.
