## Method

1. Put all the ingredients in a blender and blend until completely smooth and creamy. If it seems too thick, add a little more almond milk; if too thin, add another tablespoon of oats.
2. Pour the mixture into bowls or jars and refrigerate overnight (or at least a few hours). Before serving, top with fresh fruit, shredded coconut, chia seeds or anything you like.

## Tips

- For an even creamier result, use ripe, soft mango for blending.
