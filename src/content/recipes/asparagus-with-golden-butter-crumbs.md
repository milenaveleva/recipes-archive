## Method

1. Carefully peel the asparagus and remove the woody ends. Tie the asparagus into a bundle.
2. In a tall pot, bring water to the boil with the icing sugar and salt, then place the asparagus in the boiling water. After 10 minutes of cooking, take the bundle apart and arrange the asparagus on a plate.
3. Melt the butter in a frying pan. Toast the breadcrumbs until golden brown, then pour them over the asparagus. Or, if it is more convenient, toss the asparagus in the breadcrumbs and then pour the melted butter over it.

## Tips

- Peel the asparagus with a potato or asparagus peeler, starting just below the tip. You do not need a knife to remove the woody part: if you bend the spear, it snaps at the woody end. It is worth saving the broken-off ends, as they make a very tasty addition to soup.
- When cooking, it is best to keep the asparagus tips out of the water: the water should not fully cover the spears, and the tips should only go under for the very last minute so they soften too.
- Breadcrumbs brown very quickly and suddenly, so take care they do not burn. Toast them over medium heat while stirring constantly; if the pan is too hot they burn fast and turn bitter.
