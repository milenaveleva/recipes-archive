## Method

1. Drain the soaked beans, cover with fresh water in a pot and simmer for 45–75 minutes, until very tender.
2. Keep 4 tbsp of the cooking water, then drain the beans and let them cool.
3. Blend the beans, reserved cooking water, avocado, cilantro, jalapeño, lime juice, garlic, cumin and coriander until completely smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Spoon the dip into a bowl, scatter over a little cilantro and serve with the vegetables arranged around it.

## Tips

- Short on time? Use 1½ cups canned black beans, rinsed, and a few spoonfuls of the liquid from the can.
