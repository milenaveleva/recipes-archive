## Method

1. Meanwhile, cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Drain, return it to the pot and set aside.
2. Steam the cauliflower in a pot of water for 8-15 minutes until tender, then drain well.
3. In a small pan, heat the oil, add the finely chopped onion and garlic and cook over low heat for 4-5 minutes until soft and fragrant. Take care not to burn them.
4. Put the softened cauliflower, the sautéed onion and garlic, the milk, nutritional yeast, lemon juice, salt and pepper into a high-powered blender. Blend until you get a very smooth, creamy sauce. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
5. Pour the cauliflower sauce into the pot with the pasta and mix thoroughly. Heat over low-medium heat until everything is hot. Because the pasta mellows the flavour of the sauce, taste it and add more salt, pepper, lemon or other seasoning if needed.
6. To serve, divide the warm pasta among plates and sprinkle with a little more salt and pepper. Squeeze over a little lemon juice and garnish with freshly chopped parsley. If you like, add your favourite cooked vegetables, such as broccoli, peas, leeks, asparagus or pumpkin.
