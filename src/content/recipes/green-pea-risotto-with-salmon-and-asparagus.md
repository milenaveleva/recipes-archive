## Method

1. Cut the salmon fillets into 4 equal pieces and soak in a mixture of 2 litres of water and 120 g salt for 1.5 hours.
2. Sauté the finely chopped onion and crushed garlic in a little oil, then add the risotto rice and toast it, then pour in the white wine.
3. Meanwhile heat the broth and add it to the risotto a ladle at a time while simmering, adding only as much as the rice absorbs.
4. Meanwhile take the salmon out of the brine and gently pan-fry it in a little mixture of olive oil and butter, taking care to keep it juicy inside.
5. When the risotto is almost completely tender, take it off the heat and stir in cold butter, grated Parmesan and a little grated lemon zest, season with salt and pepper and add a few drops of lemon juice.
6. Sauté the asparagus in a little butter for 2-3 minutes, season with salt and cut into bite-sized pieces.
7. When the risotto is ready, stir in the pea purée and serve with the salmon, pea shoots, fresh dill and asparagus.

## Tips

- You can also make a purée from the asparagus and add fresh green peas.
- Can be made with other vegetables, such as zucchini, broccoli or green beans.
