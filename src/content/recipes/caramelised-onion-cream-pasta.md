## Method

1. **Preparation and caramelising:** Peel the onions and cut them into thin slices. Crush the garlic cloves or chop them finely.
2. In a large frying pan over low-medium heat, warm the two olive oils together. Add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Caramelise slowly, stirring occasionally, for 20-25 minutes until the onions are deep golden brown, silky and sweet.
3. Add the crushed garlic to the caramelised onions and fry for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
4. **Cooking and tossing:** Meanwhile, bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot and cook the pasta al dente (medium-firm) according to the packet instructions. Set aside about 150 ml of the pasta cooking water before draining.
5. Pour the plant-based cooking cream over the caramelised onions in the pan and heat together for 2-3 minutes. Season with freshly ground black pepper and a little salt as needed.
6. Stir the grated Parmesan and the drained pasta into the sauce. If the sauce is too thick, add 50-100 ml of the reserved pasta water to get a perfectly creamy coating.
7. Serve hot, sprinkled with freshly chopped parsley and a little more grated Parmesan.

## Tips

- Do not rush the caramelising of the onions over high heat, because they will burn instead of browning. Slow, patient cooking brings out their natural sugars and sweet flavour.
- Also try it with penne, rigatoni or tagliatelle, as their surface holds the thick, creamy sauce very well.
- The first oil line replaces the butter in the source (75 % of its weight).
