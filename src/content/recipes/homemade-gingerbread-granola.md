## Method

1. Mix the oats, walnuts and desiccated coconut together and stir in the dried fruit.
2. Warm the oil and honey together briefly so they become more fluid, then pour over the mixture.
3. Spread on a baking tray lined with baking paper and bake at 150 °C for about 40 minutes. Stir 2–3 times during baking so it browns evenly.
