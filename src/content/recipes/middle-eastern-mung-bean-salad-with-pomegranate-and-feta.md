## Method

1. **Preparation and dressing:** Rinse the mung beans thoroughly in cold water, then put them in a saucepan with three times their volume of lightly salted water. Bring to the boil, then simmer over low heat for about 25-30 minutes until tender, taking care that the beans do not fall apart. Drain and let cool completely.
2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until golden brown and fragrant, then chop roughly. Cut the red onion and long cucumber into fine cubes and halve or quarter the cherry tomatoes. Remove the fresh, crunchy seeds from the pomegranate. Finely chop the parsley and mint leaves.
3. In a small bowl, mix the freshly squeezed lemon juice, crushed garlic, ground cumin, fine salt and ground black pepper. Trickle in the extra virgin olive oil while stirring constantly until you get a nice, smooth vinaigrette.
4. **Assembly and serving:** In a large salad bowl, mix the cooled mung beans with the chopped red onion, cucumber, cherry tomatoes and two thirds of the finely chopped herbs.
5. Pour the lemon-cumin dressing over the salad and toss gently so that the spicy flavours permeate every ingredient evenly. Let it stand for 5-10 minutes.
6. Before serving, sprinkle the top with crumbled feta, toasted walnuts, fresh pomegranate seeds and the remaining herbs. Serve with soft pita warmed in a pan or the oven.

## Tips

- Mung beans do not need to be soaked in advance, but if soaked in cold water for a few hours, the cooking time drops to 15-20 minutes.
- The salad is excellent both cold and at room temperature; covered in the fridge it keeps fresh for 2 days, so it is also perfect for meal prep.
