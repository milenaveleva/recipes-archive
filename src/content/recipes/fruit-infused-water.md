## Method

1. In summer heat it is always a challenge to get children to drink enough water. If you want to be a little more mindful and avoid starting with syrups or fruit juices, try fruit-infused water. All you need to do is add some seasonal fruit to the water, and its aroma instantly makes the water more exciting.
2. Recommended from 10 months of age.
