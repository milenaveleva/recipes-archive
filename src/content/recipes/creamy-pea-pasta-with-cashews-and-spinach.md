## Method

1. First put the pasta on: bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot. Salt it, add the pasta and cook over medium heat for the time given on the packet (about 14–15 minutes) until al dente.
2. Meanwhile, peel and roughly chop the garlic. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan, add the garlic and fry over medium heat for 2–3 minutes. Then add the baby spinach, 50 g of the peas and about half a ladle of the pasta cooking water. Cook for 2–3 minutes, then take off the heat. Put the mixture in a blender, add the cashews, the oat milk, the nutritional yeast flakes, salt and pepper and blend until completely smooth.
3. When the pasta is cooked, drain it and return it to the pot. Add the remaining peas and the blended sauce. Mix thoroughly, divide into 4 portions and serve warm.

## Tips

- Before serving you can garnish with fresh herbs or a little extra nutritional yeast for an even more intense flavour.
