## Method

1. Bring plenty of water to a boil in a large pot for the pasta and salt it generously. Peel the kohlrabi and cut it into small cubes about 0.5 cm. Peel the garlic cloves and slice them thinly or chop finely.
2. When the cooking water is at a rolling boil, add the penne and cook according to the package instructions, usually 11-13 minutes for whole-wheat penne, until springy and al dente. Before draining, scoop out and set aside about a mugful of the starchy cooking water.
3. While the pasta cooks, heat the extra virgin olive oil in a deep, wide pan over medium heat. Add the diced kohlrabi and fry, stirring constantly, for 5-6 minutes until lightly golden brown at the edges but still pleasantly crunchy inside.
4. Add the sliced garlic and chilli flakes (adjusting the heat to taste) to the kohlrabi. Fry gently for 1 more minute, taking care that the garlic does not burn. Pour in the canned chopped tomatoes, season with salt and freshly ground black pepper, then simmer over medium heat and reduce for 6-8 minutes. If it becomes too thick, loosen it with a little pasta water.
5. Toss the drained al dente penne directly in the pan with the bubbling spicy sauce, together with the finely chopped fresh parsley and half of the grated Parmesan. Shake over high heat for 1 minute so the emulsion coats the pasta velvety smooth.
6. Serve immediately on warm plates, sprinkled with the remaining freshly grated Parmesan and drizzled with a few drops of good extra virgin olive oil.

## Tips

- Cut the kohlrabi into even, small cubes so it softens perfectly at the same time in the tomato sauce while keeping its pleasant, refreshing texture.
- Adjust the amount of chilli flakes to your family's taste: 1 teaspoon creates a pleasantly fiery, authentic Roman arrabbiata character.
