## Method

1. **Making the yeast dough:** In a large bowl, mix the lukewarm water with the honey and dry yeast, and leave for 5 minutes until it begins to foam lightly.
2. Sift in the flour, then add the fine salt and extra virgin olive oil. Knead by hand or machine for about 8-10 minutes into a smooth, elastic dough with a shiny surface.
3. Shape the dough into a ball, place it in a thinly oiled bowl, cover with a damp tea towel and let rise in a warm place for about 60 minutes until doubled in size.
4. **Preparing the cheese filling:** In a bowl, mix the ricotta with the freshly grated Parmesan, dried oregano and freshly ground black pepper.
5. Drain the mozzarella thoroughly, pat dry with paper towels, then cut into small cubes or tear into smaller pieces and fold into the seasoned ricotta mixture.
6. **Assembly and baking:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C (top and bottom heat). Divide the risen dough into two parts (a larger piece of about 60% and a smaller piece of about 40%).
7. Lightly grease a round (about 26-28 cm diameter) pie dish or round baking tin with olive oil. Roll out the larger piece of dough thinly and line the dish with it so that it has a rim.
8. Spread the rich cheese filling evenly over the dough base. Roll out the smaller piece of dough as well and cover the filling with it. Pinch and roll the edges together all around so the cheese can't leak out during baking.
9. Prick the top dough layer in several places with a fork so the steam can escape, then brush thinly with olive oil. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes until the crust is nicely golden brown and crisp. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving, then cut into wedges.

## Tips

- Be sure to dry the mozzarella thoroughly with paper towels before baking so that excess whey doesn't soak the bottom crust during baking.
- For an even more rustic flavour, you can mix a few chopped Italian black olives (Gaeta olives) or sun-dried tomatoes into the filling.
