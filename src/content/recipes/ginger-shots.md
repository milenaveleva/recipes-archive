## Method

1. First peel the lemons and the orange, then clean the ginger (it does not need to be fully peeled; just wash it thoroughly or scrape the skin off with a spoon).
2. Put the peeled lemons, orange and cleaned ginger into a blender and blend until completely smooth.
3. Strain the mixture through a fine sieve or a clean kitchen towel. Stir the honey into the strained liquid and pour into a well-sealing bottle.
4. Store the finished drink in the refrigerator, where it keeps for 3-4 days. Drink 20-30 ml (about 2 tablespoons) every morning.

## Tips

- If you like it extra spicy, leave a little of the pulp in.
