## Method

1. **Roasting the beetroot:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Peel the beetroot and cut it into even cubes of about 1.5 cm. Line a baking tray with baking paper, place the beetroot cubes on it, drizzle with half of the extra virgin olive oil (about 20 ml), add the crushed garlic, then sprinkle with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Toss thoroughly.
2. Roast the beetroot at 200 °C for 25-30 minutes, until the edges caramelise lightly and the inside is tender when pierced with a fork. Once roasted, take it out of the oven and let it cool to lukewarm.
3. **Cooking the orzo and toasting the nuts:** Bring plenty of lightly salted water to a boil in a large pot. Add the orzo and cook it perfectly al dente according to the package instructions (whole-wheat orzo usually takes 10-12 minutes).
4. Drain the pasta, let it drain thoroughly, then toss with 1 teaspoon of olive oil so the grains don't stick together. Let it cool to room temperature.
5. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan without fat over medium heat for 2-3 minutes, shaking constantly, until fragrant and golden brown. Pour them immediately into a small bowl so they don't burn in the hot pan.
6. **Assembly and serving:** In a large salad bowl, whisk the remaining extra virgin olive oil with the balsamic vinegar, a pinch of salt and pepper until smooth.
7. Add the cooled orzo, the roasted beetroot cubes and the fresh, washed rocket to the dressing. Fold together gently so the beetroot juice gives the orzo a lovely ruby colour without the rocket leaves collapsing.
8. Crumble the creamy feta over the salad, sprinkle with the fragrant toasted pine nuts, and serve lukewarm or as a fresh cold main-course salad.

## Tips

- Even quicker with pre-cooked beetroot: if you're in a hurry, you can use vacuum-packed pre-cooked beetroot. In that case the roasting time drops to 15 minutes, just enough for the edges to brown nicely.
- Great for the next day too: this grain salad keeps well in the fridge, so it's ideal for a work lunch or picnic. If making it ahead, add the rocket and toasted nuts only right before serving so they stay crisp.
