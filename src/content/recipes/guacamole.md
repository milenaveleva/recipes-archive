## Method

1. Cut around the avocados along the pit, then separate the halves with a twisting motion. Remove the pit and spoon the flesh out of the skin into a bowl.
2. Squeeze the lime juice over the avocado to keep its vibrant colour. Add the crushed garlic, then mash with a fork to a rustic or creamy consistency, as you like.
3. Halve the jalapeño, remove the seeds and chop it finely. Cut the shallot and the tomato (with its core and seeds removed) into small dice. Mix the vegetables into the avocado, and finally season with salt and freshly ground pepper.

## Tips

- If you don't like heat, you can replace the jalapeño with bell pepper.
- Guacamole is best when it rests in the fridge for 30 minutes before serving so the flavours blend.
