## Method

1. Cook the bulgur in plenty of salted water, drain and leave to cool. Finely chop all the other tabbouleh ingredients and mix everything together.
2. Fry the sliced cheese in the olive oil until golden and serve with the tabbouleh.

## Tips

- If you have a little mint at home, add it for extra freshness.
