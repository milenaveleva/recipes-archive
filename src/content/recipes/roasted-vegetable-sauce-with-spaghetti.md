## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Place the bell peppers, halved and deseeded, on a baking tray. Add the cherry tomatoes and the roughly chopped red onion.
3. Cut the top off the garlic head and place it among the vegetables.
4. Drizzle everything with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, then roast for 40 minutes, until all the vegetables are completely soft.
5. Meanwhile, bring water to the boil in a large pot, salt it generously and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
6. When the roasted vegetables have cooled a little, squeeze the soft garlic cloves out of their skins (use a paper towel if still too hot).
7. Blend the roasted vegetables, the garlic, the sun-dried tomatoes, the basil and the ricotta into a smooth sauce.
8. Toss with the cooked pasta; if it is too thick, add a little pasta cooking water.
9. Serve sprinkled with freshly grated Parmesan and chopped basil. Enjoy!
