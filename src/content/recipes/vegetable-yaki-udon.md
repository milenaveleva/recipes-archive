## Method

1. Heat the oil in a hot wok or pan, then fry the cleaned, prepared mushrooms until golden brown.
2. Meanwhile, cook the whole-wheat udon in boiling unsalted water according to the package instructions (usually 1-2 minutes longer than white udon), then drain and rinse with cold water. Tip: use plenty of water for cooking.
3. Add the finely chopped garlic and ginger to the mushrooms, drizzle in the honey and pour in the soy sauce. Then add the spring onions cut into larger pieces and fry together for one more minute.
4. Add the cooked noodles and the rest of the soy sauce (to taste) and toss thoroughly so the sauce coats everything. If it seems too dry, add a little vegetable broth.
5. Take off the heat, garnish with fresh spring onion and serve immediately.

## Tips

- If the noodles seem too dry while frying, add a little vegetable broth for a juicier consistency.
