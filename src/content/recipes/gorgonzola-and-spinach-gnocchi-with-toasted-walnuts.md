## Method

1. In a dry frying pan over medium heat, toast the roughly chopped walnuts for 2-3 minutes until fragrant and crisp, then put them in a small bowl to cool.
2. In a larger pot, bring plenty of salted water to the boil for the gnocchi. When the water is boiling vigorously, add the gnocchi and cook until they float to the surface (about 2-3 minutes). Drain, but set aside a little (about half a ladleful) of the cooking water for the sauce.
3. In a deeper frying pan, heat the olive oil, add the finely crushed garlic and fry for half a minute, taking care that it does not burn. Add the thoroughly washed, drained fresh spinach and just wilt it in 1-2 minutes.
4. Pour the plant-based cooking cream over the wilted spinach and crumble in the gorgonzola. Stir constantly over low heat until the cheese has melted completely and you have a silky, creamy sauce. Season with a pinch of ground nutmeg and freshly ground black pepper, and add fine salt if needed. If the sauce is too thick, loosen it with the reserved cooking water.
5. Pour the cooked gnocchi into the gorgonzola and spinach pan and gently toss with the hot sauce so that it coats each piece richly. Serve on warm plates and sprinkle generously with the toasted, crunchy walnuts. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- If the gorgonzola is on the saltier side, taste the sauce only after the cheese has melted, and add salt only if necessary.
- With Gorgonzola Dolce you get a distinctly soft, sweet-creamy sauce, while Gorgonzola Picante provides a spicier, more pronounced flavour.
