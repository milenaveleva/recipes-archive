## Method

1. Clean the mushrooms and cut them into thick slices (halve or quarter the smaller ones). Peel the shallot and chop it finely.
2. Heat one tablespoon of the oil in a frying pan and fry the mushrooms for about 3 minutes, until golden brown. Take them out and set aside. In the remaining oil, sweat the shallot until translucent, then pour in the broth and plant-based cooking cream. Add the nutritional yeast as well. Cook the sauce over low heat for at least 5 minutes, until it thickens.
3. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water according to the packet instructions (al dente).
4. Return the mushrooms to the sauce, season with pepper and, if needed, salt. Drain the cooked pasta and toss it into the sauce immediately. Serve garnished with fresh parsley.

## Tips

- Nutritional yeast not only thickens the sauce but also gives it a pleasantly cheesy, nutty flavour, making it an excellent vegan alternative to Parmesan.
