## Method

1. Put the onions in a large pot with a couple of tablespoons of water and cook over medium-high heat for 25 to 35 minutes, stirring often and adding a splash of water when they catch, until deep brown.
2. Add the garlic, thyme, coconut aminos, broth and bay leaf and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Stir the arrowroot into the 3 tbsp water, add it to the pot and simmer for 5 minutes more until slightly thickened. Season to taste and remove the bay leaf.
4. For the topping, drain the cashews and blend them with the arrowroot, onion powder, garlic powder, lemon juice, paprika, salt and ½ cup water until completely smooth.
5. Whisk the cashew mixture in a small saucepan over medium-high heat until thick, then take it off the heat.
6. Ladle the soup into ovenproof bowls, lay a slice of toast spread with a few spoonfuls of cashew cheese on each and broil for 2 to 3 minutes until browned. Serve at once.

## Tips

- Water can stand in for the broth.
- No ovenproof bowls? Broil the topped toasts on a tray and float them on the soup.
- The topping is optional; the soup is good on its own.
