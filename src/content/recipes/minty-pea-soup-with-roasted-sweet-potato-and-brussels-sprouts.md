## Method

1. Chop the red onion and start sweating it in a little olive oil. When it has taken some colour, fry the finely chopped garlic with it until fragrant.
2. Add the frozen peas and the peeled, diced potatoes. Pour in the broth, season with salt to taste and cook until soft.
3. While the soup is cooking, peel the sweet potato, cut it into small cubes and spread on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Peel off the outer 1–2 small leaves of the Brussels sprouts and halve them. Place them cut side down next to the sweet potato cubes. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and roast in an oven preheated to 180 °C for 15–20 minutes.
4. Blend the finished soup in a blender until creamy.
5. Serve topped with some of the roasted vegetables, a few mint leaves and a little chopped nuts.
