## Method

1. First prepare the garlic. Preheat the oven to 185-190 °C. Cut the top (about 1 cm) off the heads of garlic so the cloves are exposed. Drizzle 2 tablespoons of olive oil over them, salt them, then wrap them in aluminium foil. Place on a baking tray and roast in the preheated oven for 40-45 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, bring salted water to the boil in a large pot. Add the tagliatelle and cook for the time given on the packet (about 9-11 minutes) until al dente. When done, drain and rinse with cold water.
3. When the garlic is roasted, take it out of the oven. In a frying pan or saucepan, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Peel and finely chop the onion and sweat it in the oil for 4-5 minutes, until translucent.
4. Pour in the 150 ml of plant-based cooking cream and the 80 ml of oat milk. Add the finely grated Parmesan, season with salt and pepper, and cook over medium heat for 4-5 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly. Then take it off the heat.
5. Take the roasted garlic out of the foil and squeeze the creamy cloves straight into the cream base. Throw away the garlic skins.
6. Blend the sauce completely smooth with a stick blender. Finally stir in the finely chopped parsley and the cooked pasta. Toss thoroughly, then serve immediately.
