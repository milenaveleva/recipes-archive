## Method

1. For the rice and peas, soften the onion in the oil, season with salt, sprinkle in the rice and after 1-2 minutes of toasting add the peas.
2. Stir, pour in the broth and, once it boils, cook covered for 30-35 minutes (brown basmati needs about 2-2.25 times its volume of liquid; add a splash more if it dries out), then turn off the heat and leave the lid on for 10 minutes until serving.
3. Rub the salmon with the lemon juice, salt and pepper and cook it in the olive oil in a pan on the grill over medium heat for about 10 minutes until done.
4. For the sauce, fry the onion in olive oil, add the thyme, mustard and cream, season with salt and pepper and bring to a boil, then turn off the heat and stir in the fresh lemon juice. Then you are ready to serve.
5. If salmon is on offer on the site, it is worth buying several pieces and freezing them in portions, so you can save money.
