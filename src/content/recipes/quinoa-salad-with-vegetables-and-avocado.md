## Method

1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly with warm water. Salt 600 ml of water with a teaspoon of salt, bring to a boil, then pour in the quinoa. Bring to a boil again, reduce the heat to the minimum and cook for 15 minutes. Pour the cooked quinoa into a bowl and let it cool.
2. Chop the onion and garlic finely. Cut the tomatoes and cucumber into roughly 2 cm cubes. Halve the avocados, remove the stones, scoop out the flesh with a spoon and also cut it into 2 cm cubes.
3. In a bowl, mix the tomatoes, cucumber and onion. Add the olive oil, lemon juice and red wine vinegar and toss well. Finally add the avocado, garlic, fresh herbs and the cooled quinoa. Mix gently, then season with salt and pepper to taste.
