## Method

1. **Preparation and vegetables:** Take the tofu out of its packaging, wrap it in paper towel and press it under a light weight for 10 minutes to remove excess moisture. Then cut it into roughly 3 × 3 cm cubes.
2. Peel and finely grate the ginger. Slice the spring onions thinly and set the green parts aside for serving. Wash the pak choi and cut it into quarters lengthways.
3. **Making the broth and frying the tofu:** Bring the vegetable broth to the boil in a pot. Add the soy sauce, grated ginger and sesame oil. Add the pak choi, cook over low heat for 2-3 minutes until tender-crisp, then take off the heat.
4. Toss the tofu cubes thoroughly in the cornstarch so every side is evenly covered with a fine coating. Gently shake off the excess.
5. Heat the sunflower oil in a frying pan to medium-high. Fry the starch-coated tofu cubes for 4-5 minutes, turning on each side, until they go from snow-white to light golden brown and completely crisp. Lift them onto paper towel to drain off the excess oil.
6. **Serving:** Ladle the steaming aromatic ginger broth and the steamed pak choi into deep bowls. Place the freshly fried, hot, crispy Agedashi tofu cubes on top.
7. Sprinkle with the sliced fresh spring onions and toasted sesame seeds. Serve immediately so the outer layer of the tofu stays pleasantly crisp in the flavourful broth!

## Tips

- The secret of a real Agedashi is to put the tofu cubes into the hot broth right before serving, so the top of the starch coating stays crisp while the bottom soaks up the aromatic broth.
- If you like even more piquant flavours, add finely grated daikon or a pinch of shichimi togarashi on top of the tofu when serving.
