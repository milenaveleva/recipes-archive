## Method

1. Peel the orange and banana. Wash (or peel) the carrot thoroughly and cut it into smaller pieces together with the fruit.
2. Put all the prepared ingredients into a blender. Add the ground ginger and turmeric.
3. Blend everything until completely smooth and creamy. If it is too thick, thin it with a little water.

## Tips

- For an even more refreshing drink, add a few ice cubes before blending.
