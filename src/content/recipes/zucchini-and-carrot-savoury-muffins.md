## Method

1. Finely grate the zucchini and carrot and squeeze out the excess liquid.
2. In a bowl, mix the eggs, oil and salt, then fold in the grated vegetables.
3. Add the flour mixed with the baking powder and half of the cheese, and stir into a smooth batter.
4. Fill a muffin tin and sprinkle the tops with the remaining cheese.
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 °C for about 20 minutes.

## Tips

- For a school snack: in a sealed container in the fridge it keeps for up to 3 days.
