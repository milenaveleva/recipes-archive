## Method

1. In a bowl, mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, lime juice, finely chopped spring onion, grated ginger, crushed garlic and chopped chilli.
2. Place the salmon fillets in the marinade and coat them well. Leave to rest in the fridge for at least 30 minutes so the flavours penetrate the fish.
3. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Place the salmon on a baking tray lined with baking paper and bake for 15-18 minutes, until the fish is tender and flaky.
4. Meanwhile, cook the brown jasmine rice according to the package instructions. Serve the baked salmon with the rice and finish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.

## Tips

- Don't overbake the salmon, so it stays juicy. It is done when the flesh separates easily with a fork.
