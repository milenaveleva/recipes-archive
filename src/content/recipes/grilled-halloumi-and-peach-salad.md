## Method

1. **Preparation and grilling:** Wash the peaches, remove the stones and cut into slices or wedges about 1 cm thick. Slice the halloumi as well.
2. Heat a grill pan or grill and cook the cheese and peaches for about 2-3 minutes per side until they get nice grill marks.
3. **Assembly and serving:** In a small bowl, make the dressing: mix the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper.
4. Toss the rocket with the dressing, then arrange the grilled peaches and halloumi on top. Sprinkle with the mixed seeds and serve immediately.

## Tips

- For an even more intense flavour, briefly toast the seeds in a dry pan before serving.
- The recipe also works very well with nectarines instead of peaches.
