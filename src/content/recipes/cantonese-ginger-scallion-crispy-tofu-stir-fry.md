## Method

1. **Preparation:** Pat the tofu thoroughly dry with kitchen paper, then cut into 2-3 cm cubes. In a bowl, toss with 20 g cornstarch and a pinch of salt so it gets an even, thin coating.
2. Peel the ginger and cut into thin matchsticks (julienne). Trim the spring onions: cut the white and light green parts into 4-5 cm pieces and slice the dark green stalks into rings for garnish. Chop the garlic finely.
3. In a small bowl, mix the glaze: the soy sauce, sesame oil, 10 g cornstarch and 50 ml cold water, until the starch has completely dissolved.
4. **Frying and assembly:** Heat a wok or deep frying pan over high heat, then pour in the sunflower oil. Add the tofu cubes and fry for 6-8 minutes until golden brown and crisp on all sides. Remove the tofu to a plate.
5. In the same hot wok, add the ginger strips, garlic and the white parts of the spring onions. Fry, stirring constantly, for about 1 minute until intensely fragrant, taking care not to burn them.
6. Pour the pre-mixed sauce into the wok and bring to the boil until it becomes nicely thick and glossy in 30 seconds. Return the crispy tofu cubes to the sauce so that the glaze coats every piece evenly, then turn off the heat immediately.
7. **Serving:** Meanwhile, cook the rice until tender in boiling, lightly salted water according to the package instructions.
8. Serve the crispy glazed tofu on a bed of warm rice, generously sprinkled with the reserved green spring onion rings and toasted sesame seeds.

## Tips

- The secret to truly crispy tofu is thorough removal of moisture: press the tofu between paper towels with a weight for 10 minutes before cubing.
- If you like spicy flavours, you can also add a little fresh chopped chilli or dried chilli flakes to the hot oil along with the ginger.
