## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Drizzle the eggplant with the vegetable oil, season with salt and pepper, place it on a baking tray and bake in the preheated oven for 20 minutes. Then let it cool, peel it, cut it in half lengthwise and cut each half into 6-8 strips depending on its size.
3. Put the cut eggplant in a bowl, add 2 tablespoons of olive oil and the thyme, mix and set aside.
4. Crumble the goat cheese into smaller pieces and roughly chop the salmon. In a bowl, mix the yogurt, cream, vinegar, the remaining olive oil, salt and most of the dill.
5. Put the eggplant in a sieve and let the oil drain off into a bowl.
6. Arrange the drained eggplant on a plate and top with the goat cheese and smoked salmon. Pour over the yogurt dressing and the oil left from marinating the eggplant. Garnish with the raisins and the remaining dill.
