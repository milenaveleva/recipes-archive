## Method

1. **Preparing the beetroot and buckwheat:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Peel the beetroot, cut it into about 1.5-2 cm cubes and toss with 15 ml of extra virgin olive oil, a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper. Spread on a baking tray lined with parchment paper and roast for 30-35 minutes until caramelised and tender.
2. While the beetroot roasts, rinse the buckwheat thoroughly under cold running water in a fine sieve. Put it in a pot with twice its volume of boiling, lightly salted water and cook, covered, over low heat for 15 minutes until tender and the liquid is absorbed. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork and let cool to lukewarm.
3. **Toasting and making the dressing:** In a dry pan without any fat, toast the walnut halves over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until fragrant, taking care not to burn them. Leave to cool on a chopping board, then chop roughly.
4. Make the Italian honey-balsamic dressing: in a small bowl, whisk the remaining 30 ml of extra virgin olive oil, the balsamic vinegar and the honey until smooth, then season with a little salt and freshly ground black pepper.
5. **Assembly and serving:** In a large salad bowl, mix the lukewarm cooked buckwheat, the sweet roasted beetroot cubes and the washed, drained fresh arugula. Drizzle with the balsamic dressing and toss gently so the dressing coats everything.
6. Divide the salad among plates, then crumble the creamy, piquant gorgonzola on top and sprinkle with the toasted, crunchy walnuts. Serve fresh, while still lukewarm.

## Tips

- You can roast the beetroot the day before, so assembling the salad takes just 20 minutes for weeknight dinners.
- If you prefer milder flavours to the intense tang of gorgonzola, choose gorgonzola dolce, or substitute goat cheese.
