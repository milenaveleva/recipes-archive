## Method

1. Wash the eggplants and cut them into 3–4 mm thick slices. Lay out the slices, salt them and let rest for 15 minutes to draw out moisture. Then pat the slices dry with paper towels and season lightly with pepper.
2. Brush a pan with a little olive oil and heat it. Fry both sides of the eggplant slices until golden brown.
3. Cut the feta into 0.5 cm thick sticks. Halve the olives lengthwise. Halve the avocado, remove the pit, peel and slice it.
4. Place a piece of cheese, a slice of avocado and half an olive on each fried eggplant slice. Roll up tightly. Sprinkle the finished rolls with grated Parmesan and chopped parsley. Serve immediately cold, or bake them under the grill for 2 minutes. Excellent as a standalone appetizer or as a main course with a side.
