## Method

1. **Preparation and frying:** Put plenty of water on to boil in a large pot for the pasta, and salt it generously.
2. Halve the fennel, remove the core and cut the bulb into thin strips. Cut the garlic into thin slices and the sun-dried tomatoes into strips.
3. In a large frying pan, heat 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil. Add the sliced fennel, then fry over medium heat for about 7 minutes until golden brown and softly caramelised.
4. Add the garlic and the strips of sun-dried tomato to the fennel. Fry for another 1-2 minutes until the garlic smells pleasantly fragrant. Season with a little salt and freshly ground black pepper.
5. **Cooking the pasta and serving:** Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti al dente in the boiling salted water according to the packet instructions.
6. Before draining the pasta, scoop out a cup (about 100 ml) of the starchy cooking water. Drain the spaghetti, then immediately toss it into the fried fennel ragù in the pan.
7. Pour in half of the reserved cooking water and the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, then toss over a lively heat for 1 minute so that a silky, glossy sauce coats the pasta.
8. Take it off the heat, sprinkle with the freshly grated Parmesan and the finely chopped parsley. Serve immediately, hot.

## Tips

- Do not throw away the green, dill-like fronds of the fennel: chop them and sprinkle them over the finished dish together with the parsley for extra freshness.
