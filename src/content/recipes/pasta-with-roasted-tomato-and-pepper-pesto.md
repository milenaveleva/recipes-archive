## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 210–215 °C and prepare a roughly 30 cm × 25 cm baking tray.
2. Deseed the red bell peppers, cut them into larger pieces and put them in the tray. Peel the onion, quarter it together with the tomatoes and add to the peppers. Peel the garlic, grate it finely and put it in the tray too. Finally sprinkle with the dried oregano, basil, salt and pepper, drizzle with the rapeseed oil and toss thoroughly. Bake in the preheated oven for 40–45 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, cook the pasta: bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot. When the water is boiling, add the whole-wheat pasta and cook for 14–15 minutes (or as stated on the packet) until al dente. Then drain.
4. When the vegetables are roasted, take the tray out of the oven. Put the roasted vegetables in a tall blender jug, add the fresh parsley and purée completely smooth with a stick blender.
5. Mix the resulting pesto with the cooked pasta. Divide into 4 portions and serve immediately.

## Tips

- When serving, you can sprinkle a little grated Parmesan or toasted pine nuts on top for an even more intense flavour.
