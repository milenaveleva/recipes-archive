## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Cook the pasta in lightly salted boiling water. The cooking time depends on the type of pasta: fresh pasta takes 2-3 minutes, while dried pasta usually needs 12-20 minutes.
2. While the pasta cooks, finely chop the onion, crush the garlic and wash the spinach in cold water. If the spinach leaves are too large, chop them roughly. Chop the dill as well.
3. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Heat a large pan over medium heat, then place the salmon fillets in it skin side down. Cook for about 2 minutes, then carefully turn them over and cook the other side for another 1.5 minutes.
4. Remove the salmon from the pan and place it in a heatproof dish (about 20x30 cm). In the same pan, heat the olive oil, add the chopped onion and sauté for about 1 minute. Then lower the heat, stir in the garlic and immediately add the spinach as well. Cook together for 4 minutes over low heat.
5. Pour the coconut milk over the contents of the pan, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 3 minutes. Pour the resulting sauce over the salmon fillets in the heatproof dish. Place the dish in the preheated oven and bake at 170-180 °C for 7-8 minutes.
6. Serve the baked salmon with the cooked pasta and sprinkle the top with the fresh chopped dill.

## Tips

- For an even more intense flavour, add a little freshly grated ginger or lime juice to the sauce.
