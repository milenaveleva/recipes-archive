## Method

1. Peel the bananas and kiwis, then cut them into larger pieces.
2. Put all the ingredients in a blender. Set aside a little of the walnuts for garnish.
3. Blend everything until completely smooth.
4. Pour the smoothie into 4 glasses and garnish with fresh basil leaves and the reserved chopped walnuts.

## Tips

- For an even more refreshing drink, use frozen banana slices!
