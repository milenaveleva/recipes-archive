## Method

1. Put a large pot of water on to boil and, when it is bubbling, salt it generously. Add the penne and cook it 1 minute less than the package time so it stays perfectly al dente. Before draining, scoop out a mugful (about 150 ml) of the starchy cooking water, as it is key to making the pesto creamy.
2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant, then tip them onto a cutting board and let cool.
3. Make the pesto: into a food processor put three quarters of the washed, dried arugula, most of the cooled toasted walnuts, the crushed garlic clove, the grated Parmesan and the grated pecorino. Add 1 teaspoon of freshly squeezed lemon juice, salt and pepper. Start pulsing, drizzling in the extra virgin olive oil in a thin stream, until you get a silky, creamy paste.
4. Return the drained hot penne to the warm pot and immediately stir in the fresh arugula pesto. Add 3–4 tablespoons of warm pasta water and toss energetically so the pesto and cooking water form a silky, glossy emulsion that coats the penne perfectly.
5. Serve the pasta immediately in deep plates. Sprinkle the top with the reserved toasted walnut pieces, the remaining fresh arugula leaves, a little freshly grated Parmesan and finely grated lemon zest.

## Tips

- Never heat arugula in a pan over a flame, as it loses its bright green colour and can turn bitter. Always toss it with the hot pasta and cooking water off the heat for a silky texture.
- If you prefer a milder flavour, you can replace a third of the arugula with tender baby spinach or fresh basil leaves.
