## Method

1. Wash the cucumbers thoroughly and remove their prickly hairs with a brush.
2. Cut off both ends, then cut a cross-wise slit in each without cutting them in half.
3. Put a large bunch of dill and the peeled garlic cloves at the bottom of the jar.
4. Pack the cucumbers tightly against the jar wall and fill the gaps with smaller pieces.
5. Pour the brine (1.5 litres of water and 33-35 grams of salt) over them so that the cucumbers are completely covered.
6. Close the jar and place it somewhere not too warm and out of direct sunlight.
7. Open the lid slightly each day so the gases that form can escape.
8. Watch the fermentation, which shows as bubbles and cloudiness. Taste as you go; you can learn a lot from it!
9. The cucumbers are usually ready in 7-10 days, but taste best after 14-20 days.
10. For longer storage, transfer the cucumbers into smaller jars and keep them in the fridge.
11. We think dill and garlic are plenty, but you can experiment with horseradish, mustard seeds or even coriander seeds to shape the flavour to your taste.
