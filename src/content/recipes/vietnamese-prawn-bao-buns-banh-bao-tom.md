## Method

1. **Preparing and proving the bao dough:** In a bowl, mix 170 ml lukewarm water with the yeast and honey, then let stand for 5 minutes until it foams slightly.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the whole-wheat flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt. Pour in the yeast water and knead into a smooth, elastic dough in about 8-10 minutes. Cover with a clean tea towel and prove in a warm place for 1 hour until doubled in size.
3. Divide the risen dough into 8 equal parts. Shape them into balls, then roll them out into slightly oval shapes. Brush the surface thinly with sunflower oil, fold in half with a small piece of baking paper between the halves, and let rise for another 15 minutes.
4. **Steaming and preparing the prawns:** Cut the carrot and long cucumber into thin matchsticks. Toss them in a small bowl with the rice vinegar, a pinch of salt, and set aside to pickle. In another small bowl, mix the Greek yogurt with the sriracha sauce.
5. Place the bao buns in a steamer or bamboo steamer and steam them over boiling water for 10-12 minutes. Do not open the lid while steaming so the buns do not collapse.
6. Remove the woody outer layer of the lemongrass and chop the white inner part finely together with the garlic and chilli. In a hot frying pan, heat sunflower oil, fry the fragrant aromatics for 1 minute, then add the cleaned prawns. Drizzle with the fish sauce, soy sauce and sesame oil, then fry over high heat for 3-4 minutes until the prawns are pink and crisp-tender.
7. **Assembly and serving:** Open up the warm steamed bao buns and spread the insides with the spicy sriracha yogurt sauce.
8. Fill with the lemongrass prawns, pile on the crunchy pickled carrot and cucumber, and sprinkle with fresh coriander and thinly sliced spring onions. Serve immediately, hot!

## Tips

- The secret to perfectly soft bao buns is finely milled whole-wheat flour and resting after steaming: after turning off the heat, leave the steamer covered for another 2-3 minutes so the buns do not collapse.
- If you want an even juicier result, add the freshly squeezed juice of half a lime to the pan at the last moment when frying the prawns.
