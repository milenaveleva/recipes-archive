## Method

1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pan and fry the shrimp over medium-high heat for a few minutes. Set aside.
2. Finely chop the garlic, red onion and ginger.
3. In 1 tbsp olive oil fry the onions and ginger, then add 1-2 tbsp (to taste) of curry paste and a can of coconut cream. Cook for a few minutes, then stir in the shrimp.
4. Serve with cooked rice, lime and fresh cilantro leaves.
