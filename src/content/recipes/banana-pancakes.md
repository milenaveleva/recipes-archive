## Method

1. Blend the oats, almond milk, banana, baking powder, cinnamon, lemon juice and vanilla until smooth. Loosen with a splash more almond milk if the batter is too stiff to pour.
2. Heat a large non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
3. Pour in about ¼ cup of batter per pancake and cook for 2–3 minutes, until bubbles appear on top. Flip and cook 30 seconds more.
4. Serve hot with sliced banana and a drizzle of honey.

## Tips

- Vanilla powder works in place of the extract.
