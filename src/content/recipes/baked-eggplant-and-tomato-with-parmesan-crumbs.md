## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190–195 °C. Line a roughly 20 x 30 cm baking tray with baking paper.
2. Wash the eggplant and tomatoes, then cut them into slices about 0.5 cm thick. Arrange them in the tray, alternating eggplant and tomato slices. Bake the vegetables in the preheated oven for 12–13 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, make the Parmesan crumbs: chop the basil finely, then mix it in a small bowl with the breadcrumbs (preferably cornmeal-based), the grated Parmesan, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, salt and pepper.
4. Take the tray out of the oven and raise the temperature to 210–215 °C. Sprinkle the crumb mixture evenly over the vegetables and tear the mozzarella over the top. Return to the oven and bake for another 10–11 minutes, until the cheese has melted and the crumbs are golden brown.
5. After baking, divide into 3 portions and serve fresh.
