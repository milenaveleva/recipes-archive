## Method

1. Fill a large pot with water for the pasta and bring it to the boil over high heat. Salt the water, and as soon as it boils, add the spaghetti. Cook for the time on the packet (about 10-12 minutes) until al dente, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, peel the garlic and cut it into thin slices. Wash the cherry tomatoes and halve them. Heat the olive oil in a medium frying pan over medium heat. When the oil is hot, add the garlic slices and fry for about 1 minute.
3. Then add the halved cherry tomatoes, press them gently with a wooden spoon so they release their juices, and keep cooking for 4-5 minutes over medium heat.
4. Finally, add the drained pasta to the pan. Tear the basil roughly, cut or tear the mozzarella into larger pieces, and stir both into the pasta. Season with pepper to taste, toss everything together and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Use mini mozzarella balls to make it even quicker.
