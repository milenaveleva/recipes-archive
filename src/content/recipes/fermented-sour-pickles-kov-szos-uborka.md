## Method

1. Wash the cucumbers thoroughly and trim off the ends. Cut a cross into each end. Place half of the dill and garlic in the bottom of the jar.
2. Arrange the cucumbers in the jar. Put the remaining dill and garlic on top and finish with the bread.
3. Cover the jar with a saucer and put it in a warm place, but not in direct sunlight.
4. Check it every few days, and if the water has evaporated, top up with salted water.
5. Dissolve the salt in 0.9 litres of lukewarm water, then fill the jar with the salted water.
6. Depending on the weather, the pickles are ready in 3-4 days; check by tasting. When they are done, remove the bread and dill, transfer the cucumbers to a container and pour the brine over them through a strainer, adding the garlic. Serve cold.
