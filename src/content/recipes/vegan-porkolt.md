## Method

1. Chop the garlic and onion.
2. Wash and peel the potatoes, then cut them into cubes together with the tofu.
3. Heat the oil in a large pot and sauté the onions until they begin to brown.
4. Add the cubed potatoes and tofu and fry them together.
5. Add the paprika and stir, then pour in about 650 ml of water before the paprika burns.
6. Add salt and cook uncovered over medium heat for 30 minutes, until thickened and the potatoes are tender (if the liquid evaporates too fast, add more water).
7. Stir in the tomato paste and marjoram, mixing until the paste dissolves. Then remove from the heat and serve.
