## Method

1. **Preparation and vegetable base:** Rinse the pearl barley thoroughly in a sieve, then cook in twice its volume of lightly salted water or broth for about 25-30 minutes until half-soft, then drain.
2. Boil water with a little salt in a large pot. Carefully peel 8 intact, nice outer leaves from the savoy cabbage and blanch them in the boiling water for 2-3 minutes until pliable. Plunge into ice water, then drain and carefully trim the thicker leaf ribs thinner.
3. Chop the onion finely, peel the pumpkin and cut it into small half-centimetre cubes, and chop the cleaned cremini mushrooms finely too.
4. **Filling and assembly:** Heat 2 tablespoons of sunflower oil in a pan. Add the diced onion and sauté until glassy, then add two thirds of the crushed garlic, the pumpkin cubes and the mushrooms. Fry over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until the vegetables brown slightly and collapse.
5. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the pre-cooked barley, 1 teaspoon of sweet paprika, half of the caraway, salt, ground black pepper and half of the finely chopped fresh parsley. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
6. Spread the blanched cabbage leaves on a cutting board. Divide the pumpkin-mushroom-barley filling evenly among them, fold in the two sides of the leaves, then roll up tightly, just like stuffed cabbage.
7. **Baking and serving:** Pour 1 tablespoon of sunflower oil into the bottom of an ovenproof dish or tray, stir in 1 teaspoon of paprika, the remaining crushed garlic and the remaining caraway, then pour in the hot vegetable broth. Arrange the cabbage rolls tightly in it.
8. Cover the dish with aluminium foil and bake in an oven preheated to 180 °C for 35-40 minutes. Remove the foil for the last 10 minutes so the tops of the rolls brown slightly and the paprika sauce thickens.
9. To serve, place 2 rolls on each plate, drizzle with the rich vegetable sauce, spoon over plenty of cool plant-based sour cream and sprinkle with the remaining fresh parsley. Serve with fresh whole-wheat sourdough bread!

## Tips

- After blanching, it is worth shaving the thick central rib of the savoy cabbage flat with a sharp knife, so the leaves roll up much more easily and without tearing.
- For even more intense flavours you can roast the pumpkin cubes in the oven first, or add a pinch of smoked paprika to the filling.
