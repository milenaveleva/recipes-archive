## Method

1. **Preparing and frying the vegetables:** Deseed the bell pepper and cut it into thin strips or bite-sized cubes. Clean and slice the cremini mushrooms, halve the cherry tomatoes and cut the garlic cloves into thin slices.
2. Heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large frying pan. Add the sliced garlic, the pepper and the mushrooms. Fry over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes, until the mushrooms turn golden brown and the pepper softens slightly but stays crunchy.
3. Now add the halved cherry tomatoes to the pan, season with salt and pepper. Fry for another 3–4 minutes, until the tomato skins start to wrinkle and release their sweet juices, forming a saucy base.
4. **Cooking the pasta, tossing and serving:** Meanwhile, bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, salt it well, and cook the penne for 1 minute less than the packet time, to al dente. Before draining, scoop out about 150 ml of starchy cooking water.
5. Tip the cooked penne straight into the pan of vegetables. Sprinkle with about 100 ml of the pasta water and toss intensely over high heat for 1–2 minutes, so that the starchy water and the olive oil form a silky, glossy emulsion on the pasta.
6. Take off the heat, sprinkle over the roughly torn fresh basil leaves and half of the freshly grated Parmesan. Mix gently, then serve immediately in deep plates, garnished with the remaining Parmesan and extra basil.

## Tips

- The pan should be big enough that the vegetables fry and do not steam – the intense frying of the mushrooms and pepper gives Ortolana its authentic, full flavour.
- The pasta water is a real kitchen miracle: its starch helps form a glossy, saucy sauce with the olive oil that clings perfectly to the ridges of the penne.
