## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190-195 °C and prepare a baking tray or ovenproof dish of about 25 × 30 cm.
2. Bring salted water to the boil in a medium pot and cook the spaghetti according to the packet instructions (about 10-12 minutes). When done, drain.
3. Dice the celery and onion, and halve the cherry tomatoes. Put all the vegetables in the baking dish, add the olive oil, salt, pepper and the canned chopped tomatoes, and mix well. Place the feta in one piece on top of the vegetable mixture. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes.
4. Take the dish out of the oven and sprinkle with the finely chopped basil and parsley. Use a fork to mash the baked feta and mix it into the vegetables until you have a creamy sauce.
5. Finally, add the cooked spaghetti, toss with the sauce and serve immediately.
