## Method

1. **Prep and toast the walnuts:** In a dry frying pan over medium heat, toast the roughly chopped walnuts for 2–3 minutes, until they smell nicely roasted. Set aside to cool.
2. Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot and add half of the fine salt. Wash the broccoli and cut it into bite-sized florets.
3. **Cook the pasta and broccoli:** Add the dry penne to the rolling, hot salted water. After 5 minutes of cooking add the broccoli florets to the water as well and cook together for another 4–5 minutes, until the pasta is al dente and the broccoli is tender and bright green.
4. Before draining, scoop out 1 cup (about 150–200 ml) of starchy pasta water for the sauce, then drain the pasta together with the broccoli.
5. **Assemble the gorgonzola sauce and serve:** Meanwhile, warm the extra virgin olive oil in the frying pan, add the finely crushed garlic and sweat it over low heat for 1 minute, taking care that it does not brown.
6. Pour in the plant-based cooking cream, crumble in the gorgonzola and season with the ground nutmeg, the remaining salt and freshly ground black pepper. Stir over low heat for 2–3 minutes, until the cheese has melted completely and forms a silky sauce.
7. Add the drained broccoli and penne to the sauce. Pour in 3–4 tablespoons of the reserved pasta water and toss over high heat for 1 minute, so that the creamy sauce coats the pasta perfectly.
8. Divide among warm plates and sprinkle with the crunchy toasted walnuts and freshly grated Parmesan. Serve immediately, piping hot.

## Tips

- Pasta water is an indispensable part of the sauce: its starch helps emulsify the plant-based cooking cream and the melting cheese, so the sauce does not split but stays velvety smooth.
- If you prefer milder flavours, choose sweet Gorgonzola Dolce; if you like a stronger, spicier profile, Gorgonzola Piccante is the perfect choice.
