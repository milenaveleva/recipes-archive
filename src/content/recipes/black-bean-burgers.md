## Method

1. Soak the black beans overnight, then drain, cover with fresh water and simmer for about 1 hour until tender. Drain well.
2. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and line a baking tray with parchment.
3. Warm the water in a frying pan over high heat and cook the onion and garlic, stirring, for about 5 minutes until soft; add a splash more water if they catch.
4. Pulse the walnuts, chia, parsley, thyme, sage, poultry seasoning, paprika and salt in a food processor to a coarse crumb, then tip into a mixing bowl.
5. Add the beans and the onion mixture to the processor and pulse a few times until roughly broken up, then add the zucchini and pulse briefly. Stir into the walnut crumb until evenly mixed.
6. Shape into small patties about 7 cm across and set them on the tray. Bake for 30 minutes, turning once halfway.
7. Serve in whole-grain buns with tomato, cucumber, lettuce, avocado, grilled onion and hummus.

## Tips

- Mix and match the toppings; any crisp salad vegetables work.
