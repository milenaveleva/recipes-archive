## Method

1. Bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a pot and cook the penne al dente according to the packet instructions. Set aside half a cup of the pasta cooking water.
2. Meanwhile, wash the zucchini and cut it into thin half-moons or strips. Crush the garlic and chop the fresh dill finely.
3. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and zucchini and fry for 4-5 minutes until the zucchini is lightly browned and soft.
4. Pour the plant-based cooking cream into the pan and season with salt, freshly ground black pepper and the finely chopped dill. Bring to the boil over low heat and simmer for 2 minutes until the cream thickens slightly.
5. Stir the pasta into the dill and zucchini cream sauce. If it seems too thick, add 2-3 tablespoons of the reserved pasta water. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan, mix thoroughly and serve immediately, garnished with fresh dill.

## Tips

- When serving, you can drizzle a little fresh lemon juice over the top, which makes the dill and zucchini combination even fresher.
