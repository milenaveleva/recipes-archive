## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Put a large pot with plenty of water on to cook the spaghetti. Salt it and bring it to the boil. When the water is boiling vigorously, add the spaghetti and cook it for the time given on the packet (about 14-15 minutes) over medium heat.
2. Meanwhile, prepare the other ingredients: peel and finely chop the onion and garlic. Roughly chop the parsley and finely grate the Parmesan. Wash the courgette, then use a vegetable peeler to cut long ribbons from its skin and seedless flesh. Cut the seeded inner part into cubes of about 1 × 1 cm.
3. Before draining the pasta, ladle out 200 ml of the cooking water, then drain the spaghetti.
4. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, garlic and courgette cubes and fry for 2 minutes. Then pour in 100-200 ml of the reserved cooking water, add the courgette ribbons, the plant-based cooking cream, the grated lemon zest, salt and pepper. Cook everything together for 1 minute. Finally fold in the drained spaghetti and the chopped parsley.
5. Divide the finished courgette spaghetti into 4 portions, sprinkle with the grated Parmesan and garnish with fresh basil. Serve warm.

## Tips

- For a vegan version, use soy cooking cream and a vegan Parmesan substitute.
