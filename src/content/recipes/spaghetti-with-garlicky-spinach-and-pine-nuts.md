## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the pasta:** Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, then salt it generously with the fine salt. Add the dry spaghetti and cook for 1–2 minutes less than the packet says, so it stays perfectly al dente (firm to the bite). Before draining, scoop out and set aside about 150–200 ml of the starchy pasta water.
2. Toast the pine nuts in a dry frying pan without any fat over medium heat until golden, about 2–3 minutes, shaking continuously so they do not burn. Take them out into a small bowl and set aside.
3. **Spinach base and tossing:** In the same roomy pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil. Add the thinly sliced fresh garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes. Fry over low-medium heat for 1 minute until the garlic becomes fragrant, but take care it does not turn brown, because then it becomes bitter.
4. Throw in the washed, drained fresh spinach in several batches. Wilt over higher heat for 1–2 minutes, until it has just collapsed but is still a nice bright green. Season with a little salt and freshly ground black pepper.
5. Pour the drained hot spaghetti straight onto the spinach in the pan. Add 1 ladle of the reserved pasta water and sprinkle over half of the grated Parmesan. Toss and shake vigorously over high heat for 1 minute so that a silky, creamy emulsion coats the strands.
6. **Serving:** Turn off the heat. To taste, drip over a few drops of fresh organic lemon juice and grate on a hint of lemon zest for freshness. Divide the pasta among deep plates and sprinkle with the crisp toasted pine nuts and the remaining grated Parmesan. Serve immediately, piping hot.

## Tips

- The pasta water is essential for the perfect sauce: the starch cooked out of the pasta and the olive oil form a creamy emulsion with the grated cheese, even without cream.
- Because of their high oil content, pine nuts burn quickly, so do not leave the pan unattended while toasting, and as soon as they are toasted, tip them out of the hot pan immediately.
