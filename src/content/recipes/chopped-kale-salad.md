## Method

1. Put the kale and green onions in the food processor and run it on high until very finely chopped, scraping down the sides as needed. Tip into a large bowl.
2. Pulse the cucumber for a few seconds until chopped and add it to the bowl. Pulse the celery once or twice, then the tomatoes, adding each to the bowl.
3. Return everything to the processor with the asparagus, orange juice and any of the garlic, chili and date you are using. Pulse once or twice to combine.
4. Divide between bowls and serve right away.

## Tips

- Use the juice of a second orange for a juicier salad.
