## Method

1. Cook the onion and garlic in a large nonstick pot over medium-high heat for 3 to 5 minutes until translucent, adding a splash of water if they stick.
2. Add the chopped asparagus and cook for 3 minutes until it starts to soften.
3. Pour in the water, add the thyme, lemon juice and lemon zest, bring to a simmer and cook for 10 to 15 minutes until the asparagus is very soft.
4. Blend until smooth, in batches if needed and with the lid vented, or use a stick blender in the pot.
5. Return the soup to the pot, bring back to a simmer and adjust the seasoning.
6. Serve straight away, topped with raw asparagus ribbons shaved with a vegetable peeler if you like.

## Tips

- Leeks can replace the onion and dried basil the thyme.
- A homemade vegetable broth in place of the water gives a richer soup.
