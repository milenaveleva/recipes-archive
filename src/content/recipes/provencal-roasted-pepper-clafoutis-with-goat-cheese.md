## Method

1. **Preparation and sautéing:** Preheat the oven to 190 °C and grease a ceramic or ovenproof dish of about 24-26 cm diameter with olive oil.
2. Wash the kápia peppers, deseed them and cut them lengthwise into thin strips. Crush the garlic cloves finely.
3. In a frying pan, heat 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil, then add the pepper strips and garlic. Cook over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until the peppers are tender and lightly browned. Season with salt and pepper and stir in half of the thyme.
4. **Assembly and baking:** In a bowl, whisk the eggs smooth with the flour using a hand whisk, then gradually pour in the oat milk. Whisk until lump-free and silky, like crêpe batter, then add a pinch of salt and pepper.
5. Spread the browned pepper strips evenly in the dish, crumble the soft goat cheese over them and sprinkle with the remaining thyme.
6. Pour the oat milk and egg batter over the vegetables and cheese in the dish.
7. Bake in the preheated oven for 30-35 minutes until the clafoutis is nicely golden brown and set in the middle. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- The clafoutis slices best when lukewarm; served with a fresh green salad it makes a perfect autumn dinner.
- Instead of goat cheese you can make it with creamy feta or ricotta if you prefer a milder flavour.
