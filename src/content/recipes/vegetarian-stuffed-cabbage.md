## Method

1. Cut the mushrooms into roughly 1 cm cubes and sear them in a dry, hot pan.
2. When the mushrooms have nice colour, add the oil, chopped onion and thinly sliced garlic and keep frying.
3. Add the meat substitute, season with salt, pepper, sweet and smoked paprika and caraway, and sprinkle with the soy sauce. After a short fry the base of the filling is done.
4. Add the pre-cooked rice to this base, mix and leave to cool. Once cool, add the eggs and mix again.
5. For the cabbage base, sauté the chopped onion in oil in a pot over medium-high heat, add the garlic and fry briefly. Then spoon in the tomato paste and fry until golden brown.
6. Sprinkle with caraway and paprika, season with salt and pepper and, after stirring, add the shredded sauerkraut. (Taste the sauerkraut first; if it is too sour, rinse it once.)
7. Mix the cabbage base with the onion base, add water and simmer over medium heat while you stuff the leaves.
8. Thinly slice off the thick stem part of the stuffing leaves and place an oblong, meatball-sized portion of filling at the bottom of each. Roll up and tuck in any overhanging parts.
9. When all are stuffed, lift out half of the cabbage base and arrange the stuffed cabbage rolls on the cabbage left in the pot. Cover with the removed cabbage, add water to almost cover the rolls and cook covered for 1 hour over low heat.
10. Then lift out the stuffed cabbage and thicken the base with sour cream stirred with the cornstarch and 100 ml water.
11. To serve, ladle some of the base onto a plate, top with the stuffed cabbage, a little sour cream and a sprinkle of paprika.
