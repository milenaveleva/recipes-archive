## Method

1. Combine the oats and apricot in a pot, then pour in ¾ cup water.
2. Cook over medium heat for about 5-7 minutes, then let cool. Serve with the fruit.

## Tips

- If you would like a thinner porridge, thin it with breast milk, formula, plant-based drink or cow's milk, as appropriate for the child's age.
