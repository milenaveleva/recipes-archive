## Method

1. Put the whole squash in a large pot, cover with water and boil for 5–7 minutes to soften slightly. Drain and leave to cool.
2. Cut the potatoes and carrots into rough chunks. Halve the cooled squash, scoop out the seeds and cut it into chunks.
3. Return the squash, potatoes and carrots to the pot with about 5 cm of water, bring to a boil, cover and steam until just cooked, stirring now and then and topping up the water if needed.
4. In a large pot, heat the coconut oil and cook the onions over high heat for about 5 minutes until soft, adding a splash of water if they stick.
5. Stir in the garlic, ginger and curry powder for 1 minute, then add the coconut milk, honey and salt.
6. Add the steamed vegetables, bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 10–15 minutes until tender.
7. Serve topped with cilantro, a squeeze of lime and a pinch of red pepper flakes.
