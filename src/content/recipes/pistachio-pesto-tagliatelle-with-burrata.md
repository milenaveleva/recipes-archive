## Method

1. **Making the pesto and cooking the pasta:** Bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta.
2. Toast the shelled pistachios in a dry frying pan over low heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant. Set aside one tablespoon for serving.
3. Put the toasted pistachios, fresh basil, grated Parmesan, peeled garlic clove, extra virgin olive oil, a pinch of salt and ground black pepper in a food processor and blend into a creamy pesto.
4. **Assembly:** Cook the tagliatelle al dente according to the packet instructions. Before draining, set aside a ladleful of the cooking water.
5. Mix the warm pasta with the freshly made pistachio pesto and add 2–3 tablespoons of cooking water so the sauce coats the pasta beautifully.
6. Serve the pasta in deep plates, top with the halved fresh burrata, and sprinkle with the reserved pistachios and fresh basil leaves.

## Tips

- If you want an even creamier texture, add the pasta cooking water gradually while mixing in the pesto.
