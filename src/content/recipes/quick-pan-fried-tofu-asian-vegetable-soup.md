## Method

1. **Preparation:** Pat the tofu thoroughly dry with paper towel, then cut into 2 cm cubes. Slice the shiitake and the peeled carrot thinly and break the broccoli into small florets. Grate the ginger and garlic and slice the spring onions.
2. **Wok frying:** Heat a wok or deep pan over high heat and pour in the sunflower oil. Add the tofu cubes and fry until golden brown and crisp on all sides, about 4-5 minutes, then lift onto a plate.
3. Into the hot oil left behind throw the grated ginger and garlic and stir-fry quickly for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the sliced shiitake, carrot and broccoli florets and toss-fry for another 2 minutes.
4. **Simmering and serving:** Pour the vegetable broth and soy sauce over the vegetables. Bring quickly to the boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 4-5 minutes until the vegetables are still pleasantly crunchy.
5. In the last minute stir the golden-fried tofu cubes back into the hot soup to warm through. Serve in deep bowls, generously sprinkled with fresh spring onion rings.

## Tips

- If you like it spicy, drizzle a little chili sesame oil over the soup or add fresh chili slices when serving.
