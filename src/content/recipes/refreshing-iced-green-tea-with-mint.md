## Method

1. Bring 1 litre of water to a boil.
2. Remove from the heat and add the green tea and the 3 sprigs of mint.
3. Cover and let steep. Remove the tea bags after 3 minutes and the mint after 20 minutes.
4. After removing the mint, stir in the honey, add 500 ml cold water and 1 cup of ice.
5. Pour into glasses and serve with fresh mint leaves. You can also add lemon and lime.
