## Method

1. Pour 2.5 l of water into a large pot and bring it to the boil. Meanwhile, peel the garlic and cut it into thin slices, and halve the cherry tomatoes. When the water boils, salt it, add the pasta and cook for 8-12 minutes (or as long as the packet says) over medium heat.
2. Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large frying pan. Add the sliced garlic, the anchovy fillets and the chili flakes. Fry over low to medium heat for 3-5 minutes until the garlic is fragrant and the anchovies have completely dissolved into the oil. Then add the halved cherry tomatoes (careful, they may spit!) and fry for another 2-3 minutes until the tomatoes release their juices.
3. By now the pasta is almost done. Before draining, scoop out 100 ml of the cooking water and pour the rest away. When the tomatoes are soft, pour the reserved cooking water into the pan, add the drained tuna, and heat for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens slightly.
4. Turn off the heat and stir the cooked pasta and the fresh basil leaves into the sauce. Season with freshly squeezed lemon juice, salt and pepper to taste. Toss thoroughly, divide among 3 plates and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Anchovies add a natural salty depth to the dish, but you can leave them out if you do not like them.
- Use good-quality chunky canned tuna for the best texture.
