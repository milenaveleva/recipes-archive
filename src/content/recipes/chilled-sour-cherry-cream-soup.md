## Method

1. Pit the sour cherries. Put the cherries, coconut yogurt, half of the plant-based whipping cream, the pitted dates and the ground vanilla into a blender and blend until creamy.
2. Whip the other half of the cream until stiff. Serve the soup with the plant-based whipped cream and a few chopped pistachios.
