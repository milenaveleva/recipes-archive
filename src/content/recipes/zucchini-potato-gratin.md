## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180–185 °C (355–365 °F). Prepare a round baking dish (about 25 cm in diameter) and a larger bowl for mixing.
2. Peel the potatoes and zucchini and cut them into slices about 0.5 cm thick. Put them in the large bowl. Peel the garlic, grate it finely and add it to the vegetables with the olive oil, thyme, salt and pepper. Toss thoroughly.
3. Arrange the potato and zucchini slices in the baking dish, working from the edge toward the centre and alternating the two vegetables. Cover the dish with a lid or foil and bake at 180–185 °C for 30 minutes. Then remove the lid/foil and bake another 45–50 minutes until the vegetables are tender and golden.
4. Meanwhile, finely grate the mozzarella and mix it in a small bowl with the cream, eggs, and a little salt and pepper.
5. When the baking time is up, take the dish out of the oven, pour over the mozzarella-egg mixture, then return it to the oven for another 12–15 minutes until the top browns nicely.
6. Divide the finished gratin into 4 portions and serve warm.

## Tips

- Serve with fresh thyme or a little extra grated cheese for even more flavour.
