## Method

1. Bring plenty of lightly salted water to the boil in a pot. Cook the orzo in it according to the package instructions (about 8-9 minutes) until al dente, then drain and drizzle with a little extra virgin olive oil so it doesn't stick together.
2. In a dry frying pan, toast the pine nuts to golden yellow over low heat in 1-2 minutes, taking care they don't burn, then set aside in a small bowl.
3. In the same pan, heat a tablespoon of olive oil. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and the strips of sun-dried tomato and fry for 2-3 minutes until the fresh tomatoes soften slightly and caramelise.
4. Take the pan off the heat and toss in the still-warm cooked orzo, the fresh baby spinach, the toasted pine nuts and half of the grated Parmesan. The warmth of the pasta wilts the spinach pleasantly. Season with freshly squeezed organic lemon juice, salt, pepper and the remaining extra virgin olive oil.
5. Make the poached eggs: bring simmering water to a small pot, create a whirlpool in it with a spoon, then slide in the eggs, cracked one by one into a separate bowl. Cook for exactly 3 minutes so the white sets but the yolk stays perfectly runny. Lift out carefully with a slotted spoon.
6. Serving: Divide the warm Tuscan orzo salad among deep plates. Place a poached egg on top of each portion, sprinkle with the remaining shaved Parmesan and freshly ground black pepper. At the table, cut open the egg so the silky yolk coats the salad.

## Tips

- For the perfect poached egg, use the freshest eggs possible, and you can add 1 tablespoon of vinegar to the cooking water, which helps the white set quickly.
- The salad is also excellent cold the next day as a pasta salad, so it makes a perfect make-ahead work lunch.
