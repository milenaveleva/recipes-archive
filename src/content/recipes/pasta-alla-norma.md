## Method

1. **Preparation and frying the vegetables:** Wash the aubergines, cut them into cubes of about 2 cm, sprinkle with a pinch of salt and leave to stand for 10 minutes on a clean kitchen towel so they release their excess moisture.
2. In a larger, deep frying pan, heat 3-4 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil. Add the aubergine cubes and fry them over medium-high heat for about 8-10 minutes until golden brown and soft, then take them out onto a plate.
3. **Making the sauce and the pasta:** To the oil left in the pan add the thinly sliced fresh garlic and a pinch of chilli flakes. Fry for 1 minute until fragrant, but take care that it does not burn.
4. Pour in the canned chopped tomatoes and season with fine salt and freshly ground black pepper. Simmer the sauce over low heat for 12-15 minutes, until pleasantly thickened. At the end stir in the torn fresh basil leaves.
5. Meanwhile, bring plenty of lightly salted water to the boil in a large pot and cook the dry penne for 1 minute less than the time on the packet, so that it stays perfectly 'al dente'. Drain, but set aside 1 ladleful of the cooking water.
6. **Assembly and serving:** Toss the cooked penne into the thick tomato sauce together with three quarters of the fried aubergine cubes. If necessary, add a few tablespoons of cooking water for a silky texture.
7. Serve the hot pasta in deep plates, topped with the reserved crisp aubergine cubes, fresh basil leaves and plenty of crumbled creamy ricotta.

## Tips

- For the real Sicilian experience, fry the aubergine crisp in good-quality olive oil; the starch in the pasta cooking water helps the sauce coat the penne beautifully.
- If you want an even more characterful flavour, besides fresh ricotta you can try aged salted ricotta (ricotta salata) or a little grated pecorino.
