## Method

1. **Preparing and cooking the bulgur:** Cook the bulgur in twice its volume of lightly salted boiling water for 12–15 minutes until tender yet pleasantly chewy, then drain and leave to cool to lukewarm. In a dry pan, toast the walnuts over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant, then tip them onto a chopping board and chop coarsely.
2. **Grilling the radicchio:** Wash the radicchio head, wipe it dry and cut it lengthwise into wedges so that the core holds the leaves together. Brush with a little olive oil, then grill in a hot grill pan for 2–3 minutes per side until nicely striped, so the outer leaves caramelise and the inside stays crunchy. Place the grilled radicchio on a board, cut out the thicker core part and chop the leaves into bite-sized pieces.
3. **Making the dressing and assembling:** In a small bowl, whisk the remaining extra virgin olive oil smooth with the balsamic vinegar, honey, crushed garlic, a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper. In a large salad bowl, toss the lukewarm bulgur, fresh arugula and grilled radicchio with the balsamic dressing. Divide the salad among plates, crumble the creamy gorgonzola over it and finally sprinkle with the toasted fragrant walnuts. Serve lukewarm or at room temperature.

## Tips

- The slightly bitter character of radicchio is brought into perfect balance by caramelising it in the grill pan and by the sweetness of the silky honey-balsamic dressing.
- For an even richer dish, you can top the salad with crispy roasted chickpeas.
