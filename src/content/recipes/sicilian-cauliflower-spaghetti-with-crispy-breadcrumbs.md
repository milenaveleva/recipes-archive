## Method

1. **Preparation and toasting the crispy crumbs:** Break the cauliflower into small florets, and finely chop the onion and garlic. Finely chop the fresh parsley.
2. In a frying pan, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil, add the breadcrumbs and pine nuts and toast over medium heat until golden brown and crisp, 2-3 minutes. Take them out into a small bowl and mix with half of the finely chopped parsley.
3. **Making the pasta and the cauliflower ragù:** Bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot. When it boils, throw in the spaghetti and the cauliflower florets at the same time and cook until al dente, about 8-9 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, in a large frying pan, sweat the finely chopped onion in the remaining olive oil until translucent, 3-4 minutes. Add the chopped garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes and fry for another minute until fragrant.
5. Drain the cooked spaghetti and the soft cauliflower (set aside a mug of pasta cooking water!), then tip them straight into the pan with the onion and garlic base.
6. Pour in 1-2 ladlefuls of pasta cooking water, sprinkle with the grated Parmesan, salt and freshly ground black pepper, then shake and toss over high heat for 1-2 minutes until a silky, creamy emulsion coats the pasta and cauliflower.
7. **Serving:** Divide the pasta between deep plates, sprinkle generously with the crispy pine nut and parsley breadcrumbs and a little more freshly grated Parmesan. Serve immediately, hot!

## Tips

- In Sicilian cuisine, toasted breadcrumbs (pangrattato) are traditionally known as the poor man's cheese, giving pasta an irresistible crunchy texture and nutty aroma.
- If you like richer Mediterranean flavours, you can fry 2-3 chopped anchovy fillets or 1 tablespoon of soaked golden raisins into the ragù together with the onion.
