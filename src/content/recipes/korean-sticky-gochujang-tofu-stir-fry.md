## Method

1. **Preparation:** Drain the tofu, press the moisture out thoroughly with paper towels, then cut it into even 2 cm cubes.
2. Toss the tofu cubes in 15 g of cornstarch so that every side is covered in a fine white coating; this ensures a perfectly crispy texture when frying.
3. Make the spicy gochujang glaze: in a small bowl, mix the gochujang paste, soy sauce, honey, finely grated garlic and ginger, the remaining 5 g of cornstarch and 60 ml of water until you get a homogeneous, smooth sauce.
4. Slice the spring onions diagonally, keeping the lower white stalks and the upper green leaves separate. Meanwhile, wash the rice and cook it in about 2-2.25 times its volume of lightly salted water for 35-45 minutes, then let it rest covered for 10 minutes.
5. **Stir-frying in the wok:** Heat a wok or deep pan over high heat until smoking, then pour in 20 ml of sunflower oil.
6. Add the cornstarch-coated tofu cubes and fry over high heat for 6-7 minutes, turning occasionally, until all sides are golden brown and super crispy. Remove the tofu to a plate.
7. Pour the remaining 10 ml of oil into the wok, add the white parts of the spring onions, and stir-fry vigorously for 1 minute until the sweet, oniony aromas are released.
8. **Glazing and serving:** Return the crispy fried tofu to the wok with the spring onions. Stir the gochujang glaze once more, then pour it directly into the hot pan.
9. Toss quickly for 1 minute: the glaze thickens immediately from the heat and forms a beautifully glossy, sticky coating on the crispy tofu cubes.
10. Turn off the heat under the wok and drizzle with the fragrant toasted sesame oil. Sprinkle with the fresh green spring onion rings and toasted sesame seeds. Serve immediately, hot, accompanied by steamed brown jasmine rice.

## Tips

- The secret to truly crispy tofu is the starch: the cornstarch coating sets immediately in the hot oil and creates a crunchy protective layer that keeps its texture even after the sticky glaze is poured over.
- In wok dishes, preparation (mise en place) is key: measure out all the ingredients and mix the glaze before turning on the wok, because the frying and thickening happens in just 7-8 minutes!
