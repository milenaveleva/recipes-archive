## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a pot and sauté the onion with a pinch of salt for 2-3 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, sauté briefly, then stir in the tomato paste and let it cook.
3. Add the rinsed red lentils and the diced potatoes and stir to coat.
4. Pour in the water or broth, add salt and cook covered over medium-low heat for about 20 minutes, until the lentils and potatoes are completely soft.
5. After cooking, add the parsley, then blend until creamy (adjust the consistency by adding water or broth), and finally correct the seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Serve with a few drops of lemon juice, a spoonful of yoghurt (optional/plant-based), fresh parsley and a few chilli flakes on top.
7. Tip: in my video on the ZsomBisztró YouTube channel I show the recipe in more detail.
