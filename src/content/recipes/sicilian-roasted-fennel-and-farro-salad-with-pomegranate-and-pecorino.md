## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Rinse the farro, then cook it in lightly salted boiling water until tender but still pleasantly firm (al dente, about 20-25 minutes). Drain and let it cool to room temperature.
2. Wash the fennel and set the green fronds aside for serving. Cut the bulbs in half, then into wedges lengthways. Toss with 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil, a pinch of salt and freshly ground black pepper, and spread on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Roast in the oven for about 25-30 minutes until golden and caramelised.
3. Toast the shelled pistachios in a dry frying pan over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until fragrant, then let cool and chop roughly. Cut the pomegranate in half and remove the crunchy seeds.
4. **Assembly and serving:** Make the dressing: in a small bowl mix the remaining extra virgin olive oil, the freshly squeezed juice and finely grated zest of the organic lemon, and a pinch of salt and ground black pepper.
5. In a large mixing bowl toss the cooled farro with the washed, drained fresh baby spinach and half of the dressing. Pile the salad base onto a serving platter.
6. Place the still-warm roasted fennel wedges on top, sprinkle with the fresh pomegranate seeds and toasted pistachios. Drizzle with the remaining lemon dressing, use a vegetable peeler to shave thin pecorino flakes over the top, and finally garnish with the delicate fennel fronds. Serve immediately, warm or at room temperature.

## Tips

- To make it more substantial, you can add some roasted chickpeas.
- The easiest way to remove pomegranate seeds without splattering the kitchen with red juice is under water in a bowl.
