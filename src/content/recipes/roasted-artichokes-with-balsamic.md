## Method

1. First clean the artichokes. Cut off the woody stem, then the top third of the artichoke. Remove the tough outer leaves until you reach the yellowish, softer inner leaves. Cut the artichokes in half lengthways and scrape out the hairy choke in the centre with a spoon.
2. Put the artichokes into lightly salted boiling water and cook for 20 minutes.
3. Meanwhile make the marinade. Mix the olive oil, balsamic glaze, crushed garlic, finely chopped herbs, salt and pepper.
4. Drain the artichokes and place them in a baking tray lined with baking paper. Rub them well with the herb mixture. Bake in an oven preheated to 170 °C for about 30 minutes.
5. Serve warm as a starter, or as an excellent side dish to roasts.

## Tips

- When cleaning artichokes, use lemon water to prevent the leaves from browning.
