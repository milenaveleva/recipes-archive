## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Cut off the green stalks of the fennel (set aside the delicate green dill-like fronds for garnish), then cut the bulbs into thin wedges about half a centimetre thick. Place them on a baking tray lined with baking paper, toss with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the crushed garlic, and salt and freshly ground black pepper.
2. Roast the fennel in the oven for 20-25 minutes until the edges caramelise to golden brown and the vegetable is pleasantly tender.
3. **Cooking the orzo and toasting:** Meanwhile, bring plenty of lightly salted water to the boil in a saucepan and cook the orzo al dente according to the package instructions (whole-wheat orzo usually takes 10-11 minutes). Drain, rinse with cold water and drain thoroughly.
4. In a dry frying pan over low heat, toast the pine nuts until golden for 2-3 minutes, shaking constantly, then immediately tip them onto a plate to cool so they do not burn.
5. **Assembly and serving:** Peel the orange with a sharp knife, removing the white pith as well, then cut it into nice fillets (segments). Squeeze the juice from what remains of the orange into a small bowl and mix with the remaining 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. In a large salad bowl, toss the cooled orzo with the roasted caramelised fennel, the fresh rocket, the orange segments and the orange dressing. Divide between two deep plates.
7. Place a whole burrata on top of each portion. Gently cut a cross in the cheeses so the creamy centre slowly flows out. Sprinkle with the golden pine nuts and the reserved fennel fronds and serve immediately.

## Tips

- For an even more intense citrus flavour, grate a little organic orange zest over the pasta when mixing the dressing.
- The salad is fantastic both warm and cold, so it can be made the evening before for lunch at work the next day, but always put the burrata on top just before eating!
