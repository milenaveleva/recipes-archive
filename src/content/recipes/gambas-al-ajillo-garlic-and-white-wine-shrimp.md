## Method

1. Pat the shrimp dry with a paper towel, then season with sea salt. Let stand for at least 5–10 minutes.
2. Scatter the chopped garlic, chilli flakes and the parsley stalks, cut into small crunchy rings, into the cold oil. Turn the heat to medium and warm until the garlic starts to "dance". It must not brown, as it would turn bitter.
3. Add the shrimp to the spiced oil. Cook over medium heat, stirring or shaking frequently, until they turn a bright reddish-pink; this takes about 2–3 minutes.
4. Take the oil off the heat, squeeze in the lemon juice, add the wine and stir well.
5. Serve the shrimp in a deeper plate and sprinkle with herbs to taste. Parsley is the authentic choice, but fresh chilli, coriander and chives go wonderfully too. Some people also put a bay leaf in the oil. It is best with fresh baguette and a glass of white wine.
6. If you buy shrimp in their shells, toast the shells in the oil for a few minutes to extract extra flavour. When the shells have browned, simply strain the oil and start the recipe from the first step.
