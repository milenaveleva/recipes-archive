## Method

1. **Preparation and roasting:** Preheat the oven to 190 °C and prepare a baking tray.
2. Cut the top off the garlic head so the cloves are exposed. Peel the squash, remove the seeds and cut it into small cubes. Spread the squash on the tray, place the garlic alongside, season with salt and pepper and drizzle with a little olive oil. Roast in the preheated oven for about 25 minutes until the squash and garlic are soft.
3. Squeeze the creamy cloves out of the roasted garlic and put them in a blender together with the roasted squash. Add the thyme leaves, cream and broth. Blend into a smooth, thick sauce. If it seems too thick, add a little more broth.
4. Put the raw gnocchi in a baking dish, pour over the squash sauce, mix well and level it out. Sprinkle the top with grated mozzarella and finely grated Parmesan. Bake in the hot oven for another 25 minutes until the gnocchi is completely tender and the cheese is golden brown on top.

## Tips

- For an even creamier result, use 33% whipping cream.
- The leftover roasted squash makes an excellent cream soup for the next day.
