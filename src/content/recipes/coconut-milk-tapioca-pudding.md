## Method

1. Add a can of coconut milk to the tapioca pearls, plus enough water to cover them.
2. Cook over low heat, stirring often and adding water as needed, until the pearls turn from white to translucent.
3. When cooked, stir in the vanilla extract and the honey.
4. Spoon into small bowls and chill in the fridge. Serve decorated with fruit and, if you like, nuts or seeds.
