## Method

1. Put salted water on to boil in a large pot for the pasta. When the water is at a rolling boil, add the whole-wheat penne and cook according to the packet instructions until al dente.
2. While the pasta cooks, make the sauce. Cut the avocado in half, remove the stone and spoon the flesh into a food processor. Add the peas, baby spinach, parsley, lemon juice, finely grated Parmesan, chilli flakes and oat milk.
3. Blend everything until completely smooth and creamy. Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste. Important: do not heat the sauce, as the avocado and cheese can change its colour and texture.
4. Drain the cooked pasta and let it cool for about 10 minutes. Then toss it with the avocado sauce in a large bowl. Divide among three plates and serve immediately. Stored in an airtight container in the fridge, the sauce keeps for up to 2 days.

## Tips

- Do not cook or heat the sauce, as the avocado can turn bitter and the Parmesan can clump. The warm pasta will warm the sauce enough for serving.
