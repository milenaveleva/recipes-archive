## Method

1. **Preparation:** Wash the tomato and zucchini, then cut them into small cubes of about 0.5 cm. Peel the onion and the red onion and slice them thinly. Peel and crush the garlic.
2. **Cooking:** Heat the oil in a larger pot over medium heat. Add the onion and sweat for 2–3 minutes. Then add the crushed garlic and the diced zucchini and fry for another 1–2 minutes.
3. Pour in the vegetable broth, turn up the heat and bring to the boil. As soon as it boils, add the pasta, the red onion and the tomato. Reduce the heat to medium and cook until the pasta is tender (about 8–12 minutes).
4. When the pasta is cooked, stir in the baby spinach and heat for another 1–2 minutes, stirring continuously. Take off the heat and season with freshly squeezed lemon juice, salt and pepper.
5. **Serving:** Divide the finished soup among 4 bowls and sprinkle each portion generously with grated Parmesan before serving.
