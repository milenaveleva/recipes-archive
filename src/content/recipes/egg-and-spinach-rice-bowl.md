## Method

1. Prepare the ingredients: cut the broccoli into small florets, peel the onion and slice it thinly together with the mushrooms.
2. Heat the rapeseed oil in a medium frying pan over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and onion, salt them and cook for 3-4 minutes.
3. Add the broccoli and baby spinach to the pan and fry for another 1-2 minutes. Push the vegetables to the edges of the pan to form a ring in the middle. Crack the egg into the centre, season with salt and pepper, then cook covered for 3-4 minutes until the white is set.
4. Meanwhile, put the cooked rice into a bowl. As soon as the egg is done, carefully transfer the contents of the pan on top of the rice. Drizzle with soy sauce and serve immediately.

## Tips

- It is worth cooking the rice in advance, or you can use leftover rice and reheat it before serving.
