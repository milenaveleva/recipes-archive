## Method

1. **Prepare and fry the zucchini:** Wash the zucchini thoroughly, dry them and cut into thin rounds about 2–3 mm thick. Heat the extra virgin olive oil in a large frying pan, add the slightly flattened garlic cloves, then fry the zucchini rounds in batches until golden brown (in about 6–8 minutes). Transfer the fried zucchini to paper towels and remove the garlic.
2. Bring plenty of water to the boil in a large pot, salt it, then cook the spaghetti al dente (2 minutes less than the packet time). Measure out and set aside about 150–200 ml of the starchy pasta water.
3. **Assemble and serve:** Return three quarters of the fried zucchini to the pan, add the extra olive oil and about 100 ml of the hot pasta cooking water. Mash some of the zucchini slightly with a fork or wooden spoon so that a creamy emulsion forms.
4. Tip the drained spaghetti into the pan with the zucchini. Take it off the heat, add the finely grated Parmesan, the freshly ground black pepper and the basil torn roughly by hand. Work it into a creamy sauce with constant shaking and stirring, adding a little more cooking water if needed.
5. Pile onto plates, top with the reserved crispy zucchini rounds, garnish with extra grated cheese, fresh basil leaves and a few drops of extra virgin olive oil, and serve immediately, piping hot.

## Tips

- The secret of a perfect Spaghetti alla Nerano is the emulsion of starchy pasta water and cheese: always stir the cheese in off the heat, so you get a silky sauce without the cheese clumping.
- For a more authentic flavour you can use half grated Provolone or Pecorino and half Parmigiano Reggiano.
