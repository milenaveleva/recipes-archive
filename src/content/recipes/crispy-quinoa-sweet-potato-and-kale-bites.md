## Method

1. To make oat flour, put oats in a high-speed blender and blend on the highest setting until you get a fine, flour-like texture. If you find it easier, you can use store-bought oat flour.
2. Rinse 1 cup of raw quinoa in a fine sieve, then put it in a medium saucepan and pour over 1½ cups of water. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low and cover the pan. Simmer the quinoa for 14–17 minutes, until most of the water has been absorbed and the quinoa is light, soft and fluffy. Take it off the heat, fluff with a fork and put the lid back on to let it steam for another 4–5 minutes. The cooked quinoa will make nearly 3 cups, but you only need 1½ cups for this recipe. You can use the rest in other dishes.
3. Preheat the oven to 200 °C and line a large baking tray with baking paper.
4. In a small bowl, mix the ground flaxseed and the water, then let stand for about 5 minutes to thicken.
5. In a large bowl, combine all the ingredients, including the flaxseed mixture and 1½ cups of cooked quinoa. Mix well until the mixture holds together. Do not leave out the flour, because it helps the patties keep their shape.
6. With wet hands, shape the mixture into patties of about ¼ cup each. Press them together firmly so they are stable, then place them on the lined baking tray.
7. Bake the patties for 15 minutes, then carefully turn them over and bake for another 8–10 minutes until golden brown and firm. Let them cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then they are ready to eat.
8. Store leftover patties in an airtight container in the fridge, where they keep for 5–6 days. To reheat, warm a pan over medium heat, add a little oil and fry the patties for about 3 minutes on each side until heated through.
