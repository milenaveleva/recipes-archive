## Method

1. Wash and clean the vegetables. Cut the zucchini and carrots in half, then into quarters, halve the peppers and cut the onion into quarters.
2. Line a baking tray with baking paper, place the vegetables on it, sprinkle with dried thyme and salt, and drizzle with olive oil. Roast at 200 °C for about 25 minutes, or until the vegetables are soft.
3. Chop the almonds and hazelnuts into smaller pieces, then mix with the poppy seeds. Heat a pan and toast the mixture over medium heat, stirring constantly.
4. Put the roasted vegetables in a blender, add the vegetable broth and cottage cheese, and blend to a silky cream. Season with salt and pepper. Serve sprinkled with the toasted seeds and garnished with fresh basil.
