## Method

1. Bring salted water to the boil in a large pot, add the pasta and cook for 15–16 minutes over high heat until it reaches the desired texture. Drain when done.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion, the finely chopped garlic and the fresh tomato and fry for 2–3 minutes. Then lower the heat, add the drained tuna, the canned chopped tomatoes, the lemon juice, salt, pepper, oregano and the grated Parmesan. Cook for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
3. Take the pan off the heat, add the cooked pasta and toss thoroughly. Divide the pasta among plates, sprinkle with fresh chives and torn mozzarella, and serve.
