## Method

1. **Roasting:** Wash the peppers and place them on a baking tray. Add the head of garlic with one side cut off, and drizzle with a little olive oil.
2. Bake in an oven preheated to 220 °C for about 30 minutes. Turn the peppers during baking until they are soft and their skins are browned or blackened in places.
3. Immediately put the roasted peppers in a bowl and cover tightly with cling film so the steam cannot escape; this makes the skins easier to remove.
4. After 5-10 minutes, take the peppers out and remove the seeds and skins.
5. Squeeze the cloves out of the roasted garlic and blend them smooth together with the peeled peppers.
6. **Sauce and pasta:** Cook the spaghetti in plenty of salted water according to the packet instructions. Make sure it stays "al dente" (firm to the bite), because it will cook further in the sauce.
7. Pour the blended pepper sauce into a wider pot or frying pan and start heating it.
8. Add the salt and the leaves of the fresh thyme and oregano to taste.
9. Stir in the mascarpone until it has melted completely into the sauce. Finally, season with freshly ground black pepper.
10. Drain the cooked pasta, add it to the sauce and heat them together for half a minute.
11. **Crunchy topping and serving:** In a food processor, grind the Parmesan with the breadcrumbs and a little thyme. If the crumbs are not crunchy enough, toast them briefly in a dry frying pan, then let them cool.
12. Serve the finished pasta sprinkled with the cheesy crumbs, fresh herbs and extra black pepper.
