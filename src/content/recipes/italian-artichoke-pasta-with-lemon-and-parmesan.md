## Method

1. **Preparation and frying:** Drain the artichokes and cut them into quarters. Finely grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze out its juice. Finely chop the parsley and the fresh garlic.
2. In a large frying pan, heat 3 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and the pinch of chilli flakes, then fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the artichokes to the pan, season with salt and black pepper, then fry for 5-6 minutes, until a nice golden-brown crust forms on them.
4. **Cooking the pasta and assembly:** Meanwhile, bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot and cook the penne until al dente. Before draining, set aside 1 ladleful of the cooking water.
5. Pour the drained penne into the pan with the browned artichokes. Add the lemon juice, the grated lemon zest, the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and half of the reserved cooking water.
6. Over high heat, shake and toss for 1-2 minutes so that a silky emulsion coats the penne. Turn off the heat and fold in the fresh parsley and half of the Parmesan.
7. Serve immediately in deep plates, sprinkling the remaining grated Parmesan on top.

## Tips

- The starchy cooking water of the pasta and the olive oil, mixed with the cheese, make the perfect silky sauce.
- The zest and fresh juice of an unwaxed organic lemon complement the full, nutty aroma of the artichoke fantastically.
