## Method

1. Wash the kale and spinach thoroughly. Cut out the tougher ribs of the kale, then cut both greens into smaller pieces.
2. Peel the mango, kiwi and ginger. Cut them up depending on the size of your blender: for larger machines it is enough to halve the fruit, and cut the ginger into a few smaller slices.
3. Put the leaves, fruit and ginger into the blender and pour in 250 ml of water. Blend until the smoothie is completely creamy and lump-free. If you don't have a countertop blender, you can use an immersion blender, although it may take a little longer.

## Tips

- If you don't like kale, you can replace it with the same amount of spinach.
