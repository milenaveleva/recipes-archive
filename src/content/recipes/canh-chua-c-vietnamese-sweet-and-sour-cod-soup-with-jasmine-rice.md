## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the rice:** Rinse the jasmine rice thoroughly in cold water until the draining water runs clear, then cook it in twice its volume of lightly salted water, covered, over low heat for 12-14 minutes until tender, and leave to rest covered.
2. Cut the cod fillet into larger bite-sized pieces (about 4-5 cm). Peel the pineapple and cut it into smaller wedges, cut the tomatoes into wedges, and rinse and drain the bean sprouts.
3. Finely chop the garlic, slice the spring onions and the red chilli into rounds, and roughly chop the coriander.
4. **Making the aromatic broth and vegetables:** In a medium pot, heat 15 ml of sunflower oil, add the finely chopped garlic and fry over medium heat until golden brown and fragrant (about 1 minute). Remove half and set aside for serving.
5. Add the pineapple pieces and half of the tomatoes to the garlic remaining in the pot and fry for 2 minutes so they release their aroma.
6. Pour in 800 ml of water (or light broth), bring to the boil, then stir in the 2 tablespoons of tamarind paste, 45 ml of fish sauce and 25 g of cane sugar. Taste: the flavour should be refreshingly sour, mildly sweet and pleasantly salty all at once.
7. **Gently cooking the fish and serving:** Reduce the heat to a gentle simmer, carefully lower the pieces of cod and the remaining tomato wedges into the hot broth. Cook gently, without stirring, for 4-5 minutes until the cod is flaky and snow white.
8. In the last minute, stir in the crunchy bean sprouts and turn off the heat so the sprouts keep their fresh texture.
9. Ladle the hot, steaming soup into deep bowls with the tender pieces of fish and vegetables. Sprinkle with sliced spring onions, fresh coriander, chilli and the reserved crispy fried garlic. Serve with warm jasmine rice.

## Tips

- Don't overcook the cod: as soon as the flesh turns opaque and flaky, take it off the heat immediately so it stays juicy and buttery soft.
- If the tamarind paste is very thick, dissolve it in a little warm water before adding it to the soup.
