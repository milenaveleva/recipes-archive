## Method

1. In a bowl, mix the plain cream cheese thoroughly with the finely grated Gouda. Season with a little ground black pepper.
2. Shape the mixture into small, bite-sized balls. If the mixture is too soft, chill it in the fridge for 15-20 minutes so it is easier to shape.
3. Prepare the coatings: in separate bowls, coarsely chop the pistachios, walnuts and frozen cranberries (thawed and patted dry).
4. Roll the cheese balls in the different coatings: some in the pistachios, some in the walnuts and the rest in the cranberries.
5. Keep in the fridge until serving. Serve with salty crackers or slices of fresh whole-wheat baguette.

## Tips

- For an even more intense flavour, lightly toast the walnuts and pistachios in a dry pan before chopping.
