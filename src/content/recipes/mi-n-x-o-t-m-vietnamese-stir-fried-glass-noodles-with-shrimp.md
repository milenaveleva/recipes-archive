## Method

1. **Preparation:** Soak the glass noodles in warm water for about 8-10 minutes until soft and springy. Drain thoroughly and cut with kitchen scissors in 2-3 places so they are easier to toss together later.
2. In a small bowl, mix the sauce: the soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, cane sugar, half of the black pepper and the sesame oil, until the sugar dissolves completely.
3. Cut the carrot into thin matchsticks (julienne) and slice the white button mushrooms. Chop the garlic finely and cut the spring onions into 3 cm pieces, separating the white and green parts. Wash the shrimp, drain them and toss with the remaining ground black pepper.
4. **Stir-frying in the wok:** Heat a wok or deep pan over high heat with 1 tablespoon of sunflower oil. Add the shrimp and half of the finely chopped garlic. Fry quickly for 1-2 minutes until the shrimp turn nicely pink and are cooked through, then transfer them to a plate.
5. Pour another tablespoon of sunflower oil into the hot pan. Add the remaining garlic and the white parts of the spring onions and fry for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the sliced mushrooms and julienned carrot and stir-fry, constantly shaking and stirring, for 2-3 minutes so the vegetables stay crisp.
6. Add the drained glass noodles and the pre-fried shrimp to the pan. Pour over the prepared sauce and add the green parts of the spring onions. Toss over high heat for 1-2 minutes until the glass noodles absorb the tasty, spicy sauce and turn nicely glossy.
7. **Serving:** Take the wok off the heat, sprinkle with fresh coriander and toss once more. Serve immediately, hot, in deep plates or bowls.

## Tips

- Do not soak the glass noodles in warm water for too long: they should just soften, because in the hot wok they reach their perfect, silky, springy texture as they absorb the sauce.
- Speed is key when stir-frying in a wok: prepare all the ingredients in advance (mise en place) so they are at hand during the high-heat frying!
