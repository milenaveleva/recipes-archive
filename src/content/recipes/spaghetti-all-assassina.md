## Method

1. **Making the tomato broth:** In a smaller pot, bring 800 ml of water to the boil with the tomato paste and the fine salt. Keep this tomato broth gently simmering while you make the pasta.
2. **Frying and caramelising:** In a large, heavy-bottomed (preferably cast-iron) frying pan, heat the extra virgin olive oil. Add the finely chopped fresh garlic and the sliced red chilli peppers and fry over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Pour the passata into the pan, mix it with the garlic oil and simmer for 2 minutes. Lay the dry spaghetti lengthways directly on the sauce in the pan in a single layer.
4. Leave the spaghetti to fry over high heat for 3-4 minutes without moving it, until the bottom caramelises and scorches. Carefully turn it over with tongs and fry the other side too, until a crisp surface forms on it.
5. **Risottata technique and serving:** Start adding the hot tomato broth ladle by ladle, just as when making risotto. Wait until the spaghetti has completely absorbed the liquid and the bottom of the pan starts to sizzle again before adding the next portion.
6. Repeat the process for 10-12 minutes, until the spaghetti is spicily caramelised and crisp on the outside and perfectly al dente inside. Serve immediately, piping hot!

## Tips

- The key to the authentic experience is to let the pasta scorch slightly on the bottom of the pan: the smoky, caramelised layer gives the real assassin character!
- If you like it even hotter, leave the seeds of the chilli peppers in when frying them in the oil.
