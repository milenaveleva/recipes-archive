## Method

1. **Prep:** Put a large pot of generously salted water on to boil for the pappardelle. Clean the cremini mushrooms and slice them into thick wedges. Finely chop the shallots and garlic, grate the zest of the organic lemon and squeeze out its juice.
2. **Sauté and make the ragù:** Heat the olive oil and the extra virgin olive oil in a large frying pan. Add the sliced mushrooms and fry over high heat for 4–5 minutes, until nicely golden brown. Add the finely chopped shallots, garlic and fresh thyme leaves, and fry for another 1–2 minutes, until the aromas develop.
3. Pour the plant-based cooking cream and half of the grated lemon zest into the pan. Season with salt, freshly ground black pepper and a little lemon juice. Simmer over low heat for 2–3 minutes, until the silky sauce thickens slightly.
4. **Toss and serve:** Cook the pappardelle in the rolling boil until al dente according to the packet instructions (about 7–8 minutes). Before draining, set aside half a ladleful of pasta water.
5. Toss the drained warm pasta directly into the creamy mushroom sauce. Add half of the grated Parmesan and the reserved pasta water, so that the sauce coats the pasta perfectly.
6. Serve immediately in deep bowls, sprinkled with the remaining grated Parmesan and lemon zest, and garnished with fresh sprigs of thyme.

## Tips

- If you want an even richer flavour, you can stir a spoonful of mascarpone or a few drops of truffle oil into the sauce at the end of the sautéing!
