## Method

1. Remove the pin bones from the salmon fillet with tweezers. In a bowl, mix the honey with the coarse sea salt, the coarsely crushed pepper and the finely grated lemon zest.
2. Spread a third of the honey-salt paste over the bottom of a dish just large enough for the fish. Place the salmon fillet on it skin side down, cover with the fresh dill, then spread the remaining paste over the flesh.
3. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate to cure for 12-16 hours.
4. After curing, rinse the salmon under cold running water and pat thoroughly dry. With a long, sharp knife, cut thin 2-3 mm slices, holding the knife at an angle. Serve with blini or toast.

## Tips

- When grating the lemon zest, make sure only the yellow part goes into the dish, because the white pith is bitter.
- For a more authentic experience, serve the finished gravlax with a dill-mustard sauce.
