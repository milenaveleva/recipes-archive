## Method

1. **Preparing the tofu balls:** Drain the tofu thoroughly, then gently press out the excess moisture with paper towels. Crumble it into a bowl or mash it finely with a fork. Grate the ginger and half of the garlic, then add to the crumbled tofu together with the cornstarch, 1 tablespoon of soy sauce and a few drops of sesame oil. Knead thoroughly into a shapeable mixture, then with slightly wet hands form walnut-sized balls.
2. **Making and cooking the soup:** In a larger saucepan, heat one teaspoon of sesame oil, then fry the sliced shiitake mushrooms and the carrot cut into thin strips for 2–3 minutes until they become fragrant. Add the remaining grated ginger and garlic, pour in the vegetable broth and the remaining soy sauce, and bring the soup to the boil. Carefully lower the tofu balls into the boiling broth, then reduce the heat to medium and simmer gently for 5–6 minutes, until the balls float up to the surface. In the last 2 minutes add the washed pak choi, halved lengthwise, so it stays crisp and fresh green.
3. **Serving:** Ladle the hot soup into deep bowls so that each gets some tofu balls, vegetables and mushrooms. Sprinkle with thinly sliced spring onion and toasted sesame seeds.

## Tips

- If you like spicy flavours, drizzle a little chilli sesame oil over the finished soup or sprinkle with fresh chilli flakes when serving!
- You can also mix a little finely chopped spring onion or fresh cilantro into the tofu ball mixture for an even more aromatic result.
