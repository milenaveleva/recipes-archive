## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C.
2. Wash the wild garlic and chop it finely.
3. In a bowl, mix the skyr, cottage cheese, one egg and the chopped wild garlic.
4. Add the flour, baking powder and salt, then knead into a dough.
5. Cut out small scones from the dough and place them on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
6. Brush the tops with the other beaten egg and sprinkle with the grated cheese.
7. Bake until golden brown, about 20-25 minutes.
