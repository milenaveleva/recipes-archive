## Method

1. **Cook the pasta:** Bring plenty of salted water to the boil in a large pot. Add the whole-wheat pasta and cook it according to the packet instructions (about 8-10 minutes) until al dente.
2. Preheat the oven to 180 °C. Prepare a tray of about 40 cm × 40 cm and line it with baking parchment.
3. Meanwhile, prepare the vegetables: grate the courgette on the large holes of a grater, finely chop the diced onion and finely chop the parsley.
4. **Assembly and baking:** In a large bowl, beat the eggs, then stir in the grated courgette, the onion and the parsley. Season the mixture with chilli flakes, sweet paprika, salt and pepper.
5. Drain the cooked pasta, rinse it with cold water, drain it thoroughly, then add it to the egg and vegetable mixture. Toss thoroughly and pour into the prepared tray.
6. Cut the tomatoes into thicker slices and arrange them on top of the frittata. Bake at 170-180 °C for about 25-30 minutes, until the egg has set and the top is golden brown. Serve with basil pesto if you like.

## Tips

- The frittata is excellent cold too, so it is perfect as lunch the next day.
