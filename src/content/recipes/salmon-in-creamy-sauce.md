## Method

1. Heat a frying pan and, once hot, add the canola oil and let it warm for a few seconds. When it is hot, add the salmon fillets and sear them until golden brown on both sides. Take the salmon out of the pan (it may not be cooked through yet) and add the olive oil. Once it is warm, add the finely chopped onion and let it brown, then add the sliced garlic and keep cooking gently.
2. Add the halved cherry tomatoes to the pan as well and heat for another two minutes until the tomatoes soften, then stir in a tablespoon of tomato paste and cook for about a minute, stirring constantly. Pour in the vegetable broth and the plant-based cooking cream, season with salt and pepper, and stir until smooth.
3. Return the salmon fillets to the sauce and let it bubble gently until the sauce is thick and velvety and the salmon is cooked through, which takes a few minutes. Finally, stir in the baby spinach, and you can add some finely chopped basil as well. Serve with pasta or roast potatoes.

## Tips

- The source suggests serving with roast potatoes or pasta; they are not included in the ingredient list.
