## Method

1. Add the pasta to a pot of boiling salted water and cook according to the packet instructions (usually about 10 minutes, counting 100 g of pasta per 1 litre of water).
2. While the pasta cooks, make the sauce: in a large, deep frying pan warm the ricotta, then add a ladleful of the pasta cooking water, the lemon zest, the rosemary, a pinch of salt and a pinch of pepper. Stir together and cook over low heat for 6–8 minutes.
3. Drain the cooked pasta, add it to the sauce in the pan and mix thoroughly. Divide the finished dish into 3 portions, sprinkle with grated Parmesan and serve.
