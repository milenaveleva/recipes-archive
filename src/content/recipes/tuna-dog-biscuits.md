## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. Drain the tuna, put it in a bowl and mash it with a fork. Add the egg and 3 tablespoons of water and mix. Sprinkle in the oats and flour, then knead into a smooth dough.
3. Form the mixture into about 40 small balls and place them on the tray. Bake for 25-30 minutes until golden brown.
4. Once cooled, store in an airtight container in the fridge (keeps for 7-10 days).

## Tips

- If the dough is too sticky, add a little more flour; if too dry, a little water.
