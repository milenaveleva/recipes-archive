## Method

1. First bring salted water to the boil in a large pot over high heat. While it heats, rinse and pat the salmon dry, remove the skin and cut the fish into 1 × 1 cm cubes. Peel and chop the onion.
2. As soon as the water boils, add the pasta and cook for 14–15 minutes over high heat. Drain after cooking.
3. Heat a frying pan with the olive oil over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onion and sweat for 4–5 minutes. Then add the salmon, season with salt and pepper, and fry together for another 2–3 minutes (still over medium heat).
4. Pour in the plant-based cooking cream, add the cream cheese and cook until the sauce thickens (about 4–5 minutes). Finally stir in the peas, parsley and pasta, turn off the heat and serve.
