## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Wash the butternut squash, cut it in half and remove the seeds with a spoon. Cut it into slices about 2 cm thick and place them on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Drizzle with a little olive oil and bake for 15–20 minutes, until soft. Leave to cool.
2. Weigh out 350 g of the roasted squash (set the rest aside for other dishes; it keeps in the fridge for days). Purée the squash with a stick blender.
3. Add the egg, flour, grated Parmesan and salt to the squash purée. Knead into a uniform dough. On a floured surface divide the dough into several parts, shape them into rolls, then cut into small pieces. Use extra flour for dusting if needed. Shape the typical ridged gnocchi with the back of a fork.
4. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and briefly fry the sage leaves in it until fragrant and crisp.
5. Bring 2.5 litres of water to the boil in a large pot and salt it. When the water is boiling, reduce the heat, add half of the gnocchi and cook for about 3 minutes (until they float to the surface). Lift them out with a slotted spoon, then cook the second batch.
6. Toss the drained gnocchi with the sage oil. When serving, you can sprinkle with extra Parmesan. Also an excellent side dish for baked salmon.

## Tips

- The gnocchi dough is best when it is not kneaded for too long, so that it stays light and soft.
