## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Soak the dried white beans in plenty of cold water overnight (8–12 hours). Drain, cover with fresh water, bring to the boil and simmer for 60–90 minutes until tender, then drain.
2. Bring water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta and salt it generously. Meanwhile, peel and finely chop the onion. Break the broccoli into florets; peel the stem and cut it into small cubes.
3. When the water boils, add the pasta and cook for the time given on the packet (about 10-12 minutes). Add the broccoli florets to the pasta 5 minutes before the end of cooking. When done, drain and rinse with cold water so it keeps its colour and stops cooking.
4. **Making the sauce:** Make the creamy sauce: in a blender or tall container, combine the cooked white beans, lemon juice, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, nutritional yeast, onion granules, vegetable broth, salt and pepper. Add the peeled garlic too, then blend with a stick blender until completely smooth.
5. **Assembly and serving:** In a large frying pan, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and the broccoli stem and fry for 2-3 minutes. Add the cooked pasta and the broccoli florets and fry for another 3-4 minutes. Finally pour in the prepared sauce, lower the heat and, tossing thoroughly, cook for 4-5 more minutes, until the sauce coats the pasta creamily.
6. Divide the finished dish between plates, sprinkle with the pine nuts and serve warm.

## Tips

- When serving, you can drizzle over a little fresh lemon juice and garnish with fresh basil leaves for an even fresher taste.
