## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking:** Bring water to the boil in a large pot for the pasta and salt it generously. Meanwhile, peel and finely chop the onion. Break the broccoli into florets; peel the stem and cut it into small cubes.
2. When the water boils, add the pasta and cook for the time given on the packet (about 10-12 minutes). Add the broccoli florets to the pasta 5 minutes before the end of cooking. When done, drain and rinse with cold water so it keeps its colour and stops cooking.
3. **Making the sauce:** Make the creamy sauce: in a blender or tall container, combine the drained white beans, lemon juice, 2 tablespoons of olive oil, nutritional yeast, onion granules, vegetable broth, salt and pepper. Add the peeled garlic too, then blend with a stick blender until completely smooth.
4. **Assembly and serving:** In a large frying pan, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and the broccoli stem and fry for 2-3 minutes. Add the cooked pasta and the broccoli florets and fry for another 3-4 minutes. Finally pour in the prepared sauce, lower the heat and, tossing thoroughly, cook for 4-5 more minutes, until the sauce coats the pasta creamily.
5. Divide the finished dish between plates, sprinkle with the pine nuts and serve warm.

## Tips

- When serving, you can drizzle over a little fresh lemon juice and garnish with fresh basil leaves for an even fresher taste.
