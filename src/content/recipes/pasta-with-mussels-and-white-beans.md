## Method

1. Soak the beans in water at least half a day before cooking so that they swell. Then cook them in fresh water together with the bay leaf until soft, about 45 minutes. When done, drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a frying pan and sauté the crushed garlic and half of the finely chopped parsley in it. Add the cooked beans and fry briefly together.
3. Cook the pasta in lightly salted water until al dente. It is worth taking it out a little early, because it will finish cooking in the pan with the sauce.
4. Clean the mussels, open them and put the meat together with its juices in a bowl. Pour olive oil into another hot pan, add one crushed garlic clove and the remaining parsley, then pour in the mussel meat with its juices.
5. Fry briefly, then add the quartered cherry tomatoes and the drained beans. Cook everything together for 2–3 minutes. Finally toss in the cooked pasta, season with pepper and drizzle with a little fresh olive oil. Serve in deep plates.

## Tips

- Use a good-quality extra virgin olive oil for serving, to bring out the flavour of the seafood even more.
