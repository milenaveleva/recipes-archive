## Method

1. First wash the broccoli, then separate the florets from the stalk. Chop the florets into small pieces, and peel and dice the stalk into smaller cubes.
2. Bring water to the boil in a large pot, salt it, then add the spaghetti. Cook over medium heat for about 10 minutes (or as long as the packet says) until al dente.
3. While the pasta cooks, make the pesto: put the peeled garlic in a food processor with the grated Parmesan, walnuts, olive oil, baby spinach, basil, lemon juice, the diced broccoli stalk, salt, pepper and half of the broccoli florets. Blend until creamy, then taste and add more salt and pepper if needed.
4. When there are only 1-2 minutes of cooking time left, add the remaining broccoli florets to the water and cook them with the spaghetti for the remaining time.
5. Finally, drain the pasta together with the broccoli, toss with the prepared pesto, divide among 5 plates and serve fresh.

## Tips

- Toast the walnuts in a dry frying pan before serving for an even more intense flavour.
