## Method

1. Cut the cherry tomatoes into sixths and lightly press them with a spoon in a bowl so they release their juices.
2. Add the remaining ingredients: the cooked shrimp, mayonnaise, finely chopped spring onion, garden cress, cognac and sugar. Season with a pinch of salt and freshly squeezed lemon juice.
3. Mix everything thoroughly, then garnish with fresh basil leaves. Chill in the fridge before serving so the flavours meld and the cocktail is pleasantly cold.

## Tips

- Serve the cocktail in elegant glasses, or on iceberg lettuce leaves for an even more striking effect.
