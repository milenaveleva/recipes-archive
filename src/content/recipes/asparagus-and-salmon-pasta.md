## Method

1. Pat the salmon dry with paper towels and salt it. Grill both sides in a grill pan for a few minutes, so that it is cooked through but stays juicy and does not dry out. When done, flake it into bite-sized pieces.
2. Wash the asparagus, cut off the tips and slice the stalks lengthwise into thin ribbons with a vegetable peeler. Chop the remaining pieces smaller.
3. Bring 2 litres of water to the boil in a pot, add salt, then put in the prepared asparagus. Blanch until the asparagus ribbons are pliable, then drain immediately. Stir the plant-based crème fraîche, lemon juice, salt and ground pepper into the asparagus.
4. Cook the pasta al dente according to the packet instructions. Drain, setting aside a little of the cooking water. Add the pasta to the sauce, pour in a few spoonfuls of cooking water and toss together.
5. To serve, sprinkle with grated lemon zest and top with the salmon pieces.

## Tips

- Use an organic lemon, as the zest is used in the recipe.
