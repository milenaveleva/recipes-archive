## Method

1. In a deep frying pan, heat a little olive oil and sweat the finely chopped onion until translucent.
2. Add the finely chopped carrot, zucchini and red pepper; fry briefly until they start to soften. Then add the finely chopped garlic and cook for another minute.
3. Add the chopped fresh tomatoes, the rinsed lentils and the tomato paste. If needed, add a little water too.
4. Cook covered over low heat for about an hour (or until the lentils and vegetables are soft). Stir occasionally and add water if the mixture becomes too thick.
5. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in salted water according to the packet instructions.
6. Mix the cooked pasta with the sauce. (If you are making portions ahead, it is best to combine the pasta and sauce fresh, just before serving.)
7. Season with salt and pepper and garnish with chopped basil. If you like, sprinkle with vegan cheese or nutritional yeast flakes.
