## Method

1. **Preparation and cooking the pasta:** Bring a large pot of plenty of lightly salted water to the boil. Snip off both ends of the green beans and cut them into 3-4 cm pieces. Wash the cherry tomatoes and halve them, and cut the garlic cloves into thin slices.
2. When the water is at a rolling boil, add the penne and the chopped green beans. Cook them together for about 9-10 minutes, until the pasta is pleasantly "al dente" and the beans stay bright green and crisp-tender. Before draining, scoop out 1 mug (about 150 ml) of starchy cooking water.
3. **Making the garlicky vegetable base:** While the pasta cooks, heat the extra virgin olive oil in a wide, deep frying pan over medium heat. Add the thinly sliced garlic and the pinch of chili flakes and fry for 1 minute until you can gently smell it (take care not to brown it too dark).
4. Add the halved cherry tomatoes to the pan, season with salt and pepper, and fry over medium-high heat for 3-4 minutes until the skins start to wrinkle and the tomatoes release a juicy sauce.
5. **Tossing and serving:** Tip the drained hot penne and beans into the pan. Add about half a ladle of the reserved pasta water and half of the grated Parmesan, and toss, shaking the pan, for 1-2 minutes over low heat so that the starchy cooking water, the olive oil and the Parmesan form a silky sauce on the pasta.
6. Take the pan off the heat and fold in the roughly torn fresh basil leaves. Serve immediately in deep plates, topped with the remaining freshly grated Parmesan and a little more fresh basil.

## Tips

- Cooking the pasta and green beans together in one pot not only saves time and washing up, but also makes sure the beans finish at exactly the same time as the penne.
- The reserved starchy cooking water is the secret of Italian pasta: mixed with the olive oil and cheese it forms a natural emulsion that makes the sauce cling perfectly to the penne, even without cream.
